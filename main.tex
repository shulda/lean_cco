\begin{abstract}
We consider transitive homogeneous structures in a finite complete
atomic symmetric binary language whose ages have AP/JEP and semantic
homomorphic cycle completion (CCO).  Our main \emph{working candidate}
strengthens ordinary EPPA: for any palette \(S\) admitting strong
amalgamation on finite \emph{real algebraically closed} spans and
any nonempty finite \(A_0\subseteq M\), a single finite ordinary
EPPA witness \(W\) contains \(\operatorname{acl}_M(A_0)\) and allows
every non-\(S\) clique to be moved by an automorphism into this
closure, with any selected root moved into \(A_0\) itself.
This is rooted automorphism-transport closure-relative
\(S\)-clique faithfulness (rooted ACF); it does not assert
ordinary unclosed SAP or coherent EPPA.

The proof uses one diversity-rank induction with ordered
rooted, ordinary, and finite-master phases; ordinary EPPA
is the full-palette consequence of the strengthened theorem.  The strengthening uses the
A32 canonical algebraic-hull and exact-sort calculus, concentration
of unsafe pairs in correspondence classes, unary-closure MI
quotients and full-support safe palettes, relative rooted
HKN/R07 witnesses, and a Birkhoff fibre induction producing a
simultaneous finite master for all exact owner quotients.
The final rooted gluing handles arbitrary numbers of minimal
infinite directions, unequal owner joins, and finite exact kernels.
All essential in-house proof steps are incorporated below.

Part~I also proves a full identity-aware typed exact calculus and
homogeneity, ordinary AP and homomorphic CCO for simultaneous full
exact quotients of several independently named original sorts,
without requiring SAP. This auxiliary foundational extension does
not assert lifting of quotient automorphisms or broaden the
one-original-sort EPPA/Ramsey headline.

A separate Part~III proves a finite-language precompact Ramsey expansion
by pure-clone SAPification, safe finite-index unary splitting and a
functorial readout from compatible meet-irreducible imaginary coordinate
tuples. The needed unpublished SAP/CCO Ramsey inputs and the pure-clone
lemma are included in the internal technical appendices of this manuscript,
with the published Hubi\v{c}ka--Ne\v{s}et\v{r}il finite-model and
locally finite Ramsey theorems as external inputs. The Ramsey proof does
not invoke the EPPA witness construction.

A separate Part~IV proves, relative to the represented exact/phase/quotient
source calculus, that \emph{every genuine terminal maximal-exact-character
unary tower has an automatically core terminal type table}. More strongly,
any distinct terminal labels are joined by an original bipartite atom,
which cannot occur on a terminal diagonal. Exact trace-product
identities and canonical original gate lifts give quotient commutation;
a diversity-rank induction treats identity and nonidentity gates
separately. The resulting full, \emph{unfiltered} terminal unary
expansion has the expansion property by simultaneous singleton Ramsey
comparison. Transition-synchronised subquotient orders and the full
compatible-MI-tuple readout then yield a finite unary/binary
Ramsey expansion \emph{with} the expansion property.
No minimum-image core retraction is used.

Part~V proves that every transitive homogeneous symmetric
SAP+homomorphic-CCO source with \emph{no finite relative index
between distinct invariant equivalence relations} has a
stationary independence relation on all finite real sets,
including over the empty base. In fact only (NR2) is needed
in each actual named sort. The proof constructs a canonical
strong amalgam from the Part~I joint hereditary selector on
strong exact base-path domains and verifies both directions
of transitivity and stationarity. We separately record the
finite-global-quotient obstruction and a relative index-two
positive control. This SIR conclusion is \emph{not} asserted
for a general AP-only original reduct; nor is SAP inferred
merely from absence of finite relative indices.

These are working human-text proofs, not Lean-certified results. The
main statements concern one original sort and do not assert coherent
EPPA. Their distinct hypotheses and provider dependencies are collected
in the scope section; local theorem interfaces specify the inputs needed
by each construction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting and semantic cycle completion}
\begin{definition}
A \emph{complete atomic symmetric binary language} consists of a finite
set $\mathcal D$ of irreflexive symmetric binary relation symbols, each
with its own name, in addition to logical equality. In any finite
structure under consideration, each pair of distinct vertices has
exactly one colour $a\in\mathcal D$. A \emph{triangle table} specifies
which colour triples occur on three distinct vertices. A \emph{triangle-defined
age} contains exactly the finite complete colourings whose every
three-vertex substructure is allowed by the table.
\end{definition}

A \emph{partial diagram} assigns a colour from $\mathcal D$ to some pairs
of distinct formal vertices, with no relation imposed on the other pairs.
A colour-preserving \emph{homomorphism} into a complete structure $M$ maps
vertices to vertices and preserves every assigned colour. It need not be
injective: vertices not joined by a specified edge may be identified.
Every specified simple cycle is interpreted as the partial diagram
consisting of the edges on that cycle.

\begin{definition}[Semantic homomorphic CCO]
A class $\mathcal K=\Age(M)$ has \emph{semantic homomorphic CCO} if every
finite partial diagram $P$ with the property that each of its specified
simple cycles admits a colour-preserving homomorphism into $M$ itself
admits a colour-preserving homomorphism $P\to M$.
\end{definition}

The \emph{amalgamation property} (AP) requires an amalgam of any two
embeddings over a common finite structure; \emph{strong} amalgamation
(SAP) additionally requires that the two images meet exactly in the image
of their common domain. We never assume SAP of the original age.

\begin{definition}[Ordinary EPPA]
A class of finite structures has the \emph{extension property for partial
automorphisms} if for every $A$ in the class there is a finite $B$ in
the class containing $A$ such that \emph{every} isomorphism between
induced substructures of $A$ extends to an automorphism of $B$.
One common $B$ must work for all such maps. No coherent choice of these
extensions is required.
\end{definition}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main-rooted-acf} will be proved by a common,
phased induction.  At each diversity rank the rooted statement
precedes its ordinary EPPA consequence, which in turn supplies the
ordinary local providers needed for the finite owner master.
The two roles of protected rooted witnesses and unprotected
owner masters remain distinct.  The earlier independently reviewed
ordinary EPPA induction is retained as an archival comparison
source, but is not invoked in the main phased proof.

\section*{Reading routes}

Each route starts at its headline statement and follows the principal
construction. The cited providers state the foundational inputs needed
at each step.

\paragraph{Rooted ACF and ordinary EPPA.}
Start with \cref{thm:main-rooted-acf,thm:main-ordinary-eppa} and the
phase order $P_n<O_n<U_n$ in \cref{sec:global-phased-induction}.
The rank step \cref{lem:acf-rank-step} uses the normalized relative MI
witness \cref{lem:acf-relative-mi-normal} as its direct construction.
Its fine-owner ordinary EPPA providers and joint owner master
\cref{thm:acf-owner-master} are invoked only on strict lower-rank exact
quotients; finite exact kernels are restored by \cref{lem:acf-finite-lift}.
The MI construction uses the HKN/R07 reservoir and its
all-row correction actions. Ordinary EPPA is the full-palette
specialization of rooted ACF; the owner-master phase at the same rank
comes afterwards.

\paragraph{Ramsey expansions.}
Start with \cref{thm:ramsey-general-ap} and the pure-clone cover
\cref{thm:internal-clone-sap}. Safe splitting and finite-cover elimination
lead to the terminal theorem \cref{thm:internal-ramsey-terminal}.
For that theorem, follow the direct hidden-SAP proof
\cref{thm:typed-hidden-sap}, the qualified bounded-obstruction result
\cref{cor:typed-bounded-hidden}, actual hidden irreducibility and direct HN
transfer \cref{lem:typed-terminal-hidden-irreducible,thm:typed-direct-irreducible-unary-HN}, and
\cref{lem:typed-finite-ordered-real-readout}.
The final AP transfer restricts the ordered clone hidden class to its
unary-closed pure-imaginary sorts, then reads compatible MI tuples using
\cref{thm:ramsey-tuple-readout,lem:ramsey-template-bijection}.
The older safe-splitting and sparse-graph/pruning results in the appendix
remain inputs to this route. The second proof of hidden SAP is an
alternative representative-synchronization construction.

\paragraph{Expansion property.}
Read \cref{thm:ep-unfiltered-main} together with the Ramsey route.
The additional chain is exact trace product and quotient commutation,
automatic core of the genuine terminal labels, the simultaneous-singleton
criterion, synchronized subquotient orders, and same-host full-code EP
transfer. These are
\cref{thm:ep-trace-product,thm:ep-quotient-tower,thm:ep-automatic-core,thm:ep-finite-core-criterion,thm:ep-terminal-orders,lem:ep-tuple-transfer}.
The AP-only character-completion provider supplies the exact-quotient
tower; the relative EP steps compose by
\cref{lem:foundational-relative-EP-composition}.

\paragraph{Stationary independence.}
Read \cref{thm:sir-no-finite-cover} through the strong typed selector
\cref{thm:typed-HS}, then the base-path PC calculation, selected amalgam
and stationarity verification
\cref{lem:sir-free-span-PC,prop:sir-HS-amalgam,thm:sir-HS-positive}.
This route assumes actual real SAP and sortwise NR2. The finite-global-
quotient obstruction and relative-index-two control are separate
boundary results in \cref{sec:sir-scope}.

\section{Scope, proof dependencies, and audit status}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-rooted-acf} is the new headline
working candidate.  Ordinary EPPA is the full-palette
Corollary~\ref{thm:main-ordinary-eppa}.  The owner-master
theorem is conditional on ordinary EPPA for all actual exact
quotients and is invoked only at smaller ranks during the
rooted phase.  This is an explicit noncircular phase order.  The target in the general AP-not-SAP case is
\(\operatorname{acl}_M(A_0)\), and the strong \(S\)-hypothesis is
only on algebraically closed finite amalgamation spans.

The new algebraic and finite owner arguments are stated with
their proofs.  The most delicate inherited part is the
\(\Gamma\)-safe completion of unrecorded HKN/R07 packet matrices
and preservation of all full-row correction automorphisms
under the fixed-named-sort hereditary selector and
path-consistency decoder.  The CCO-specific canonical real-hull
theorem, global correspondence, exact pseudocomplement calculus,
and relative MI construction are also load-bearing.
No AP-only classification theorem, BBGTV/CFSG assertion, or
standalone external FOUND result is used.

The Ramsey theorem in Part~III uses the internal SAPification and
hidden-structure construction, independently of the EPPA witnesses.
The separate \cref{thm:ep-automatic-core,thm:ep-unfiltered-main}
establish automatic core and the expansion property for the
\emph{unfiltered} terminal construction. Its dependency chain is
\cref{thm:ep-trace-product,thm:ep-quotient-tower,thm:ep-automatic-core,thm:ep-finite-core-criterion,thm:ep-terminal-orders,lem:ep-tuple-transfer}.
The AP-only character completion and its termination are provided in
Part~I by \cref{sec:ep-ap-only-safe-character,lem:exact-character-rank-drop};
they do not import the later SAP-specific terminalisation. The final
order and tuple arguments use the Ramsey provider, not EPPA.

The main positive SIR conclusion of Part~V is
\cref{thm:sir-no-finite-cover}: SAP+homomorphic CCO and no
proper finite relative-index equivalence interval imply
stationary independence on every finite real base (including
the empty base). The stronger named-sort result requires
only (NR2) and has its construction and detailed proof in
\cref{lem:sir-free-span-PC,prop:sir-HS-amalgam,thm:sir-HS-positive}.
These statements use the \emph{full invariant-equivalence
lattice}, rather than merely the exact sublattice, and the
strong/SAP rather than equality-inclusive AP-only endpoint
calculus. The latter does not support this proof directly,
as shown by \cref{rem:sir-HS-AP-firewall}.
For context, \cref{thm:sir-finite-global-no} excludes SIR on
the original one-sort reduct with a finite global quotient;
\cref{prop:sir-relative-positive} shows that a relative
index-two packet need not prevent SIR. These are boundary
controls, not a general SIR classification. No SIR is
inferred from Ramsey+EP or EPPA.
No minimality of the expansion language, coherent EPPA, general
multi-original-sort theorem, or non-CCO extension is asserted.

The manuscript records human proofs with explicit imported hypotheses;
none is claimed to be Lean-certified. The current task is to improve
these interfaces and their proofs so that modules can be formalized
independently. Formalization of a module need not wait for a separate
whole-manuscript human audit. The evolving audit findings and remaining
obligations are recorded in the accompanying continuity document.
