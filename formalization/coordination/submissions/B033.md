`Table` stores finite named sorts, finite NONEMPTY diversity palettes for
EVERY ordered pair, involutive typed converse, symmetric same-sort atoms,
and the allowed triangle permutations. `Table.product` is defined by
existential atomic triangle witnesses, hence by union extension, rather
than by an opaque Boolean operation. `TableLaws` adds precisely NONEMPTY
atomic totality and associativity of typed menu products. The given
`Word.Helly` is the finite positive-profile Helly INPUT; it is not derived
from assumed source SAP or CCO. Indexed duplicate families are proved
equivalent to using their finite image family.

`CompleteNetwork` is exactly a complete atomic network with all its
distinct-vertex triangles legal. It has no occupancy bound, forbidden
higher configuration or selected-subclass condition. `Network.Hom` fixes
sort names and preserves recorded atoms, allowing nonadjacent collisions.
`Network.Embedding` is injective and preserves ALL tagged pair colours,
including the literal diagonal. Arbitrary finite carriers, including empty
carriers and repeated named sorts, are allowed.

`SemanticHomCCO` is ordinary semantic cycle feasibility iff existence of
a homomorphism into SOME finite complete allowed target. `Network.Completable`
does not demand that the target carrier equal the input or that the map be
injective. The separately named internal `SemanticCCO` is the stronger
same-vertex completion equivalence. Both are proved; the source conclusion
`ProfileHellyConverse` uses the ordinary `SemanticHomCCO` and retains the
optional distinct-fork premise ONLY in its SAP clause.

`helly_to_cco` implements that precise conditional source conclusion.
`source_providers` also constructs the actual three-distinct-vertex forks
and discharges the SAP clause under the intended fully supported,
nonempty-total FULL-class interpretation. No stronger hidden source provider
is an input.
