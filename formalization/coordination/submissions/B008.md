## Hypotheses and provider discharge

The abstract part uses an inhabited carrier and a binary `Sublattice (Setoid A)`
whose actual represented products commute. The sublattice need not contain
ambient Id/Top, and neither it nor the carrier is assumed finite. `ClassHelly`
is finite, including empty and repeated families; CRT's converse only needs
nonempty finite families. The two-factor fibre product has no Helly or
lattice-distributivity premise. Finite Birkhoff uses an actual finite bounded
distributive lattice. The MI list is nonempty; selected coordinates themselves
need not be MI or distinct. Properness of source quotient coordinates can be
imposed without weakening the proved binary-MI result.

The actual exact sublattice uses only original homogeneous-source and semantic
CCO assumptions. `NonemptyExact` retains real identity and genuine represented
RAW products. Presentations supply invariance, source pair-flips supply
commutation, source convolution supplies joins, and source different-centre
exact BALL Helly supplies class Helly. The existing elementary `ColourEq`
finite-palette injection is reused from `ParityHasse/Original/Equivalences`
and its source algebra; NO Hasse/parity/finite-index result or premise is used.
The full invariant-equivalence lattice is not claimed distributive. SAP,
countability, NR2, EPPA, finite indices and a group section are absent.

Typed quotients are actual equivariant surjective images of their own original
sorts. `TypedPalette.source` generalizes the accepted actual-orbital palette
constructor to independent original origins, reusing its actual diversity atoms,
colours and converses. Finite palettes follow from the realized original
optional colour subtypes; non-existent cross-sort equality is never inserted.
No quotient homogeneity or lifting theorem is assumed.

For arbitrary quotient atomic colours and words, the original exact pullback is
constructed from exact kernels, actual original orbitals and proved source RAW
convolution. The intermediate kernels are retained. Equality pulls back to E_s,
not original Id. Word and intersection lift handle zero-length words and empty
restriction lists. `liftQuotientExact` has no intertwining premise.

`actualPairWord` represents one actual optional quotient colour by ONE genuine
RAW word; old diversity may collapse to quotient equality. `projectedWord`
therefore maps each old word to one actual quotient word. `WordTI` checks only
atoms occurring in that word, with identity letters automatic. Its proof removes
intermediate kernels only under precisely this guard. Source nonempty-result
saturation distribution is applied to the witnessed nonempty old exact relation.
No distribution over an empty-result meet is asserted.

The same-sort route proves `WordTI` from original invariant commutation; the
source containment and explicit saturation remain distinct hypotheses. The typed
route retains presentation-local `WordTI` and endpoint saturation as the stated
sufficient criterion. Neither route asserts an unrestricted factorwise typed
identity. `CanonicalTypedQuotients` finally uses LITERAL `Quotient (J i)` carriers,
constructs their actions from exactness, proves projections onto/equivariant with
the displayed kernels, constructs their actual palette, and instantiates both
forward guarded descent and converse unconditional full exact pullback. No
source instance is left conditional on an unproved quotient provider.

## Consumers and boundaries

`SourceLattice` includes actual represented CRT and real representative
consumers, actual MI quotient surjections, actual two-factor fibre product,
empty coordinate family, repeated coordinates and the one-element exact lattice
with empty irreducible poset. `AbstractCoordinates` independently covers empty
and singleton families without ambient Top in the sublattice.

`CanonicalTypedQuotients` is the downstream actual typed quotient consumer for
descent and converse full exact pullback. `SameSortDescent` and the canonical
same-sort wrapper supply the source descent without TI as a premise. The raw
projection and exact-word distribution inductions handle repeated/intermediate
sorts, nil/equality words, old letters collapsing to quotient equality, and empty
restriction lists. No real-point functionality, ambient lifting or section is
inferred from quotient readout or CRT.
