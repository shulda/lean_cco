## Actual endpoint-fixed paths and receiving consumer

`injective_path_fixed_endpoints` begins with a represented walk which may
repeat real points, including internal visits to its terminal images. Its
formal carrier is Fin(n+1), not a quotient by those repetitions. The designated

length and the distinct actual diversity endpoints. The accepted SAP provider

that actual path by induction. This readout itself requires neither SAP nor
NR2. `purePower_eq_guarded` separately exposes the accepted guarded
positive-word semantics. The direct upper inclusion is available for every
positive power even when the lower-branch source assumptions are absent.

`all_large_powers_with_fixed_paths` genuinely consumes the FULL-source
terminal theorem. ONE positive threshold gives, for every later exponent,
both the palette equality and, for EVERY actual pair of EVERY connected
diversity colour, a Fin(n+1) real embedding with BOTH prescribed endpoints and
ALL consecutive edges literally t. It therefore retains the semantic
injection evidence, rather than returning only an uninstantiated provider.
