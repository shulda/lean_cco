## Actual hypotheses and their boundaries

finite occurring symmetric same-sort atoms and original group homogeneity.
Semantic HOM CCO is a separate source premise. The parity export uses a
finite-relative interval and an actually occurring orbital contained in
its difference. Original `AgeSAP` is a separate additional premise only
for the nontrivial even diversity slice and STRONG upgrade. Hasse has no
SAP or endpoint exactness premise and concerns the full invariant lattice.
Full RAW even equivalences include real identity. Their diversity slices
are the positive STRONG profiles.

The independent counterexample has no source-property provider premise.
It uses the literal countably infinite product on `Point` with Nat block
and clone coordinates and two `ZMod 2` coordinates. `source` is the actual
complete singleton-sort nine-colour structure. All nine atoms occur;
logical equality is outer `none`, while `(some 0,some 0)` is an actual
irreflexive clone diversity atom. `Aut` is the full old colour automorphism
group. `homogeneous`, `strongAmalgamation`, `semanticCCO`,
`coordinate_triangle_defined` and `triangle_defined` are proved for it.

`Orientation P` assumes only constancy on each diagonal K-class and
opposite bits on the two K-classes of each containing X-class. It assumes
no definability, equivariance, regularity or homogeneity of P. The normal
form is derived. `markedModel P` has the nine original binary colours and
the unary bit value, represented by its two complementary unary atoms.
Its `RelationalCompletion.Relational.Structure.AgeAP` is the genuine
ordinary finite-age amalgamation property: both legs embed, but their
off-base images may initially be identified. The proof first excludes
that identification using opposite marks, then forces the clone atom and
uses K-constancy to contradict those marks.

The successful refinements are the explicit global labels p, then (p,q).
`Prefix.source` has literal subtypes of the same original points, actual
occurring child palettes and a whole automorphism group fixing the named
labels. Both prefixes have actual homogeneity, SAP, triangle-definedness
and semantic HOM CCO. `Prefix.oldEquiv`, `Prefix.forgetSecondEquiv`,
`Prefix.forgetSecond_literal` and `Prefix.reduct_same_point` retain the same global old
carrier and exhibit the second refinement as the further q split of the
first. No claim is made that arbitrary fine orientations work.
