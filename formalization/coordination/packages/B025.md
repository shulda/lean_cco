## Acceptance requirements

Prove anchored augmentation on ONE simple diagram. The reduced nonidentity
E word has at least TWO diversity letters: one irreflexive atom cannot be
reflexive E. Treat mixed cycles through the complete anchor by the single
union homomorphism. Equality in individual cap spectra requires a quotient
formal diagram with a descending owner assignment. Joint capped spectra
require raw cycles of the WHOLE augmented diagram.

For each collision repair fix only the displayed-neighbour images (entire
packets in the packet version) and change one formal point/packet. Never fix
an unrecorded collision target. Keep a protected COMPLETE base pointwise and
prove strict decrease of the finite collision potential.

Prove primitivity by a GLOBAL original RAW equivalence, checking thin/thin,
thin/nonthin and both kinds of nonthin products. Infinite classes come from
actual transversal SAP duplication and original transitivity. Local sort
invariance alone is not global invariance.

points. Owner labels do not determine these phases when stabilizers are
nontrivial. Prove finite full-packet age equality, the aligned extension
property and countable closed back-and-forth retaining all literal fine
colours, owner predicates and total t_g. Ambient base alignment is not unique;
the required prescribed finite base isomorphism is fixed.
