## Exact mathematical boundaries

- Original exact menus and nonempty safe traces are different families.
  A nonempty trace need not be original exact. Supply original skeletons
  with an absent/present trace bit and prove arbitrary-multiplier trace
  absorption before using two traced factors. Distribution is required
  only over NONEMPTY evaluated intersections; the empty family is Top.
- The safe branch assumes a GIVEN converse-compatible pure-owner-covariant
  nonempty S which strongly S-amalgamates EVERY finite complete typed span,
  including empty base. Closed-packet amalgamation alone is insufficient.
  This package does not construct S from its own completion conclusion.
- Explicitly extract and prove the finite-menu gate argument on the
  augmented family: bilateral support, nonempty restricted menus, all three
  PC coordinates, idempotence/current image, survivor heredity and covariant
  descent. Do not instantiate an original-H theorem on safe traces.
- No-return retains positive-factor provenance. For arbitrary possibly
  NONEXACT nonempty loop W, prove W^(2|P_tt|)=P_tt and every later power;
  use actual interpreted equality only in the equivalence proof. There is
  no STRONG equality atom. The return bound 4|Gamma|max|P_tt| counts
  iterations, not HKN edges or the later locality constant.
- Both pure Gamma and typed converse fix LITERAL old fine-colour names.
  Same-sort symmetry alone does not replace the symmetric-original source
  premise. Gamma need not act freely or faithfully on owners.
- Moving readings use the MOVED type h^g_st(D)=g h_(gs),t(gD). Prove
  switching, converse, covariance and a legal triangle for ALL independent
  g,k,ell, with no equation g+k=ell. The readings branch uses only original
  exact menus and does not acquire S, safe completion or HKN assumptions.
- Use actual B025 packet inflation for endpoint transport. No equivariant
  origins or group section is chosen. Owner labels do not determine phase.
- The safe completion certificate is injective and existential. Canonical
  greatest PC plus the joint selector supplies simultaneous covariance.
  For actual age membership use the triangle-defined transversal provider
  explicitly, or a separately justified cycle-completion bridge.
