| C07: Finite CRT gives class Helly | Surjectivity for every finite nonempty list gives nonempty-family class Helly. The empty family uses X inhabited; no infinite-family assertion follows. | N03 |
| C08: Nonzero join-irreducibles are join-prime | In a distributive lattice p<=x join y implies p=(p meet x) join (p meet y), so join irreducibility gives a choice; finite joins follow, with empty join excluded by p nonzero. | N04 |
| C09: Finite join-irreducible decomposition | Induct below a in the finite lattice: bottom is the empty join, an irreducible is itself, and every other nonbottom a splits into two smaller elements. | N04 |
