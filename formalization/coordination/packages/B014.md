The product P of its diversity squares is invariant, symmetric, reflexive and lies in F; its powers increase and stabilize to H0=P^n with H0²=H0. Hence H0 is finite-class exact, H0<=F and a_i²<=H0. The empty diversity list gives H0=Id via the empty word. | N05 |

| C28: Single-atom colour transport and central automorphisms | The equivariant endpoint switch sends one actual old pair orbital bijectively to another, so q_g;a is one atom g·a. Triangle constraints enforce two coordinate equivalences and at most two points per cell. Partial-coordinate inconsistency gives a bad specified simple cycle; remaining t-constraints require a bipartite graph in a forced cell, with odd t-cycles as obstructions. Thus the infinite source has semantic CCO. | N08 |
