# Later substantial packages — planning envelopes

These rows guide allocation after B001. They are not launch-ready work
orders. Before assigning one, the preparation project reviews the accepted
Lean API and writes its concrete contract, file boundaries and acceptance
gates. No project chooses one autonomously.

## B002 — RAW exact geometry and actual quotient lifting

RAW C06–C28: private-path/theta combinatorics, full exact convolution and
heterogeneous ball Helly; actual exact quotient orbitals, colour
reconstruction, joint ambient lifting and inheritance. Together with the
B001 elementary exports this covers the current RAW module. No SAP or
STRONG exact calculus is a prerequisite. This is the three-file RAW proof
development, not 23 separate assignments.

## B003 — STRONG exact calculus, finite PC and triangle fullness

STRONG C06–C13: injective CCO completion, exact profiles/Helly/convolution,

## B019 — full fine-coloured products and prescribed kernel actions

`owner_action` C14–C29 is a second substantial package. Prove the natural
automorphism isomorphism of actual FULL induced fibre products, separating
set-level commuting-equivalence CRT from equality-inclusive exact fine-colour
readout and recognizable quotient-colour descent. B002 supplies that
readout; accepted B001 quotient/action representations are reused.

The abstract action branch takes both a full SET pullback carrier and its
full group identity as inputs. For a nonnested target T it needs D->T
prescribed point lifting and C's prescribed owner point lifting. For a
nested product target it needs the factorization and its own group identity,
but not C's point property. The independent sparse-source EPPA consumer
needs its partial-map providers and actual quotient colours, not these
point-kernel premises. Empty systems are handled directly. Finite controls
include nonfull and extra-colour failures, exact-owner matching, an onto
but insufficient C action, and a nonsplit positive quotient action.

B019 can run alongside B018 after its accepted RAW dependencies: it takes
prescribed factor properties as quantified arguments and does not wait for
the specific provider construction. The simultaneous owner master in
owner_master/04 and its rooted joint-owner/rank consumers remain separate
future preparation; these two rows are not an assertion of their proof.
