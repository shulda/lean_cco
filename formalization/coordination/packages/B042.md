the internal high-girth/high-chromatic graph produces a partial monochrome
diagram all of whose cycles fold/backtrack into F. Semantic HOM CCO gives
a whole homomorphism, which irreflexivity makes a forbidden proper finite
colouring. Thus every atom graph is bipartite. Neither homogeneity,
transitivity nor SAP is needed. Independently code each point by its
atom-graph bipartition bits to obtain `|F|<=2^d_F`, also without those
extra hypotheses. This is internal support, not a fifth numbered export.

p-tuples. An odd-prime-order ACTUAL automorphism has a moved p-orbit,
whose successive pair colour is constant, contradicting bipartiteness.

assumption in both short source corollaries; oriented mod-three differences

moved-point step, not for generic later actions.

invariant-equivalence cover. This is the full invariant lattice, not only

Finite actual real fibres inherit transitivity, induced homogeneity and
HOM CCO from C09/C10. Their full pair orbitals are exactly the occurring
