   each old orbital onto one actual quotient orbital, and every quotient
   diversity orbital is such an image. For a nonidentity equivalence at
   least one old diversity orbital becomes equality, giving a STRICT rank
   decrease. Exactness separately supplies standing source inheritance;
   no surjectivity between full automorphism groups is presumed. Id is not
   a strict recursion step; Top and the singleton source are valid cases.

   all source requests and transitivity moves the literal root. Full

   by ONE base-fixing finite-coset avoidance motion. This elementary
   admissibility proof uses neither CCO nor EPPA nor a same-rank master.
   Its resulting amalgam need not itself be closed.

   directions. With one direction, use B028 directly with H=Top and the
   singleton owner; only ordinary EPPA in the strict quotient M/E is
   recursive. With multiple directions, use the MAXIMAL exact hats and

   Check every fine-owner EPPA quotient M/(E_i join C_i) separately.

   identify it with the ENTIRE compatible owner-tuple set. M3 to Top gives

   acl_M(A0) is the full compatible product of the factor closures, not
