| C19: Finite translate-Helly subset is an affine coset | Assume V is a finite elementary abelian 2-group, A is a NONEMPTY subset, and EVERY finite pairwise-intersecting family of translates A+v has nonempty total intersection. Choose a largest-dimensional affine subspace lying in A and translate it to K<=A. If a lies in A outside K, W=K+<a> is contained in A+A. The translates A+w for w in W meet pairwise; the stated translate-Helly assumption gives z+W<=A, contradicting maximal dimension. Thus A is an affine coset of a subgroup. | N05 |
| C20: Bisaturated invariant rigid slice contains whole arrows | Each rigid arrow is one actual quotient pair orbital because its equivariant graph is transitive. Distinct arrows are disjoint by C11.
