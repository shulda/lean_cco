## Exact scope

`eq:acf-finite-acl`, from

## Required mathematical contract

K is an ACTUAL invariant FULL EXACT equivalence with finite real classes.
It need not be the greatest finite-class equivalence F_Q; no thin-packet
normalization is assumed. K=Id and finite sources are allowed. Retain the
actual original symmetric homogeneous source and the countability scope
of the prepared joint exact-sort lifting API.

1. Prove both directions of
   `acl_Q(A) = pi_K^-1(acl_(Q/K)(pi_K A))` for EVERY finite real A,
   including empty A. Distinguish the original point stabilizer from the
   full quotient point stabilizer. The converse lifts finite joint maps
   fixing every original A point to infinitely many distinct K-classes;
   it does not posit a section of quotient automorphism groups.
2. Descend GIVEN strong closed-S amalgamation using full finite inverse
   images of closed quotient factors. Align the complete base, keep all
   whole K-classes, and prove strongness survives projection. The quotient
   safe palette requires EVERY original fine colour of a full cross
   matrix to be safe, not one chosen representative colour.
3. Fix ONE finite induced quotient witness Dbar and its full inverse image
   D BEFORE choosing requests. Recognize K equality and quotient colours
   from the original fine language, so original partial maps project to
   injective quotient partial isomorphisms. A compatible real partial map
   plus an automorphism on ALL named K-classes of Dbar forms a genuine
   joint partial isomorphism. Its ambient lift preserves that same D.
4. For an original unsafe clique, take the SET of its distinct quotient
   classes; a singleton projection is unsafe vacuously. A quotient rooted
   automorphism picks a projected original root. Choose ONE actual a in
   the original A0 above it and jointly lift the singleton z->a together
   with the ENTIRE quotient automorphism. The whole clique lands in the
   real algebraic closure by the identity already proved. No independent
   permutations of finite K-fibres are allowed.
5. For the stronger relative clause, H must also be an ACTUAL FULL EXACT
   equivalence, possibly nonnested with K. The common owner quotient is
   Q/(K join H). B is its COMPLETE finite inverse owner packet over Bbar.
   Prove B finite and the owner image of D exactly B using
   K;H=K join H. Prescribe gamma on ALL B and check every real/H and K/H
   mixed pair, the latter via the common-join orbital identity.
6. Retain the stated onto `Aut(B)->Aut(Bbar)` map and fibre-transitive
   kernel for relative selected-root lifting. Correct ONE chosen lift by
   ONE kernel element, then jointly lift the literal real root and both
   entire finite quotient actions. This property is separate from source
   requests with their externally prescribed gamma.
