| C01: Invariant owner action and normal kernel | Every original automorphism induces a permutation of the invariant F-classes; this is a group action and its kernel N is a normal subgroup. Classes may move in Gamma, while N fixes them individually setwise. | N01 |
| C02: Local intrinsic and pair groups | H_C and H_CD are finite permutation groups, with H_CD a subgroup of H_C times H_D. Preservation includes unary instances, within-fibre loops and both directions of cross relations. Local pair projections need not be onto intrinsic groups. | N01 |
| C03: Finite solution groups and restrictions | Sol(S) is a finite subgroup, and restriction from a larger finite fibre set sends solutions to solutions. In general it is not surjective. Empty S has its unique empty tuple. | N02 |
| C04: Global binary patching gives the actual restriction isomorphism | rho is an injective group homomorphism. Any product tuple satisfying every local H_CD condition assembles into one bijection of ALL points, preserving every relation in both directions by arity<=2. It lies in N, so rho identifies N exactly with the global pair-CSP solution group, including empty X. No homogeneity or countability is used. | N02 |
| C05: Actual binding marginals are globally subdirect and reconstruct N | P_CD is subdirect over actual B_C,B_D by definition of those images, even when B_C is proper in H_C. The full family satisfying all ACTUAL P_CD conditions lies in the local CSP and hence in rho(N); conversely every actual N tuple satisfies them. This global reconstruction does not assert an analogous finite projection identity. | N02 |
| C06: Countable exhaustion and nonempty finite solution levels | For finite S and one fixed tuple g on S, assume EVERY finite T containing S admits a solution restricting to g. A countable exhaustion T_n yields nonempty finite levels of such solutions; restriction connects successive levels. No coherent family is assumed in the premise; finitely many fibres are handled directly. | N03 |
| C07: Explicit finite-continuation path construction | For the solution tree with finite levels of C06, level zero is the singleton prescribed tuple g and every later node restricts to it. Thus the root has arbitrarily long continuations. Among finitely many children of an infinitely continuing node one has arbitrarily long continuations, else finitely many failure levels would give a common bound. Recursively choose it to obtain a coherent path. Finite fibre families were handled directly; no external Konig theorem is imported. | N03 |
| C08: Compact projection exact quantifiers | A tuple g on finite S is in pr_S rho(N) iff for EVERY finite T>=S there EXISTS h in Sol(T) restricting to that SAME g. Sufficiency uses C07; union of its coherent path satisfies each global pair constraint and hence C04. Necessity is restriction. This also means a nonextendible tuple has one finite obstruction T. | N03 |
| C09: Relative owner homogeneity removes hidden finite constraints | Under A04 a tuple in Sol(S) is a finite partial isomorphism on the whole selected fibre union, preserving every owner predicate. Homogeneity extends it to Aut(X^own)=N, proving pr_S N=Sol(S), B_C=H_C and P_CD=H_CD. This does not follow from homogeneity of X alone. | N04 |
| C10: Finite pair annihilators define the global kernel | For intrinsic H_C=V_C, each H_CD is exactly the kernel of its finite-dimensional annihilator, even if nonsubdirect. Span all those rows in D to form R; global patching gives rho(N)=R^perp. Unary-supported defining rows are allowed only when true for the relevant local groups; for one isolated fibre R=0. | N05 |
| C11: Countable linear-functional separation without compactness | The direct sum D is countable from A03 and finite coordinate duals. If ell is outside R, define f(r+epsilon ell)=epsilon on R+<ell>. Enumerate D and extend f one vector at a time, assigning zero to each newly independent vector. Its union is linear on D, zero on R and one on ell. No Zorn or new separation provider is required. | N05 |
| C12: Algebraic dual of finite-support tests is the full product | A linear f:D->F2 restricts to each finite V_C*, hence evaluates at a unique x_C in V_C by the explicit finite-bidual basis formula. Conversely each product tuple evaluates all finite-support tests. Thus Hom(D,F2) is canonically the FULL product, with no finite-support restriction on x. | N05 |
| C13: Double annihilator and restricted projection formula | C11/C12 separate every ell outside R from R by an actual product tuple in R^perp, so Ann(R^perp)=R. A test supported on finite S annihilates pr_S N iff its zero-extension annihilates N; hence Ann(pr_S N)=R intersect D_S. | N06 |
| C14: Finite marginal constraints span their exact test rows | On finite S, imposing every actual singleton B_C and pair P_CD marginal has annihilator span{R intersect D_T: T subset S, |T|<=2}, by C13 and finite-dimensional duality. The pair groups here are actual projections, not arbitrary intrinsic H_CD; singleton constraints must remain for |S|=1. | N06 |
| C15: Width-two criterion iff actual finite projection has only marginal constraints | Both pr_S N and the group satisfying its singleton/pair marginals are finite-dimensional subspaces. They are equal iff their annihilators coincide, equivalently R intersect D_S is generated by its support<=2 subspaces inside S. Empty S gives zero test space and one empty motion. No arbitrary-variable elimination claim is made. | N06 |
| C16: Full restriction to an entire named finite sort is onto | Under A06 any automorphism of finite induced P_i is an actual finite partial isomorphism of X, so homogeneity extends it. Thus full Gamma->R_i is onto and each ACTUAL P_ij is subdirect over the intrinsic R_i,R_j. Empty named sorts, if allowed, give the trivial group. | N04 |
| C17: Strong finite projection on entire named sorts | For finite S, union of g_i in intrinsic R_i whose every pair is an actual P_ij motion is a bijection of the finite full named-sort union. Actual pair witnesses preserve every cross binary relation, intrinsic maps preserve internal/unary relations; homogeneity gives one full Gamma extension. This includes empty and singleton S, and does not require an F partition or countability. | N04 |
| C18: Linear Goursat representation of actual subdirect pairs | For an actual subdirect P_ij<=V_i times V_j, let N_i={(x,0) in P_ij}, N_j similarly. Pair membership defines a canonical linear isomorphism V_i/N_i -> V_j/N_j. Using one common W_ij gives surjective maps pi_ij,rho_ij with P_ij={(x,y):pi_ij(x)=rho_ij(y)}. The trivial quotient for a full product is allowed. | N04 |
| C19: Named finite image equals check-map kernel and rank count | Under A07 the actual full Gamma image on finite S is ker Phi_S by C17/C18. Rank-nullity gives its cardinality 2^(sum_i dim V_i - rank Phi_S). This counts the finite IMAGE, not the full possibly infinite Gamma. Empty and singleton S have no pair rows. | N04 |
| C20: Flat graph transport from actual full-group lifts | If the actual pair images on three distinct entire finite named sorts are graphs of isomorphisms theta_ij, theta_ik and theta_jk, extend any chosen g_i to Gamma by C16. Its simultaneous three restrictions force theta_ik=theta_jk composed with theta_ij. This argument does not need elementary-abelian groups and does not apply to freely chosen pair maps. | N04 |

Its ABC pair marginals are unrestricted, but its ABC projection is the even-parity subgroup; a lone A flip on A union B union C is a partial binary isomorphism with no global extension. Thus the source is not homogeneous and hidden width-three elimination is real. | N07 |
| C22: Homogeneous moving-owner countercontrol with intrinsic/binding gap | For X=S3 name every R_g(x,y) iff y=xg.
