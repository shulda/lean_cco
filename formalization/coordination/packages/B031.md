## Exact scope

Eight definitions, 25 claims C01-C25 and six implementation tasks expand
exactly ONE new Foundations II result, `lem:consolidated-unary-closure`.
There are no auxiliary exports. This is human proof preparation, not a
Lean implementation or a certificate of the whole construction.

The result has three separately scoped real-closure conclusions:

1. For an INFINITE original transitive standing source with unique minimal
   infinite invariant equivalence E, `acl_M(A)=F[A]` for EVERY finite real
   A, including empty A. F is the greatest finite-class invariant
   equivalence and need not be exact.
2. Naming ANY finite collection B of ACTUAL E-classes individually as
   imaginary parameters does not enlarge the REAL algebraic closure.
   These are owner classes, not fixed real representatives.
3. If the source is additionally finite-exact-normalized and B is invariant
   under the induced full finite thin-group action, the ENTIRE owner union
   N_B is F-saturated and its FULL induced owner-named automorphism group
   gives `acl_N_B(A)=F[A]` for finite A contained in N_B.

These three closure conclusions use no countability assumption. The
ancillary owner-named homogeneity proof C22-C24 separately retains the
countability required by the current joint-exact lifting API. It is not a
premise of any closure equality.

## Required mathematical contract

1. Keep the unique minimal infinite direction in the FULL invariant
   equivalence lattice. E need not be the unique minimal nonidentity
   equivalence. F and spoke equivalences Gamma need not be exact. If
   x is outside F[A], every actual spoke equivalence is infinite-class
   and contains the SAME E. The canonical anchored hull therefore contains
   infinite E[x]. Contrapose ONLY the proved algebraic-implies-finite-hull
   direction. Empty A uses ambient infinitude and transitivity directly.
2. For finite individually named actual E-classes, choose good real
   representatives only as a proof device. For each bad atom its
   neighbours of x in one E-class occupy at most ONE finite F-class.
   Remove the finitely many bad sets and x, then choose a genuine diversity
   spoke whose Gamma contains E. Apply the real-hull contrapositive over
   A plus the representatives. The real-representative stabilizer is a
   SUBGROUP of the actual-owner stabilizer, so its infinite orbit sits
   inside the latter orbit. No representatives become named parameters.
3. In the normalized branch contradict any nonthin F atom using I03 C22,
   and then use I04 under the explicitly established thinness. Each t_g
   is a total 0-definable unique-neighbour function and an ORIGINAL-global
   automorphism. It may move individually named owners and therefore need
   NOT be an automorphism of the owner-named N_B.
4. Prove both directions of the owner saturation criterion: B is invariant
   under G iff its ENTIRE union N_B contains full F-packets. Under this
   condition each t_g is total on N_B, and each t_g(a) is A-definable
   inside that induced structure. This gives the finite lower closure
   target without claiming that each t_g fixes the named owners.
5. The ambient stabilizer fixing A and EACH B owner restricts to an IMAGE
   subgroup of FULL Aut(N_B/A). No injectivity or surjectivity of the
   restriction map is assumed. The ambient point orbit stays entirely
   inside N_B and equals its restriction-image orbit. Its infinitude for
   x outside F[A] forces the full induced-group orbit infinite. Together
   with the definable finite target this proves the induced-union equality.
6. Handle A empty, B empty and finite full-F unions explicitly. Empty B
   gives empty N_B and only empty A, outside a nonempty-source interface.
   Finite packet unions in N_B are absolutely closed there. No rooted
   empty-input or EPPA statement is asserted.
7. For the SEPARATE countable homogeneity branch, show p union Id_B
   preserves ALL mixed real/owner pairs, including owners not incident
   with the real partial domain. One joint-exact lift fixes the entire
   finite B and restricts to an automorphism extending p. This proof does
   not use the induced-union closure result, normalization or G-invariance.
