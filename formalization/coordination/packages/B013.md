| C05: Relative loop criterion | For J<=K, a looped diversity atom in K outside J gives arbitrarily many distinct J-classes in one K-class by triangle-full monochromatic cliques. | C06: Core monotonicity and finite refinement | Kappa(J)<=J; cores are monotone. Every looped atom in J is contained in its core, so [J:kappa(J)] is finite by the loop criterion. If J<=K has finite index they have the same looped atoms and the same core. | N02 |

| C09: Loop connectivity is a positive atomic word | For a looped diversity atom, a<=a² and Id<=a².
