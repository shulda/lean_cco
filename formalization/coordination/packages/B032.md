## Exact scope

Eight definitions, 21 claims C01-C21 and five implementation tasks expand
exactly ONE Foundations III result, `lem:typed-terminal-pure-power`.
Its one auxiliary equation is `eq:typed-terminal-pure-power`; this is not
an additional numbered result. No human TeX is changed.

Fix one inhabited named transitive sort a in the actual homogeneous
complete finite-language typed symmetric source U, and an ACTUAL
same-sort diversity atom t. With visible SAP and FULL original sortwise
NR2, there is a POSITIVE integer N_t such that EVERY positive STRONG
pure power of t of length n>=N_t is exactly the diversity connectivity
palette F_t of its undirected orbital graph. Literal equality is absent
from both output palettes. There is no zeroth STRONG power.

Finite language and finite palettes do NOT mean finite real carriers.
The source theorem quantifies over actual t; if that diversity palette is
empty, its universal assertion is vacuous. Countability, primitivity,
semantic CCO, exactness and distributivity are not assumptions.

## Required mathematical contract

1. Use G=FULL Aut(U) of the WHOLE original typed source acting on a,
   rather than substituting the possibly larger group of an induced
   reduct. Actual old same-sort atom names are its genuine pair orbitals.
   Symmetry and transitivity give each point a distinct t-neighbour and
   transport whole connected components onto one another.
2. Define connectivity C by finite t-walks and even connectivity Y by
   walks of even length, allowing the zero-length walk ONLY for graph
   reflexivity. These are actual G-invariant equivalences with Y<=C.
   If one component is bipartite, all are. Each has an edge and two
   nonempty parity sides, so the relative index [C:Y] is EXACTLY two.
   FULL original sortwise NR2 excludes this interval. C may be proper
   below Top and Y need not be exact: neither global-quotient-only NR2
   nor exact-sublattice-only NR2 suffices.
3. In each nonbipartite connected component obtain an odd closed walk
   at the chosen endpoint. For a connected distinct x,y choose a walk
   of length l>=1 and an odd closed detour of length m>=3. The two
   x-to-y walks of lengths l and l+m have opposite parity; two-edge
   backtracks give EVERY sufficiently large length, not just one parity
   or a subsequence of lengths. This whole graph argument is SAP-free.
4. F_t is finite and NONEMPTY, since t itself belongs to it. Select ONE
   realized ordered pair for EACH c in F_t and one positive threshold
   for that representative. Take their finite maximum N_t>=1 before
   varying n or later endpoint pairs. Actual pair homogeneity transports
   the representative walks to EVERY c-pair while preserving the same
   literal t labels and the exact length. No threshold optimality is
   claimed.
5. For n>=N_t and a fixed DISTINCT real pair x!=y, form the finite typed
   path diagram on Fin(n+1), all vertex sort labels a and successive
   edge labels t. Different occurrences are distinct FORMAL vertices
   even when the represented walk repeats real points. The designated
   set {0,n} is injective under the original homomorphism because n>=1
   and x!=y. Invoke B001 SAP injectivization with the forbidden set empty,
   fixing BOTH actual endpoints and preserving every edge. It supplies
   an injective positive typed path, not merely another homomorphism.
6. Use the positive guarded-profile correspondence to conclude
   F_t subset {t}^{starprod n} for every n>=N_t. Equality and RAW closed
   walks are proof tools, never STRONG diversity outputs. For the reverse
   inclusion, positive left-associated powers stay within connectivity
   by the actual legal-triangle recursion. This upper inclusion needs
   neither SAP nor NR2. Under SAP the usual positive-word associativity
   and path semantics agree with that recursion, yielding the stated
   equality for ALL n>=N_t.

The SAP and graph branches must stay separate: FULL NR2 is used for the
