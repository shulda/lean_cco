## N01: Actual rank, source inheritance and boundary cases

Let the finite old equality-inclusive orbital partition be
`{Id} union D`. For any invariant equivalence E and any old orbital a,
the image of a on pairs of E-classes is a single orbit of the old group:
equivariance maps any two representatives in a to the corresponding
class pair. Conversely every actual quotient pair orbit has a
representative pair of real points, hence is the image of some old atom.
An old atom contained in E maps to quotient equality. Any old atom
outside E maps to diversity; invariance makes these alternatives uniform
on the entire atom. If E>Id, choose x!=y with xEy. Their old diversity
atom lies in E. Therefore the surjection onto quotient diversity uses a
proper subset of the old diversity atoms, proving strict decrease.
We need no exactness for this counting fact and no surjectivity between
the full automorphism groups. Exactness is used separately when I01
supplies the standing source on the actual named quotient. At E=Id the
canonical quotient is the original source and its rank is unchanged.

For finite nonempty M and nonempty A0, every orbit is finite, so
acl_M(A0)=M. Take W=M. Homogeneity extends each partial automorphism of
A0 within this same finite structure, and transitivity moves a chosen
root into any chosen a in A0. Every unsafe clique is already in the
required target M; W is closed. A nonempty source of diversity rank zero
has one point, because two distinct real points would name a diversity
atom. It is covered by this finite boundary. None of these arguments
uses CCO or S. Applying the prepared source inheritance theorem at Id
also gives the actual triangle-defined source needed downstream; no
new unexpanded triangle-fullness hypothesis is introduced.

## N02: Full-palette strong amalgamation and closure conventions

Real acl uses FULL Aut(M/A). Finite relational homogeneity gives finitely
many point orbits over a finite real A. The union of the finitely many
finite orbits is finite. It is a closure operator. A finite absolutely

for this palette and for every other palette.
