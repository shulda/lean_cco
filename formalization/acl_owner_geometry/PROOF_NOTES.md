## N00: Branch arguments, orbit algebraicity and reuse

Assumptions in module.json are arguments of different quantified lemmas,
not all extra global premises of a single source theorem. Abstract group
avoidance uses A08 only; its finite coset-cover proof is internal. C19 and
C35 are independent group/invariant-lattice facts. Actual source branches
retain countability through the currently prepared RAW/joint-lifting type.
Finite-exact normalization is restricted to correspondence coordinates;
harmless-owner and quotient-closure results do not assume it. Strong closed
S-amalgamation enters only unsafe concentration and clique conclusions.

Real algebraicity means finite orbit under the FULL pointwise automorphism
stabilizer. In a countable homogeneous structure in a finite complete binary
language, a finite real base has finitely many one-point orbits: its finite
spoke-colour tuples determine them by homogeneity. Its acl is therefore a
finite union of finite orbits. Monotonicity follows by taking a smaller
stabilizer. If a finite B is contained in acl(A), its orbit as an ordered
tuple under Aut(M/A) is finite, so Aut(M/A,B) has finite index in Aut(M/A).
A point with finite orbit under the former then has finite orbit under the
latter by a finite coset decomposition. This proves finite-base transitivity
of algebraicity and idempotence of acl. The receiving project must express
these elementary conversions in its chosen representations; they are not
unproved constructional hypotheses.

The finite-packet, RAW exact, actual quotient-source, exact CRT, canonical
real-hull and bounded-character providers are already prepared. In
particular real_closure C11 already proves singleton closure acl(a)=F[a]
and one-direction closure acl(A)=F[A]. It does not prove harmlessness of
imaginary owners. The latter is proved here instead of being inferred from
a unary real closure statement. The finite index support C02 is included
explicitly in finite_packets' crossing-index export support map; this is
metadata about an existing proof, not a new theorem or changed API premise.

Only declared source_result_files and selected additional results are
counted as complete. The three closure results selected from maximal_cores/01
are foundational despite their physical position. Their early placement
here breaks a potential packaging cycle: maximal pseudocomplements use
original coordinates, whose closure pullback uses APJ.

## N01: Full packets and FULL quotient invariant intervals — D01, C35

All original invariant binary relations are unions of the finitely many
actual named orbitals. Symmetry makes their represented products commute.
The finite-packet provider constructs F=Sigma(Id), the greatest finite-class
invariant equivalence; F may be nonexact. It also supplies monotone cores
kappa(R), finite relative indices [R:kappa(R)] and word exactness of cores.
Gamma(a) is the generated invariant equivalence of the actual symmetric
atom a, not a claim that every such generated equivalence is exact.

C35 is a separate general fact. Let G act transitively on a nonempty carrier
and K be invariant. Name EVERY actual old-G pair orbital on the quotient.
The old G-image acts by automorphisms and is transitive within each named
pair orbital. Conversely the FULL automorphism group preserves each named
orbital. Hence its pair orbitals are exactly these old-G orbitals. A binary
relation invariant under the old G-image is a union of these orbitals and
is therefore invariant under the FULL quotient automorphism group as well.
This proves equality of the two notions of invariant equivalence, without
surjectivity from G onto that full automorphism group.

Ordinary equivalence quotient transport now identifies every original
invariant H>=K with H/K, and its inverse is literal pullback. Inclusion,
meets and joins are preserved. A class of H/K is exactly the set of
K-classes in the corresponding H-class, so finite-class and finite-relative-
index assertions are the stated class counts. This is the FULL invariant
interval, not merely an interval of a selected quotient-action subgroup.
Exactness transport is a further assertion when needed; no arbitrary
nonexact equivalence is declared exact here. Source inheritance is supplied
separately by the prepared actual exact quotient provider.

## N02: Finite exact intersections — D02, C04

Assume finite-exact normalization. For an exact invariant equivalence E and
nonempty actual symmetric atom a, D=E intersect a squared is full RAW exact
by exact product/meet closure. It contains real identity: transitivity and
nonemptiness give an a-neighbour of every point, and symmetry returns in
two steps. D is symmetric and reflexive.

Its positive powers increase; since there are finitely many named orbitals,
they stabilize. At a sufficiently large stabilized exponent n their square
is the same relation, so the stable power is a reflexive symmetric
transitive invariant equivalence. It is exact by repeated exact products.
If D is contained in F then every power is contained in F, because F is
an equivalence. The stable equivalence has finite real classes. Normalization
forces it to be Id. Since Id<=D is contained in that stable power, D=Id.
No assertion that F itself is exact or trivial was used.

## N03: Global correspondence cut — D03, C05–C10

Fix a NONIDENTITY exact invariant EQUIVALENCE E. Define R_E to be the union
of every RAW atom a (including Id) satisfying E intersect a squared=Id.
This is a relation on the WHOLE original carrier, not a selected owner pair.
It is invariant, symmetric and reflexive. Reuse ONLY the finite-square
regularizer support finite_packets C19/C22: its stabilized product H0 is a
finite-class exact equivalence containing the square of EVERY F atom.
Normalization forces H0=Id. Thus each F atom has square Id and F<=R_E (C05).
This derives thinness; it does not invoke a theorem whose hypothesis already
assumes thinness. If an atom a lies below both E and R_E, its square is below
E and its intersection with E is Id. Hence a squared=Id. Its unique-neighbour
involution has finite components of size at most two, so Id union a is a
finite-class invariant equivalence and a lies in F. Therefore
E intersect R_E=E intersect F (C06).

For C07, fix a below R_E and one point z. If x and x prime are both
a-neighbours of z in the SAME E-class, symmetry makes them related by
E intersect a squared=Id. They coincide. This is functionality into each
fixed E-owner class; it does not say that z has only one a-neighbour in
total.

To prove transitivity, realize x a y b z with a,b below R_E and outer
atomic colour c. We must show E intersect c squared=Id. Take x prime E x
with the same c-colour to z. If c=Id then x=z=x prime already; equality
cases can be removed before forming a two-real-point partial map. Otherwise
x and z are distinct, as are x prime and z, so x->x prime,z->z is a genuine
injective real partial isomorphism. Name the E-classes of x,y,z and fix all
these owners individually. The real E-projection of x is unchanged.
Nested mixed orbital saturation says every mixed real/owner pair is
determined by the relevant owner pair, so the whole finite request is a
partial isomorphism in the actual joint real/E language (C08).

Joint exact lifting supplies ONE original automorphism g. It fixes z and
the owner E[y]. As g(y) and y are b-neighbours of z in that owner class,
C07 forces g(y)=y. Now x prime=g(x) and x are a-neighbours of y in one
E-class, so C07 forces x prime=x. For any pair in E intersect c squared,
choose its common c-neighbour and realize an a,b path for one c-edge;
invariance of the actual c-orbital supplies that path for every c-edge.
The argument therefore proves E intersect c squared=Id, so every outer
colour of a;b lies in R_E. This proves transitivity and C09. No composition
law for an independently chosen conditional-bijection groupoid is used.

If R_E is finite-class, maximality of F and F<=R_E give R_E=F. Otherwise
its packet core C=kappa(R_E) has finite relative index, is infinite and
nonidentity, and is exact. E intersect C is an exact equivalence inside
E intersect F, so is finite-class and equals Id by normalization. Each of
E and C is a nonidentity original exact equivalence. The actual quotient-
source provider gives standing inheritance. The quotient pair-orbital
partition groups old pair orbitals; its equality block contains old Id AND
at least one old diversity atom for a nonidentity kernel. All other blocks
are unions of old atoms, so the number of diversity blocks strictly drops.
This local finite counting proves the rank clause for both quotients (C10). No ordinary EPPA, safe palette, owner master or rooted theorem
enters this structural dichotomy.

## N04: Anchors, unsafe pairs and the rook lemma — D04/D05, C11–C14

Enumerate ALL minimal infinite original equivalences E_i. They are exact
by the prepared minimal-cut theorem. Put R_i=R_(E_i) and C_i=kappa(R_i).
These OLD packet cores keep their notation; they will not be redefined
as later maximal exact pseudocomplements.

Suppose x E_i u but x is not F-related to u. The colour b=col(x,u) generates
an invariant equivalence Gamma(b) inside E_i. It has infinite classes,
since otherwise it lies in F and x would be F-related to u. Minimality
therefore gives Gamma(b)=E_i. For x distinct from v, the canonical real-hull
criterion says algebraicity over u,v forces
E_i intersect Gamma(col(x,v)) to have finite classes. In particular
E_i intersect col(x,v) squared lies in F and equals Id by N02; hence
x R_i v. Conversely, if x R_i v, the actual definable set
E_i[u] intersect col(x,v)[v] consists of x alone: two members are related
by E_i intersect col(x,v) squared=Id. Thus x is algebraic over u,v.
If x=v, algebraicity and R_i reflexivity are immediate. This is C11.

For i!=j, E_i intersect E_j is a proper equivalence below each minimal
infinite direction, so has finite classes. It is exact and normalization
makes it Id. Each atom inside E_j has square inside E_j, so belongs to
R_i. Thus E_j<=R_i and symmetrically E_i<=R_j (C12).

Assume an actual unsafe pair x,y belongs to neither R_i nor R_j. Choose
u in E_i[x] outside F[x], and v in E_j[y] outside F[y]; the infinite E
classes allow these choices. Because E_j<=R_i and y R_i v, x not R_i y
implies x not R_i v. Similarly y not R_j u. C11 puts both x and y outside
the finite closed base acl(u,v). Take the finite closed extensions
acl(u,v,x) and acl(u,v,y) and strongly S-amalgamate them over that base.
Their off-base images x prime,y prime are distinct and the new cross
edge is required to have a colour in S.

Let a=col(x,y) be the OLD unsafe fine colour. Complete old spoke colours
to BOTH u and v are preserved, not just two coarse incidences. The first
anchor forces the new pair into E_i;a and the second into E_j;a. RAW
nonempty meet-product distribution and E_i intersect E_j=Id give

    (E_i;a) intersect (E_j;a) = (E_i intersect E_j);a = a.

Thus the new pair has literally the old atom a, contradicting a outside S.
This proves C13 atomwise and pointwise. The palette hypothesis is precisely
strong closed amalgamation, not original SAP over an arbitrary finite base.

The elementary rook lemma C14 is as follows. Suppose every pair of a set Z
is related by one of two equivalences R,T. If all pairs are T-related then
Z is in a T-class. Otherwise choose u,v with u R v and not u T v. Any
w outside their R-class would have to be T-related to both u,v, which would
make u T v. Hence every w is in that R-class. Empty and singleton sets
satisfy the conclusion in the usual vacuous/existential reading.

Apply this to each pair R_i,R_j on an unsafe clique. If two indices were
both unconcentrated, the corresponding rook conclusion would fail. Thus
at most one index is unconcentrated. A condition only saying each pair
belongs to the union of THREE equivalences is weaker and does not suffice;
the controls include that counterexample.

## N05: Original canonical cores and unary closure — C15–C20

All R_i contain F. If their intersection were larger than F, it would have
infinite classes and contain a minimal infinite E_j, because the FULL
invariant-equivalence lattice is finite and the E_i list is exhaustive.
But E_j intersect R_j is finite-class by N03. This contradiction proves
intersection_i R_i=F. The meet of exact C_i=kappa(R_i) lies inside F and
is exact, so normalization makes it Id (C15).

For j!=i, E_j<=R_i and E_j=kappa(E_j) by the minimal-cut theorem. Core
monotonicity gives E_j<=C_i. Thus for at least two directions each C_i is
infinite and nonidentity. Also E_i intersect C_i is exact inside finite
E_i intersect R_i and hence is Id. E_i injects to Q_i=M/C_i and Q_i is
infinite. The actual quotient-source theorem supplies standing inheritance
and the same local orbital-block count as C10 proves strict rank decrease
(C16).

If an invariant D is strictly above R_i, choose an atom a in D outside R_i.
Then E_i intersect a squared is not Id. If E_i intersect D were proper in
E_i it would have finite classes, putting E_i intersect a squared inside
F and contradicting N02. Hence E_i<=D. The least strict coarsening of R_i
is therefore E_i join R_i. Its relative index over R_i is
[E_i:E_i intersect R_i]=infinite, since E_i is infinite and its displayed
intersection is finite-class. No nontrivial finite-index coarsening of R_i
can exist, so Sigma(R_i)=R_i (C17).

The packet provider gives [R_i:C_i] finite and
Sigma(C_i)=Sigma(R_i)=R_i. C35 transports the FULL invariant interval to
the actual quotient Q_i: its greatest finite-class equivalence is exactly
F_i=R_i/C_i, and it has the unique least strict coarsening supplied by C17.
This is C18. The old-core Q_i is NOT asserted finite-exact-normalized.

Here is the explicit countability-free lattice bridge C19, to avoid
silently inferring a stronger closure statement from a meet-irreducible
name. In a finite commuting invariant-equivalence lattice with greatest
finite-class F and least strict coarsening D, let U be a minimal infinite
equivalence. D<=U join F, and
[U join F:U]=[F:U intersect F] is finite. Restriction gives
[D:D intersect U] finite. D has infinite classes because it is above the
greatest finite-class F, so D intersect U is infinite. Minimality forces
U<=D, hence D=U join F. If U,V were distinct minimal infinite equivalences,
their meet would be finite-class; yet

    [U:U intersect V]=[U join V:V] <= [D:V]
                                  = [V join F:V] < infinite.

Finite classes and finite relative index would make U finite-class, a
contradiction. Thus there is exactly one minimal infinite direction.
Existence follows because the finite lattice contains an infinite Top.
This bridge uses full-lattice commutation/finite index, not distributivity
of a possibly nonexact packet interval.

Apply C19 to C18 and reuse real_closure C11 for
acl_(Q_i)(A)=F_i[A] for every finite real A. If the independent safe-palette
premise holds, C14 says all but at most one R_j projection of any unsafe
clique lie in a single class; quotienting makes that class one finite
R_j/C_j packet. These are the source C20 conclusions. Structural unary
closure is independent of the palette, even though the complete source
lemma also has that conditional clique clause.

## N06: Exact projections, finite kernels and rectangles — D06/D09, C21–C27/C33

For APJ C21 fix finite real A, actual exact E and x. Suppose the quotient
owner t=pi_E(x) has infinite orbit under FULL Aut(M/E over pi_E(A)). Choose
infinitely many distinct orbit images t_n. The finite owner map fixing
pi_E(A) and taking t to t_n is a partial isomorphism in the ACTUAL named
quotient language. If t belonged to pi_E(A), its orbit would be a singleton,
so no domain collision occurs. Join it to identity on the real A. Nested
mixed orbital saturation makes every mixed pair preserved. Joint exact
lifting yields an original automorphism fixing A and moving t to t_n.
Its images of x are in distinct E-classes, so the original orbit of x is
infinite. Contrapositive proves

    pi_E(acl_M(A)) subset acl_(M/E)(pi_E(A)).

This constructs finite lifts per request, not a globally surjective map
between full automorphism groups or a section.

For the reverse finite-family direction C22, suppose every coordinate
pi_i(x) is algebraic over pi_i(A). Original automorphisms fixing A act by
quotient automorphisms fixing pi_i(A), so the original x-orbit has only
finitely many possible images in each coordinate. There are only finitely
many joint tuples. The simultaneous map has kernel K=intersection_i K_i,
and each ACTUAL tuple fibre is one K-class, finite by hypothesis. Thus the
original orbit is finite. This needs no theorem that all formally compatible
tuples are realized, and no onto restriction to chosen finite factors.
Combining this with APJ gives C23, the source general closure pullback.
If an empty index family is admitted, finite-class common kernel Top forces
M finite and the same formula is immediate. An infinite common kernel
would invalidate the reverse direction: the Top quotient of an infinite
transitive source already gives the familiar failure.

For C24, W is the full intersection of inverse images of finite D_i. Its
image is a subset of the finite product of the D_i and each fibre is a
finite K-class. Therefore W is finite. This statement is pure finite-fibre
cardinality; marginal onto images or compatible tuple fullness are not
needed. If every D_i is absolutely closed and x is algebraic over W, APJ
sends each pi_i(x) into acl(pi_i(W)) subset acl(D_i)=D_i. Hence x is in W,
so W is absolutely closed (C25). The source assumes nonempty W, which we
retain; no common-owner closure hypothesis is involved.

For the rectangle C26, commuting exact CRT with C intersect K=Id identifies
D times_B P with the full inverse-image intersection in M. Both finite
factors are absolutely closed in their ambient quotient sources and project
onto B. C24/C25 make the actual original rectangle finite and absolutely
closed. B need not itself be acl-closed and need not be harmless in either
factor. The later safety construction uses a closed B to keep helper
closures over B; that is a different step, not a hidden rectangle premise.

C27 recalls the independent mixed orbital identity
C;a;K=(C join K);a;(C join K), already prepared in raw_exact. It is useful
for the spectrum argument; the shorter finite-preimage closure proof does
not need it. This separates the previous longer stabilizer proof from the
minimal closure API.

Finally for C33 use the old cores C_i with common meet Id and finite bottoms
F_i. C18/C19 and the existing unary closure provider do not use S. Substitute
these unary formulas in C23 to obtain

    acl_M(A) = intersection_i pi_(C_i)^(-1)(F_i[pi_(C_i)(A)])
             = intersection_i R_i[A].

This is the original closure-band identity. It is not claimed for arbitrary
quotient families whose common kernel has infinite real classes.

## N07: Harmless owner imaginaries in the FULL lattice — D07/D08, C28–C32

Assume M infinite and every owner kernel H_j contains ALL minimal infinite
invariant equivalences. Neither H_j nor F is assumed exact. Fix finite
real A and x outside acl(A). Set J_A=intersection_(a in A) Gamma(col(x,a));
for empty A use Top. If its class through x were finite, it would be a
finite A-definable set containing x, contradiction. Thus J_A has infinite
classes. Since the FULL invariant lattice is finite, choose ONE minimal
infinite E<=J_A. This SAME E lies below every H_j to be named (C28).

For each actual named owner class B_j of H_j choose an E-class inside it.
It is infinite. A colour p is bad when E intersect p squared is inside F.
Any two p-neighbours of x in that E-class then lie in one F-class; hence
that entire neighbour set is finite. There are finitely many old colours.
Choose b_j in the E-class outside their finite union AND outside {x}.
The second exclusion ensures p_j=col(x,b_j) is a diversity colour even
if x lies in that owner class. Its goodness makes
E intersect Gamma(p_j) an invariant equivalence not contained in F.
It is therefore infinite, and minimality gives E<=Gamma(p_j) (C29).

The infinite E-class of x now lies in every Gamma spoke class through the
real A and the representatives b_j. If x were algebraic over A and the
imaginary owner classes, it would be algebraic over A and the b_j, since
each owner is definable from its representative. The canonical real-hull
criterion would make that intersection finite, contradiction. For zero
owner names there is nothing to prove; for empty A with at least one named
owner the chosen b_j give a nonempty real parameter set for the hull
criterion. This proves harmlessness simultaneously for any finite collection
of such sorts (C30).

Conversely, if H omits a minimal infinite E, its proper intersection E
intersect H has finite classes. Choose x E u with x not F u. Then
E[u] intersect H[x] is a finite nonempty set definable over real u and the
one actual imaginary owner H[x]; it contains x. Hence x is algebraic over
those parameters. The existing singleton closure theorem gives acl(u)=F[u],
which excludes x. Naming that owner has enlarged real algebraicity (C31).
Together these prove the FULL invariant harmlessness criterion C32.

## N08: Internal finite coset-cover proof and action avoidance — D10/D11, C01–C03

We supply the generic group proof, rather than leaving Neumann's theorem
as a new unproved external assumption. For subgroups U,V define U preceq V
by [U:U intersect V] finite. Restriction of indices and the finite tower
formula show transitivity: if U preceq V preceq W, then
[U intersect V:U intersect V intersect W] <= [V:V intersect W], so
U intersect V intersect W has finite index in U and U preceq W. Mutual
preceq is commensurability. In a finite list choose a maximal equivalence
CLASS of this preorder, not a supposedly antisymmetric maximal subgroup
(C01). All finite index facts used here are elementary coset injections;
no invariant-equivalence or topological hypothesis is involved.

To prove C02, induct on the NUMBER OF DISTINCT SUBGROUP TYPES in a finite
left-coset cover. Suppose every listed subgroup has infinite index in G.
Choose a type H maximal for preceq. For every type K commensurable with H,
[K:K intersect H] is finite. Decompose each listed coset aK into finitely
many cosets a k_s(K intersect H), and replace them by their CONTAINING
cosets a k_s H. This may ENLARGE the cover, which is harmless: it still
covers G. After doing this for all commensurable types, there are finitely
many H-cosets and cosets of remaining K with H not preceq K. Indeed if
H preceq K, maximality would give K preceq H as well, so it would have been
in the removed class.

Because [G:H] is infinite, choose one left coset gH disjoint from the
finitely many listed H-cosets. Intersect the remaining cover with gH and
translate by g inverse. If gH intersect aK is nonempty, choose
x=g h0 in that intersection. Then gH=xH and aK=xK, so

    g^(-1)(gH intersect aK) = h0(H intersect K).

No conjugate subgroup appears. The resulting finite cover of H uses
subgroup types H intersect K, each of infinite index in H because
H not preceq K. There are strictly fewer distinct types than in the
original cover: the whole commensurable class containing H was removed,
and intersection cannot increase the count. This contradicts the induction
hypothesis applied to H. The zero-type case cannot cover a nonempty group;
this also gives the base of the induction. Thus no finite cover by cosets
of infinite-index subgroups exists. An independent review checked the
maximal-class, enlargement, intersection and induction-count steps.

For an action, each transporter T_(a,z)={g:g(a)=z} is empty or one LEFT
coset of Stab_G(a). Every a in the moving finite set A has infinite orbit,
so each stabilizer has infinite index. There are only finitely many such
transporter cosets for A times Z. C02 says they do not cover G. Choose one
g outside their union; it simultaneously takes every a away from every z.
If A or Z is empty use identity. This is exactly the source finite-action-
avoidance lemma C03, with no topology, faithfulness or model theory.

The earlier external-dependency audit already located a proved mathlib
coset-cover interface. A receiving project should reuse a compatible proved
library theorem when available in its pinned toolchain. The internal proof
here is a self-contained mathematical fallback, not an instruction to
reprove an existing library theorem or create 35 new public declarations.

## N09: Diagnostics, package and human-article return — C34

The control script checks exhaustive finite rook arguments, actual finite
canonical quotient pair orbitals and joint kernels, and cardinality-only
full-preimage intersections. Its Cartesian-phase toy checks complete fine-
lift pair tables including an opposite-coordinate diagonal, and distinguishes
existential safe projections from FULL-support safe palettes. A sparse
three-equivalence union is a countercontrol to an overweak clique premise.
These are finite interface diagnostics, not proofs of infinite source CCO,
model-theoretic acl, normalization, generic coset avoidance or EPPA.

This is one substantial foundational B022 package with internal milestones.

finite avoidance internally. B023 maximal cores and one-sided safety will
reuse its general results. No maximal-core, rooted or EPPA theorem is a
prerequisite here. Actual-source countability stays explicit; generic group
and full-invariant interval lemmas are independent of it.
