\begin{theorem}[Typed joint lifting for finitely many original exact quotients]
\label{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting}
Let $N$ be a countable homogeneous finite complete typed binary
structure satisfying the hypotheses of
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}.  For each original named
transitive sort $s$, choose finitely many invariant \emph{full exact}
equivalences $J$ on $N_s$, with $\Id_s$ included.
Form the many-sorted structure $N_{\mathcal J}$ whose sorts are
all the $N_s/J$ (identifying $N_s/\Id_s$ with the original real
sort $N_s$) and whose binary relations name \emph{all actual}
$\Aut(N)$-pair orbitals between the indicated sorts.
Then every finite partial isomorphism of $N_{\mathcal J}$ is
induced by an automorphism of the original $N$, acting on its
actual quotient classes.  No functorial choice of such lifts or
group-theoretic section is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix one finite partial isomorphism $f$ of the displayed joint
binary structure.  We show how to extend it by any original real
point $x\in N_u$ not yet in its domain.  Enumerate its finitely
many current parameters as $d_1,\ldots,d_n$, where
$d_i\in N_{t_i}/J_i$; original real parameters are the case
$J_i=\Id_{t_i}$.  For each $i$ choose an original representative
$c_i\in N_{t_i}$ of $d_i$, and let
$a_i:u\to t_i$ be the actual original atomic pair orbital of
$(x,c_i)$, allowing logical equality.  Put
$D_i=a_i;J_i\subseteq N_u\times N_{t_i}$.
This is a nonempty full exact represented relation by
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(ii), and is $J_i$-saturated
on the right.  Moreover $D_i$ is precisely the old inverse
image of the mixed $\Aut(N)$-orbital of $(x,d_i)$:
any such pair is transported by an original automorphism;
conversely if $(y,b)\in a_i;J_i$, some $c\,J_i\,b$ has
original pair orbital $a_i$ with $y$, and original pair
homogeneity moves $(x,c_i)$ to $(y,c)$, hence sends $d_i$
to $[b]_{J_i}$.  This also covers $J_i=\Id$ and true
identity $a_i=\Id_u$.

Choose arbitrary old representatives $b_i$ of the target
parameters $f(d_i)$.  The required images of $x$ are the
actual full-exact balls
$B_{D_i}(b_i)=\{y\in N_u:(y,b_i)\in D_i\}$.
For every pair $i,j$, preservation by $f$ of the COMPLETE
named $\Aut(N)$-orbital of $(d_i,d_j)$ gives some original
automorphism $g_{ij}$ sending these two source quotient
parameters simultaneously to $(f(d_i),f(d_j))$.  The point
$g_{ij}(x)$ satisfies both corresponding mixed orbital
requirements.  Since each $D_i$ is $J_i$-saturated, it lies
in $B_{D_i}(b_i)\cap B_{D_j}(b_j)$, independently of the
chosen representatives.  Thus these balls meet pairwise.
For $n=1$ each nonempty invariant relation is serial at its
old centre; for $n=0$ simply take a point of nonempty $N_u$.
In all cases the heterogeneous RAW exact-ball Helly statement
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(iii) supplies one
$y\in\bigcap_i B_{D_i}(b_i)$ (with the just mentioned
conventions for $n<2$).  The map $f\cup\{x\mapsto y\}$ is
a partial isomorphism of $N_{\mathcal J}$.  In particular,
if an original real $z$ of sort $u$ is already in the domain,
its distinct-vertex atomic pair orbital with $x$ forces
$y\ne f(z)$; no collision of old real points is introduced.

The same argument applies to inverse partial isomorphisms.
Alternate these extensions forward and backward through the
countably many original real points of every named sort.
The limiting map on the original real universe preserves and
reflects every original atomic pair orbital, and is a
sort-preserving bijection, hence an automorphism $g\in\Aut(N)$.
For an initially prescribed quotient parameter $d=[c]_J$,
the actual mixed pair orbital of $(c,d)$ records precisely
that $c$ belongs to $d$.  When the old real representative
$c$ is eventually added, preservation of this mixed orbit
forces $[g(c)]_J=f(d)$.  Therefore $g$ induces the full
original partial map $f$ on every original or quotient sort.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Equivariant unary enrichment preserves joint exact lifting]
\label{cor:typed-joint-exact-equivariant-unary}
In the setting of
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting}, add any
finite family of ACTUAL $\Aut(N)$-equivariant
unary functions between the already named
original real or exact quotient sorts.
The resulting many-sorted structure is
ultrahomogeneous: EVERY finite partial
isomorphism of its finite functionally
closed induced substructures extends to
an automorphism INDUCED BY ONE original
$g\in\Aut(N)$.

This applies to actually defined
old-real quotient projections, upward
maps of represented quotient classes,
and mixed-original-sort named quotient
transports whenever their two actual
quotient sorts occur in the chosen list.
It does NOT imply SAP for the resulting
hereditary unary-closed age, nor permit
naming arbitrary NONEXACT quotient sorts.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Forget all added unary functions in
the given finite partial isomorphism,
but retain EXACTLY the same finite
displayed vertices on each sort; no
unconstrained original real
representatives are added. This is
a finite partial isomorphism in
the FULL canonical binary orbital
reduct. By
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting},
some ONE $g\in\Aut(N)$ extends the
displayed map simultaneously on ALL
actual original/quotient sorts.
Every added unary function $\tau$
is equivariant, so
$g(\tau(q))=\tau(g(q))$
for every actual quotient vertex q.
Thus the SAME ambient g preserves
the complete expanded language.
Its restriction extends the original
finite functionally closed map.
No section or coherent choice of g
for different partial isomorphisms
is claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Joint homogeneity of all exact quotient sorts]
\label{thm:joint-exact}
Every finite binary partial isomorphism of $M_{\mathcal E}$ is induced
by an automorphism of $M$. This statement concerns arbitrary finite
collections of real and exact imaginary elements \emph{simultaneously}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting} with the single original
sort $M$ and the chosen finite family $\mathcal E$ of its actual
invariant exact equivalences (including $\Id$). The mixed
pair-orbital language is exactly that of $M_{\mathcal E}$ by its
definition. The general typed theorem gives the simultaneous ambient
lift, without an additional SAP assumption.

For reference, the former one-sort pairwise-ball calculation is the
following identity for the original symmetric RAW orbitals $a_i,a_j$
and invariant exact equivalences $J_i,J_j$, where $R_i=a_i;J_i$:
\[
 J_i;a_i;a_j;J_j
       =(a_i;J_i);(a_j;J_j)
       =R_i;R_j=R_i^\smile;R_j.
 \tag{JE-PB}\label{eq:joint-exact-pairwise-balls}
\]
This uses original one-sort symmetry and RAW commutativity; the typed
proof of \cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting} instead obtains the needed
pairwise intersections by direct old automorphism transport and makes
no corresponding commutativity assumption for mixed sorts.
\end{proof}

By Lemma~\ref{lem:nested-mixed} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:joint-exact}, compatible finite real and
exact-owner requests (including owner actions on a whole finite owner
set) admit simultaneous ambient lifts.

\begin{theorem}[Triangle fullness, AP and CCO of exact sorts]
\label{thm:exact-sorts-source}
The many-sorted age $\Age(M_{\mathcal E})$ is described by its legal
typed binary triangles, has AP, and satisfies semantic homomorphic
CCO. In particular every individual actual exact quotient, in its
named binary orbital language, inherits the standing source
properties (with any relevant transitivity checked in that sort).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For triangle fullness, inductively realize a complete legal typed
diagram. Suppose classes $d_i\in M/J_i$ with real representatives
$y_i$ already realize its first $m-1$ vertices, and the next vertex
has sort $M/K$ with prescribed pullbacks $R_i=Ka_iJ_i$.
Legality of each triangle supplies an actual triple of quotient
classes with the required three pair orbitals.  Its real
representatives witness that the pair orbital of $(d_i,d_j)$
meets $R_iR_j$.  This product is $J_i$-saturated on its left
and $J_j$-saturated on its right (indeed
$R_iR_j=J_i a_i K a_j J_j$).  Since a typed pair orbital has
full inverse image $J_i c_{ij}J_j$, the \emph{whole} pair orbital
of $(d_i,d_j)$ is contained in $R_iR_j$, independently of the
representatives $y_i,y_j$ chosen for the previously realized
classes. The exact balls $R_i[y_i]$ intersect pairwise and
Lemma~\ref{lem:exact-balls} supplies $y$; then $y/K$ realizes the
new typed element. Named same-sort diversity prevents unwanted
identification. Joint homogeneity, Theorem~\ref{thm:joint-exact},
relocates the embedding to fix a prescribed induced subdiagram;
this also supplies AP.

For typed semantic CCO, first present the pullback of each
specified typed edge orbital as an intersection of represented
word profiles, and replace each edge by private word paths for
those profiles.  Paths from distinct edges share only their marked
endpoints; duplicate length-one edges of the same colour are
merged.  Identity factors in words are deleted.  Since a
specified typed edge between distinct elements has pullback not
contained in $\Id$, its presentation does not include an empty
word; equality is handled as logical equality, not as a new edge.

Every simple cycle of this raw path diagram traverses whole
private paths, because their internal vertices have degree two.
If it visits exactly two marked vertices, it uses two paths
replacing the same typed edge.  If it visits at least three, the
sequence of marked vertices projects to a specified simple cycle
of the original typed diagram.  In the first case the typed
orbital is nonempty; in the second it is homomorphically
realizable by hypothesis.  In either case the complete raw cycle
is homomorphically realizable by
Lemma~\ref{lem:exact-cycle-lift}.  No independent, coherent choice
of representatives around a cycle is needed: $JaK$-saturation
makes \emph{every} choice of representatives work.

Raw semantic homomorphic CCO therefore completes the expanded
partial diagram.  Each word path then forces the pair of its
marked endpoints into the corresponding profile; intersecting
them recovers exactly the specified typed orbital.  Projecting
the marked images to their quotient sorts consequently yields a
typed homomorphism of the original partial diagram.  This proves
semantic CCO for the actual many-sorted age, without assuming
SAP or pre-existing typed CCO.
\end{proof}
