\section{Maximal exact pseudocomplements and normalised MI factors}
\label{sec:acf-maximal-cores}

This section replaces repeated normalisations of the nonmaximal
packet cores $C_i=\kappa(R_i)$ by one intrinsic exact-lattice
construction.  We \emph{do not redefine} those earlier cores.
Work in the original infinite finite-exact-normalised source
and let $E_1,\ldots,E_r$ be all minimal infinite invariant
relations and $R_i=R_{E_i}$ their A32 correspondences.
Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hat-cores}
 \widehat C_i=\bigvee\{H:\ H\text{ is an invariant exact
 equivalence and }H\subseteq R_i\}.
\end{equation}
These are exact equivalences, possibly strictly larger
than the older $C_i=\kappa(R_i)$.  The old cores retain
that notation throughout this manuscript.

\begin{lemma}[Exact pseudocomplement]
\label{lem:acf-hat-pseudocomplement}
For invariant exact $H$ one has
\[
         H\subseteq R_i
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad H\cap E_i=\Id.
\]
The $\widehat C_i$ are the exact-lattice pseudocomplements of
the distinct atoms $E_i$.  They contain $E_j$ for $j\ne i$,
are disjoint from $E_i$, and satisfy $\bigcap_i\widehat C_i=\Id$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $H\subseteq R_i$, its exact meet with $E_i$ lies in
$E_i\cap R_i\subseteq F$.  Normalisation forbids a nontrivial
finite-class exact equivalence, so this meet is $\Id$.
Conversely, if $H\cap E_i=\Id$, then for every actual
atomic colour $a\subseteq H$ its represented square satisfies
$a^2\subseteq H$, whence $E_i\cap a^2=\Id$; thus $a\subseteq R_i$.
This proves both directions and maximality in~\eqref{eq:hat-cores}.
For $i\ne j$, the earlier correspondence concentration
lemma gives $E_j\subseteq R_i$.  Since $E_j$ is exact,
$E_j\subseteq\widehat C_i$.  Finally $\bigcap_i\widehat C_i$
is exact and lies in $\bigcap_iR_i=F$, so is $\Id$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[No new finite exact kernels]
\label{lem:acf-hat-normalised}
For each nonempty $I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,r\}$ put
$K_I=\bigcap_{i\in I}\widehat C_i$.
Every actual quotient $M/K_I$ has no nonidentity finite-class
exact equivalence.  Its minimal infinite directions are exactly
$(E_i\vee K_I)/K_I$, $i\in I$.
In particular for $r\ge2$ each
$Q_i=M/\widehat C_i$ is a strict, normalised source with
one minimal infinite direction, finite bottom
$F_i=R_i/\widehat C_i$, and
\[
       \operatorname{acl}_{Q_i}(A)=F_i[A].
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If an exact quotient equivalence $H/K_I$ were a nonidentity
finite-class relation, then $H>K_I$ would be exact with
$[H:K_I]<\infty$.  For each $i\in I$ we have
$E_i\cap K_I=\Id$.  Each class of the exact meet
$E_i\cap H$ therefore injects into the finite set of
$K_I$-classes contained in its $H$-class.  Hence this exact
meet has finite classes and must equal $\Id$.
The pseudocomplement lemma yields
$H\subseteq\widehat C_i$ for every $i\in I$, so
$H\subseteq K_I$, a contradiction.

For the directions, by the finite distributive-lattice
representation of Corollary~\ref{lem:acf-exact-distributive},
each $E_i$ is an atom of the exact lattice, and every
nonidentity exact equivalence contains one of these atoms.
In $\operatorname{Down}(P)$ let $p_i$ be its minimal point
representing $E_i$.  Then $\widehat C_i$ corresponds to
$P\setminus\uparrow p_i$, so the upper interval above $K_I$
is the downset lattice of $\bigcup_{i\in I}\uparrow p_i$.
Its distinct minimal points are exactly the $p_i$, $i\in I$.
Thus the minimal nontrivial exact quotients are the displayed
ones.  By the A32 minimal-cut theorem and the just-proved
absence of finite exact quotients, they are precisely the
minimal infinite invariant equivalences of $M/K_I$.

For \(I=\{i\}\) this gives the
one-direction quotient.
Recall from the original packet-core and
global correspondence results that
\[
      C_i=\kappa(R_i),\qquad
      [R_i:C_i]<\infty,\qquad
      \Sigma(C_i)=R_i .
\]
Since
\(C_i\le\widehat C_i\le R_i\),
both relative indices
\([R_i:\widehat C_i]\) and
\([\widehat C_i:C_i]\)
are finite. We claim that
\(\Sigma(\widehat C_i)=R_i\).
The inclusion \(R_i\le\Sigma(\widehat C_i)\)
holds because \(R_i/\widehat C_i\)
is finite-class. Conversely if
\(H\ge\widehat C_i\) is ANY invariant
equivalence with finite
\([H:\widehat C_i]\), the nested
index identity gives
\[
 [H:C_i]=[H:\widehat C_i]
                 [\widehat C_i:C_i]
                     <\infty .
\]
Thus \(H\le\Sigma(C_i)=R_i\),
proving \(\Sigma(\widehat C_i)=R_i\).
Quotienting by \(\widehat C_i\)
therefore makes
\[
                  F_i=R_i/\widehat C_i
\]
EXACTLY the greatest finite-class
equivalence of \(Q_i\), even if
\(\widehat C_i>C_i\).
The one-direction real-hull formula
\cref{lem:consolidated-unary-closure}
now gives \(\operatorname{acl}_{Q_i}(A)
=F_i[A]\).
This step uses genuine finite relative
indices, NOT an unproved inclusion of
an arbitrary nonexact packet interval
in the exact lattice.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Projection of algebraicity through an exact quotient]
\label{lem:acf-acl-projects-exact}
Let \(Q\) be any standing countable
homogeneous transitive finite complete
symmetric binary AP+homomorphic-CCO
source, let \(E\) be one of its ACTUAL
invariant FULL EXACT equivalences,
and write \(\pi:Q\to T=Q/E\).
For every finite real \(A\subseteq Q\),
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \pi\bigl(\operatorname{acl}_Q(A)\bigr)
       \subseteq
       \operatorname{acl}_{T}(\pi A).
 \qquad}
\tag{APJ}\label{eq:acf-acl-projects-exact}
\]
The reverse inclusion after taking
full preimages is NOT claimed when
\(E\) has infinite classes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
It suffices to prove the
contrapositive for an actual
real point \(x\in Q\).
Suppose its quotient owner
\(t=\pi(x)\) has INFINITE orbit
under \(\Aut(T/\pi A)\).
Choose infinitely many distinct
orbit images \(t_n\) of \(t\).
For each \(n\), consider
the finite JOINT real/\(E\)-quotient
sort partial map fixing EACH
real point of \(A\), fixing
EACH named quotient class
in \(\pi A\), and sending
the one additional quotient
class \(t\) to \(t_n\).
Inside the real sort
nothing moves. Inside the
quotient sort this is a
partial isomorphism because
\(t,t_n\) belong to the
same orbit over \(\pi A\).
For every mixed pair
\((a,t)\) with \(a\in A\),
the mixed orbital is
determined entirely by
the pair orbital
\((\pi(a),t)\), by
\cref{lem:nested-mixed}.
That orbital is unchanged.
Thus the displayed map is
a finite partial isomorphism
of the ACTUAL joint exact
sort structure. By
\cref{thm:joint-exact}
it extends to an automorphism
of \(Q\) fixing \(A\)
pointwise and moving \(t\)
to \(t_n\).
The corresponding images
of \(x\) belong to
infinitely many distinct
\(E\)-classes, so its
\(\Aut(Q/A)\)-orbit is
infinite. Therefore
\(x\notin\operatorname{acl}_Q(A)\).
This proves \eqref{eq:acf-acl-projects-exact}
without asserting any quotient
automorphism section.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Finite intersection of absolutely closed exact quotient preimages]
\label{lem:acf-finite-exact-closed-preimages}
Let $M$ be a standing homogeneous
finite-complete symmetric binary
source satisfying original semantic
homomorphic CCO. Let
$E_1,\ldots,E_m$ be ACTUAL
invariant FULL EXACT
equivalences on $M$ whose
common meet
$K=\bigcap_{i=1}^m E_i$
has FINITE original real
classes. For each i let
$D_i\subseteq M/E_i$ be
a finite induced ABSOLUTELY
real-algebraically-closed
set. Then any NONEMPTY
intersection of FULL
original inverse images
\[
          W=\bigcap_{i=1}^m
                  \pi_{E_i}^{-1}(D_i)
\tag{FPCL}\label{eq:acf-finite-exact-closed-preimages}
\]
is FINITE and
$\operatorname{acl}_M(W)=W$.

No pairwise-compatible owner
tuple assumption, surjectivity
$\pi_{E_i}(W)=D_i$, or
algebraic closure of a
common owner quotient is
needed for this assertion.
The finite-class hypothesis
on the common kernel is
essential for FINITENESS.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The simultaneous quotient
map
\[
     \pi=(\pi_{E_1},\ldots,
               \pi_{E_m}):
               M\longrightarrow
                  \prod_i M/E_i
\]
has fibres precisely the
actual $K$-classes, which
are finite by hypothesis.
Since $\pi(W)\subseteq
\prod_iD_i$ is finite,
$W$ is a finite union of
finite $K$-classes.

Now fix
$x\in\operatorname{acl}_M(W)$.
For EVERY i, apply the
original algebraicity
projection theorem
\cref{lem:acf-acl-projects-exact}
to the ACTUAL full exact
quotient $\pi_{E_i}$ and
the finite original REAL
parameter base W:
\[
  \pi_{E_i}(x)\in
  \operatorname{acl}_{M/E_i}
             (\pi_{E_i}(W))
 \subseteq
  \operatorname{acl}_{M/E_i}(D_i)
              =D_i .
\]
Consequently x lies in
EVERY inverse image in
\eqref{eq:acf-finite-exact-closed-preimages},
so $x\in W$. The reverse
inclusion $W\subseteq
\operatorname{acl}_M(W)$
is tautological. This
proves absolute closure.

The argument uses NO
automorphism section from
individual quotient groups
to $\Aut(M)$: only the
already established finite
parameter algebraicity
projection implication
for each ACTUAL exact
quotient is invoked.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Absolute acl closure of a full exact rectangle]
\label{lem:acf-rectangle-acl}
Let \(C,K\) be original full exact invariant
equivalences of one homogeneous transitive
symmetric binary source, with \(C\cap K=\Id\),
and set \(J=C\vee K\), \(Y=M/C\),
\(X=M/K\), \(T=M/J\). Let \(B\subseteq T\)
be any finite, induced, NONEMPTY owner set;
it need NOT be absolutely
$\operatorname{acl}_T$-closed for THIS
rectangle-closure assertion.
Suppose \(D\subseteq Y\) and \(P\subseteq X\)
are finite, induced, ABSOLUTELY real-acl-closed
in their respective ambient quotient sources,
and each projects ONTO precisely \(B\).
Then their FULL rectangle
\[
       R=D\times_B P
       =\{(d,p):d\in D,\ p\in P,\
                     \pi_T(d)=\pi_T(p)\}
\]
is a finite induced, absolutely
real-acl-closed subset of the ACTUAL
original \(M\), via the exact CRT
bijection \(M\cong Y\times_T X\).
No harmlessness assumption on B
in EITHER quotient factor is needed
for this particular closure assertion.
The stronger closure assumption
$\operatorname{acl}_T(B)=B$
appearing later in the
safe-quotient DESCENT proof
is used THERE to control the
projections of auxiliary
helper closures; it is not
part of the present lemma.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The ACTUAL exact CRT
for commuting invariant
equivalences with
$C\cap K=\Id$ identifies
the FULL rectangle
with exactly
\[
       R=\pi_C^{-1}(D)
                     \cap\pi_K^{-1}(P).
\]
Since the common kernel
$C\cap K=\Id$ has
singleton original real
classes, apply the
independent
\cref{lem:acf-finite-exact-closed-preimages}
with $(E_1,E_2)=(C,K)$
and $(D_1,D_2)=(D,P)$.
It follows immediately
that R is finite and
$\operatorname{acl}_M(R)=R$.

For reference, the ORIGINAL
same-sort mixed-orbital
identity underlying the
older, longer stabilizer
proof remains valid:
for every actual old
fine atomic colour $a$,
\[
       C;a;K
             =a;(C\vee K)
             =J;a;J.
\tag{RAC}\label{eq:acf-rectangle-mixed-orbit}
\]
This follows from
commutation of original
invariant symmetric RAW
relations and $C;K=C\vee K$.
It is also the independent
mixed-colour interface
for the full-rectangle
safe-spectrum argument;
it is NOT needed to
repeat a three-case
algebraic-orbit proof here.
\end{proof}
