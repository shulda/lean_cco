\begin{lemma}[Finite avoidance while fixing harmless quotient owners]
\label{lem:acf-harmless-owner-avoidance}
Let $Y$ be a homogeneous
finite-complete ORIGINAL
exact quotient source, let
$T$ be one ACTUAL further
invariant exact quotient,
and let $B\subseteq T$
be a finite set of ACTUAL
quotient classes whose
simultaneous naming does
NOT enlarge real algebraic
closure in Y.
Let $A\subseteq Y$
be finite and ABSOLUTELY
acl-closed, and let
$L\supseteq A$ be a finite
induced extension in Y.
For every finite
$Z\subseteq Y\setminus A$,
there is ONE
\[
             g\in\Aut(Y/A,B)
\]
such that
\[
                    g(L\setminus A)
                                  \cap Z=\varnothing.
\tag{OAV}\label{eq:acf-harmless-owner-avoidance}
\]
In particular, if every
vertex of L projects into B,
its ENTIRE owner assignment
is retained pointwise
under g. Two extensions
$A\subseteq L,R$ may be
placed sequentially over
A with exactly their
original owner names,
with their off-base parts
pairwise disjoint and
disjoint from any
specified finite helper
outside A. No input
closure of L or R is
needed for this finite
avoidance lemma, although
the safe quotient descent
will subsequently close
the helper factors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each $x\in L\setminus A$,
the absolute acl-closure
of A gives
$x\notin\operatorname{acl}_Y(A)$.
The harmless-owner
hypothesis yields
$x\notin
 \operatorname{acl}^{\rm eq}_Y(A,B)$.
By the finite-orbit
characterisation of
model-theoretic algebraic
closure in this countable
homogeneous structure,
each $x$ has INFINITE
orbit under
$G=\Aut(Y/A,B)$.
The finite-action
avoidance lemma
\cref{lem:finite-action-avoidance}
applied to the finite
moving set $L\setminus A$
and finite forbidden set
Z gives ONE $g\in G$
moving ALL its points
outside Z simultaneously.
Every such g fixes A
pointwise and fixes EACH
named owner class in B
as an imaginary parameter,
so its restriction to
L is the required
literal owner-preserving
fine-colour embedding.

For two extensions first
apply the assertion to L
avoiding the finite
helper outside A;
then apply it to R
avoiding both the helper
and the chosen image of
L outside A. Since each
placement fixes A and
all owner names, the
resulting old factor
images meet exactly
in A, and have the same
original owner tags.
No assumption of a
canonical owner section
or SAP over an UNclosed
owner base has entered.
\end{proof}
