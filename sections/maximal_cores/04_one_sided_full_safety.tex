\begin{lemma}[Full-support safety under a harmless exact split]
\label{lem:acf-quotient-safety}
Suppose exact $C,K$ satisfy $C\cap K=\Id$ and put
$J=C\vee K$, $Y=M/C$, $X=M/K$, $T=M/J$.
Suppose naming finite actual $T$-owner sets does not enlarge
\emph{real algebraic closure in $Y$}.  If the original palette
$S$ strongly amalgamates finite real-acl-closed spans, then
\[
 S_Y=\{q\in\mathcal D(Y):
       \text{every original fine lift of }q\text{ lies in }S\}
\]
strongly amalgamates finite real-acl-closed spans in $Y$,
with every distinct projected unsafe original pair remaining unsafe.
\emph{No harmlessness assumption in the opposite factor $X$ is
required for this conclusion.} Symmetrically, harmlessness only in
$X$ suffices for $S_X$. In particular if both owner cuts are
harmless, both full-support palettes are strongly safe.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We give the \(Y\)-argument, without assuming
owner harmlessness in the complementary
\(X\)-factor. Start with an arbitrary
span \(A\to L,R\) of FINITE ABSOLUTELY
acl-closed induced structures in \(Y\),
and realise its common base \(A\)
literally in the homogeneous \(Y\).
Choose a finite NONEMPTY absolutely
\(\operatorname{acl}_T\)-closed owner set
\(B\subseteq T\) containing the \(T\)-owner
images of \(L\) and \(R\).
For example, take the algebraic closure
of those finitely many owner images
and, if both factors are EMPTY, add
one arbitrarily chosen actual $T$-owner
before closing. Such a point exists
because the original source and every
actual quotient sort are NONEMPTY.
This set is finite by oligomorphy.
The extra arbitrary owner in the
empty-span case is only a finite
COMMON helper parameter, not an
added vertex of the original span.

In \(X\) construct an actual finite
ABSOLUTELY acl-closed
\(P\subseteq X\) projecting onto
EXACTLY \(B\), whose COMPLETE atomic
pair table contains every realised
\(X\)-orbital over EACH ORDERED pair
of actual owners \(t,t'\in B\).
There are only finitely many named
\(X\)-orbitals and finitely many
ordered pairs of owners. For each
incidence \(b\) realised over
\((t,t')\), choose finite actual
witnesses \(p_b,p_b'\); in particular
include an \(X\)-diagonal witness
for each \(t=t'\) and at least one
point over every \(t\in B\).
Let \(P\) be the real algebraic
closure in \(X\) of their union.
It remains finite by oligomorphy.
Its owner image is precisely \(B\):
the witnesses already project
onto every \(t\in B\), and
\cref{lem:acf-acl-projects-exact}
applied to the ACTUAL exact
quotient \(X\to T\) says that
the owner of ANY newly algebraic
\(X\)-point lies in
\(\operatorname{acl}_T(B)=B\).
No owner harmlessness in \(X\)
or global section of
\(\Aut(T)\) into \(\Aut(X)\)
was used.

Next construct the COMMON \(Y\)-helper.
Choose finitely many actual \(Y\)-points
over all owners in \(B\), adjoin them
to \(A\), and take their absolute
real algebraic closure \(D\).
Its owner projection is again
precisely \(B\), by
\cref{lem:acf-acl-projects-exact}
for \(Y\to T\) and because
the chosen starting set already
represents every owner in \(B\).
It is finite, absolutely
acl-closed and contains \(A\).
Apply the direct finite
owner-anchored avoidance
\cref{lem:acf-harmless-owner-avoidance}
to the two extensions
$A\subseteq L,R$,
first avoiding $D\setminus A$
for L, then avoiding
$(D\setminus A)\cup
(L\setminus A)$ for R,
all inside the ACTUAL
homogeneous Y.
This uses precisely
harmlessness of the
finite named owner set B
and original absolute
acl-closure of A.
It gives copies with
their literal original
owner labels preserved
and with intersections
$L\cap D=R\cap D=L\cap R=A$.
The two factors have
not yet been assumed
to be closed over
the newly chosen helper
D; their absolute
closures are taken next.

Take absolute real acl after
adjoining \(D\) to each such
factor, obtaining acl-closed
extensions \(\widehat L,\widehat R\)
of \(D\), containing the copies
of \(L,R\) respectively. Apply
the ordinary closed strong
amalgamation obtained by
finite-coset avoidance over
the ABSOLUTELY closed common
base \(D\): replace one entire
\(\widehat R\) by an isomorphic
copy over \(D\), disjoint from
\(\widehat L\) outside \(D\).
Because \(D\) contains a real
point representing EVERY owner
of \(B\), an automorphism
fixing \(D\) fixes \(B\)
pointwise; the copy retains
all original owner labels.
The original \(L,R\) factors
therefore have intersection
EXACTLY \(A\), and the two
larger closed factors have
intersection EXACTLY \(D\).
Every one of \(D,\widehat L,
\widehat R\) is a finite
absolute acl-closed structure
projecting onto precisely \(B\).
This is the needed owner-complete
padding; no independent quotient
representatives will be chosen
after the subsequent \(S\)-amalgamation.

The rectangular structures
\(D\times_B P\), \(\widehat L\times_B P\)
and \(\widehat R\times_B P\)
are finite ACTUAL induced substructures
of \(M\), by the full exact CRT.
They are absolutely acl-closed by
\cref{lem:acf-finite-exact-closed-preimages,%
lem:acf-rectangle-acl}:
each of \(D,\widehat L,\widehat R\)
is ABSOLUTELY acl-closed in \(Y\)
and \(P\) is ABSOLUTELY acl-closed
in \(X\); the actual complementary
exact kernels have MEET equality.
All factors project ONTO the same
finite owner set \(B\), so their
full CRT rectangles are genuinely
defined. The fact that \(B\) is
itself acl-closed was needed
EARLIER to keep the algebraic
closures of the selected helper
points over precisely B; it is
NOT required by rectangle-closure
once the two coordinate factors
are already absolutely closed.
This closure argument uses
\emph{joint finite exact-sort lifts},
not an unproved global section
on automorphism groups or an
unverified coordinatewise acl formula.
Strongly $S$-amalgamate the latter two over the former.
We verify that the opposite $X$-coordinates remain
\emph{fixed across the two factor embeddings}, rather than
assuming independent quotient representatives can be matched
after amalgamation.  For each $t\in B$ choose
$d_t\in D$ of owner $t$ (the helper $D$ meets every owner).
For every $p\in P$ of owner $t$, the base rectangle contains
$(d_t,p)$.  In the left rectangle each $(\ell,p)$ of the
same owner is $K$-equivalent to $(d_t,p)$; the same is true
of $(r,p)$ in the right rectangle.  These $K$-relations
are preserved by the two embeddings into the strong
amalgam.  Thus the two sides use the \emph{same actual
$X$-class} indexed by $p$, witnessed by their common
base point $(d_t,p)$.  Every cross pair
$(\ell,p),(r,p')$ consequently has the $X$-pair type
of the original $p,p'\in P$; the amalgam cannot
silently alter or independently reidentify this
opposite-coordinate type.

Their projected $Y$ copies form a strong amalgam:
if off-base quotient points from opposite factors
coincided, pairing each with the same $P$-point would
identify two distinct original real points, contrary to
strongness; $C\cap K=\Id$ ensures that equal coordinate
pairs are equal real points.

Fix an arbitrary NEW cross \(Y\)-edge
\(yy'\) of the projected strong amalgam
and let \(q=\col_Y(y,y')\).
The two endpoints have actual \(T\)-owners
\(t,t'\in B\), even when \(t=t'\).
The complete complementary-owner-pair
spectrum lemma
\cref{lem:acf-complementary-spectrum}
says that varying \((p,p')\in P^2\)
with those EXACT owners realizes
EVERY old fine atomic colour \(a\)
over \(q\) among the old pairs
\[
                    (y,p),(y',p')
\]
in the FULL old rectangular factors.
Their two points lie outside the
common old rectangle on OPPOSITE
sides, so the strong \(S\)-amalgamation
assigns \(a\in S\) to each such
realized pair. Consequently ALL
fine lifts of \(q\) lie in \(S\),
hence \(q\in S_Y\).
This is the full-matrix condition,
not a weaker assertion that some
representatives give one safe
old colour. Restricting the
projected amalgam to the original
\(Y\)-factor images gives the
required strong \(S_Y\)-amalgam.  If a distinct projected unsafe
original pair had an $S_Y$ colour, every fine lift, including
its actual fine colour, would lie in $S$, contradiction.
The corresponding argument with $X$ as the recipient is obtained
by exchanging $C,K$, and only then requires owner harmlessness
in $X$.
\end{proof}
