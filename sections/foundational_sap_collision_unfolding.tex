\section{Collision unfolding under strong amalgamation}
\label{sec:foundational-collision-unfolding}

\begin{lemma}[Collision unfolding]\label{lem:collision-unfolding}
Let $A$ be a finite PARTIAL relational structure, and let
$h:A\to U$ be a homomorphism-embedding into a countable homogeneous
relational structure $U$ whose age has SAP. Suppose $h$ is
injective on every member of a fixed finite family of
distinguished COMPLETE induced substructures of $A$.
Then $h$ can be replaced by an INJECTIVE homomorphism-embedding
$h':A\to U$ preserving the complete induced isomorphism type
of the image of EACH distinguished substructure.

No unary-functional closure, CCO, exactness or original
binary-language assumption is used; the statement concerns
the full displayed relational supports of the partial source.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $h$ is not injective, choose $x\ne y$ with $h(x)=h(y)=v$,
and let $B$ be the finite COMPLETE induced substructure of $U$
on $h(A)$. Every irreducible induced source substructure
contains AT MOST ONE point of the fibre $h^{-1}(v)$, since
$h$ embeds each such substructure. In particular, every
distinguished complete factor contains at most one such
source point by the stated injectivity assumption.

Strongly amalgamate TWO copies of $B$ over the complete
induced $B\setminus\{v\}$ and embed their strong amalgam
into $U$. By homogeneity move the FIRST copy to the
literal old finite $B$. Its other copy of $v$ is a new
point $v'\ne v$, of the same sort and with exactly the
same complete atomic diagram to $B\setminus\{v\}$ as $v$.

Send a chosen nonempty proper subset of $h^{-1}(v)$
containing $x$ to $v'$ and keep its complement at $v$.
All other source images remain their old points.
For EACH irreducible source substructure $D$, its
old image contains at most one source vertex over $v$,
so the new image of $D$ is either unchanged or the
corresponding induced copy with $v$ replaced by $v'$.
Consequently the modified map is still a
homomorphism-embedding. The same reasoning preserves
the full induced type on each distinguished complete
factor, including ALL its higher-arity displayed tuples.

The number of colliding unordered pairs strictly
decreases: collisions of members of this one old
fibre are separated, and $v'$ was not previously in
the image of $A$. Repeating finitely many times yields
the required injective map. No choices of representative
colour outside the indicated completed factors are
required, and SAP (not mere AP) guarantees $v'\ne v$.
\end{proof}
