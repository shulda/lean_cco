\section{Simultaneous exact quotients of original named sorts}
\label{sec:typed-simultaneous-exact}

We now allow a finite family $S$ of \emph{original named transitive}
real sorts, rather than the one original sort used in
\cref{sec:exact-crt}.  On each original sort the binary orbitals
are symmetric; between different sorts they are paired by typed
converse, and are not assumed self-converse.  This section uses the
\emph{full represented identity-inclusive} typed algebra of
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}, not the strong diversity calculus.
It does \emph{not} require SAP of the original source, and exact
quotients formed here need not have SAP themselves.

\noindent The abstract typed invariant quotient-transport and return
holonomy tools are now stated in the preceding foundational module,
\cref{sec:foundational-typed-return-holonomy}. The present section
adds the genuinely FULL RAW exact quotient-block calculus.

For every $s\in S$, choose an invariant \emph{full exact}
equivalence $E_s$ on the original sort $M_s$.  Put
\[
  Q_s=M_s/E_s,\qquad Q=\bigsqcup_{s\in S}Q_s,
\]
and name \emph{all actual} binary orbitals of the induced
$\Aut(M)$ action, with quotient equality treated as logical
identity.  This is a finite complete typed binary language.  In
contrast to the one-original-sort construction
$M_{\mathcal E}$, different $E_s$ act on genuinely distinct
original sorts, and no functorial section of the full $\Aut(Q)$ action back
to $\Aut(M)$ is asserted.  Individual finite partial quotient
maps are separately handled by the common joint lifting theorem
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting}.

\begin{lemma}[Full exact orbital blocks and compositions]
\label{lem:typed-simultaneous-blocks}
For every original atom $a:s\to t$, including logical equality
when $s=t$, put
\[
 \langle a\rangle_{st}=E_s;\{a\};E_t.
 \tag{TQ1}\label{eq:typed-exact-block}
\]
These nonempty full exact blocks partition the original typed
atom palettes and are precisely the inverse images of the actual
named pair orbitals on $Q_s\times Q_t$.  The inverse image of
$\Id_{Q_s}$ is exactly $E_s$; every quotient diversity orbital
on $Q_s^2$ pulls back to a block disjoint from $E_s$.

For composable unions of quotient atomic orbitals $R:s\to t$
and $T:t\to u$, write $\widetilde R,\widetilde T$ for their
inverse images on original real points. Then
\[
 \widetilde{R;_Q T}
 =\widetilde R;E_t;\widetilde T
 =\widetilde R;\widetilde T.
 \tag{TQ2}\label{eq:typed-exact-quotient-product}
\]
Typed converse and intersections commute with inverse image.
Every nonempty full exact quotient menu pulls back to an original
full exact menu, and every quotient atomic word profile has an
original full exact inverse image.  The quotient full atomic
word-profile families are $2$-Helly.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each block in \eqref{eq:typed-exact-block} is exact by
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(ii).  It is saturated under
$E_s$ and $E_t$.  If an old atom $b$ occurs in the block of $a$,
there are actual representatives of a $b$-edge related by
$E_s,E_t$ to the endpoints of an $a$-edge.  Reversing those
equivalence moves shows $a\in E_s;\{b\};E_t$.  Idempotence
of $E_s,E_t$ then gives equality of the two blocks.  Thus blocks either coincide or are disjoint.
To identify them with ACTUAL \(\Aut(M)\)-orbitals
on quotient pairs, suppose
\((X,Y)\in Q_s\times Q_t\) belongs to the
block \(E_s;\{a\};E_t\). By definition
it admits representatives \(x\in X,y\in Y\)
with old atomic pair type \(a\).
Indeed, if arbitrary representatives have
some atom \(b\subseteq E_s;\{a\};E_t\),
the witnessed \(E_s\)- and \(E_t\)-moves
supply an actual \(a\)-pair in the SAME
two quotient classes. Two quotient pairs
belonging to this block therefore each
admit an \(a\)-pair of representatives.
Old homogeneity aligns those two actual
complete ordered atomic two-point diagrams
(and the identity pair when \(a=\Id\));
its automorphism maps their respective
quotient classes onto each other.
Conversely old automorphisms preserve
every saturated block. Hence these blocks
are PRECISELY the named pair orbitals of
the induced old action, without assuming
an automorphism-lifting section of the
possibly larger full quotient group.
In particular quotient equality pulls back
to \(E_s\), and a same-sort quotient
diversity block cannot meet \(E_s\).

For the quotient composition \(R;_Q T\),
consider fixed old endpoint representatives
\(x\in M_s,z\in M_u\).
If \(([x]_{E_s},[z]_{E_u})\in R;_Q T\),
there is an intermediate ACTUAL \(E_t\)-class
\(Y\) with the two prescribed quotient orbital
incidences. These incidences are witnessed by
POSSIBLY DIFFERENT old points \(y_1,y_2\in Y\):
\[
    x\widetilde R y_1,\qquad
    y_1 E_t y_2,\qquad
    y_2\widetilde T z.
\]
Thus \((x,z)\in\widetilde R;E_t;\widetilde T\).
Conversely any such old three-step walk projects
to one intermediate class \(Y\), giving the
quotient relational product. This establishes
the FIRST equality in
\eqref{eq:typed-exact-quotient-product}.
Now \(\widetilde R;E_t=\widetilde R\)
and \(E_t;\widetilde T=\widetilde T\)
because these are inverse images of quotient
relations, saturated at their endpoint classes.
Therefore
\(\widetilde R;E_t;\widetilde T
=\widetilde R;\widetilde T\),
which establishes the SECOND equality.
No commutativity of differently typed arrows,
SAP, or selection of coherent representatives
for several quotient pairs was used.  Quotient words therefore lift to
products of full exact blocks and hence to full exact menus;
finite nonempty meets lift as meets.  Pairwise intersections of
quotient word menus lift to pairwise intersections of full exact
old menus, which have a common old atom by
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(i).  Its quotient block lies in
every quotient word menu, proving $2$-Helly.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact descent in the symmetric one-original-sort case]
\label{lem:one-original-sort-exact-descent}
Suppose the initial source has exactly \emph{one
original transitive sort}, with all old binary
orbitals symmetric. Let $E$ be an invariant
full exact equivalence and let $D$ be a
nonempty old full exact invariant relation
which is \emph{$E$-bisaturated}:
\[
                         E;D;E=D.
\]
Then its image $D/E$ in the actual quotient
$M/E$ is a nonempty full exact relation
of the quotient represented algebra.
In particular, if $E\le K$ are original
full exact equivalences, $K/E$ is a full
exact quotient equivalence.

This converse-to-pullback assertion uses
\emph{one original symmetric sort}.
It is not asserted for an arbitrary full
exact cross-sort relation under mutually
independent quotients of multiple original
sorts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an original exact presentation
$D=\bigcap_{\alpha=1}^n P_\alpha$ by
nonempty represented atomic word profiles.
Since $D$ is nonempty, repeated exact
finite-meet distributivity
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(ii)
gives
\[
 D=E;D;E=\bigcap_\alpha(E;P_\alpha;E).
 \tag{TQ5}\label{eq:one-sort-exact-descent}
\]
(When $n=0$, the relation is old top,
and its quotient is top as well.)
Write $P_\alpha=a_1;\cdots;a_m$ as
a word of actual old orbitals, with
$m=0$ denoting the equality word.
Every old invariant relation is symmetric
because every orbital is symmetric.
Consequently its represented product
with any other invariant relation
commutes, and $E^2=E$. Hence
\[
 E;P_\alpha;E=
 (E;a_1;E);\cdots;(E;a_m;E),
\]
with the empty word interpreted as
$E$. Each $E;a_i;E$ is the inverse image
of one actual quotient atomic orbital
(including quotient equality if $a_i\subseteq E$).
By the saturated quotient product identity
\eqref{eq:typed-exact-quotient-product}, the
relation $E;P_\alpha;E$ is the inverse image
of the corresponding quotient \emph{word
profile}. Its quotient image is therefore
word-reachable. Intersecting these quotient
words gives exactly $D/E$, since quotient
inverse image commutes with arbitrary
intersections of saturated relations.
Thus $D/E$ is full exact.

For $D=K\ge E$ the bisaturation
$E;K;E=K$ is automatic, and its image
is an equivalence by ordinary quotient
semantics. This proves the final assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why independent mixed-sort word descent needs care]
\label{rem:mixed-word-projection-failure}
The proof of
\cref{lem:one-original-sort-exact-descent}
cannot be repeated with independent kernels on
different original sorts simply by projecting
every factor of an old word. This failure occurs
even in a homogeneous symmetric SAP+CCO example.

Take three named sorts $A,B,C$, each consisting of
two infinite pure clone fibres indexed by
$p\in\mathbb F_2$. For any two distinct old
vertices, name their original ordered sort pair
and the bit $p_x+p_y$ (with typed converse).
This source is homogeneous, SAP and homomorphic
CCO: finite partial constraints are XOR
equations whose inconsistency is witnessed by
a nonzero labelled simple cycle; infinite clone
fibres make every consistent system injectively
realizable.

The old mixed word $w=(0:A\to B,\ 0:B\to C)$
has endpoint profile
\[
 P_w=\{(a,c):p_a+p_c=0\}.
\]
Now quotient the \emph{entire} original sort $B$
by its top equivalence, keeping equality on
$A$ and $C$. Each projected edge relation
$0:A\to B/\Top$ and
$0:B/\Top\to C$ is total, so their
quotient word product is the \emph{full}
$A\times C$ relation, strictly larger than
the image of $P_w$. The intervening
$E_B$-movement is the difference.

In this particular example $P_w$ still
descends as a named \emph{atomic} $A$--$C$
quotient relation. Thus this is a
counterexample to \emph{factorwise word
descent}, not a claimed counterexample
to every conceivable multi-sort exact
descent statement. The safe general
direction remains quotient exact $\Rightarrow$
original exact pullback.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Private word-path cycle decomposition]
\label{lem:typed-private-path-cycle}
Let \(G\) be a finite simple partially coloured
typed graph, with diversity atoms on its specified
unordered edges. Replace each specified edge
\(uv\) by finitely many formal typed paths between
its marked endpoints, all internal vertices private
to their chosen paths, deleting genuine equality
factors by contracting them within each path.
Assume that no resulting path has length zero,
and that coincident length-one paths between
one marked pair have the same old atomic colour
and are merged.

Then every specified simple cycle of the
resulting raw graph either uses two different
private paths above ONE edge of \(G\), or its
successive marked vertices form a simple
specified cycle of \(G\) and it traverses one
private path above each edge of that cycle.
A single length-one path is counted as one
old edge, not as a parallel edge or a 2-cycle.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An internal vertex of a private path has
specified degree two and is not contained
in any other private path. A simple cycle
which enters a private path at an internal
vertex must therefore traverse the whole
path until its other marked endpoint.
Likewise, a simple cycle consisting solely
of internal vertices is impossible, because
the private paths are individually simple
and have two distinct marked endpoints.
Consequently any simple cycle is a concatenation
of complete private paths, and the marked
vertices it visits are pairwise distinct
except for its final return to the first.

If it visits exactly two marked vertices,
both traversed private paths have those
endpoints. Since \(G\) has at most one
specified edge on any unordered pair, they
belong to the SAME specified edge of \(G\).
If it visits at least three marked vertices,
the consecutive pairs are distinct specified
edges of \(G\), and the sequence gives
a simple specified cycle there.
The length-one merge excludes artificial
parallel edges and 2-cycles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Simultaneous full exact quotients preserve AP and CCO]
\label{thm:typed-simultaneous-exact-quotients}
Let $M$ be a countable homogeneous complete finite typed binary
structure with a finite family of nonempty original transitive
named sorts, symmetric same-sort orbitals, typed converse-paired
cross-sort orbitals, and semantic \emph{homomorphic} simple-cycle
CCO.  No SAP hypothesis is imposed.  For \emph{arbitrary and
independent} full exact invariant equivalences $E_s$ on the
original named sorts, the actual simultaneous quotient $Q$ above
is homogeneous, has a triangle-defined age with AP and JEP,
and satisfies semantic homomorphic simple-cycle CCO.

Neither global lifting $\Aut(Q)\to\Aut(M)$ nor SAP of $Q$ is a
conclusion; this theorem is not a formalisation of a coherent
choice of fine representatives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\emph{Anchored triangle extension and homogeneity.}
Consider any finite complete quotient diagram already realized
by pairwise distinct elements $C_1,\ldots,C_n$ of $Q$, and
prescribe one additional quotient vertex of sort $u$ whose
binary orbital $R_j$ to each $C_j$ makes every displayed
triangle a realized quotient triangle.  (For $n=0$ choose any element of the
requested nonempty quotient sort. For $n=1$,
transitivity of the \emph{original} named
sort action makes each requested nonempty
quotient pair orbital incident to the
prescribed existing quotient centre; if
the new sort equals its sort, a quotient
diversity orbital supplies a different class.)  Choose arbitrary old real
representatives $b_j\in C_j$, and let $D_j=\widetilde R_j$
be the corresponding old nonempty full exact block from
\cref{lem:typed-simultaneous-blocks}.

For any two distinct quotient centres $C_j,C_k$, the proposed
triangle occurs in $Q$, hence an actual pair of quotient
centres with the same named orbital has a common new quotient
neighbour of the required $R_j,R_k$ types.  Since the pair
orbital is an \emph{actual $\Aut(M)$ orbit}, some old
automorphism moves that centre pair to $(C_j,C_k)$.  Choose a
real representative $z$ of the transported third quotient
class.  Endpoint saturation makes $(z,b_j)\in D_j$ and
$(z,b_k)\in D_k$ for the \emph{arbitrarily fixed} real
representatives $b_j,b_k$.  Thus the two actual exact balls
$B_{D_j}(b_j),B_{D_k}(b_k)$ meet.  By the heterogeneous exact
ball Helly theorem \cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(iii),
all these balls have a common real point $z\in M_u$.
Its $E_u$-class has precisely the prescribed pair orbitals to
all $C_j$.  If $C_j$ has sort $u$, the requested orbital of a
new \emph{distinct} quotient vertex is diversity, so
$D_j\cap E_u=\varnothing$; therefore $[z]_{E_u}\ne C_j$.
No quotient capacity or accidental collision is assumed away.

This establishes a one-point extension over the \emph{actual}
finite quotient base, not merely over some isomorphic copy.
Induction realizes every finite complete typed atomic diagram
whose triangles are all realized in $Q$, proving triangle
fullness.  Homogeneity also follows directly from
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting}, applied to the exact
quotients $E_s$ together with each original $\Id_s$.
Here a finite partial isomorphism of the PURE
quotient structure is regarded as a partial
map of the LARGER joint structure on the
EXACT SAME displayed quotient vertices
ONLY: no arbitrary original real
representatives are added to its domain,
and no old fine pair colours between
such arbitrary representatives are
presupposed. Every pair relation on those
displayed quotient vertices is literally
the same ACTUAL original-$\Aut(M)$ quotient
orbital in both signatures. Therefore
the map is a finite partial isomorphism
of the joint structure, to which the
joint lifting theorem applies. Its lift
is an individual $g\in\Aut(M)$ and
induces the requested original quotient
partial isomorphism. In particular the
quotient age has AP and JEP.  Neither this finite local
lifting nor the one-point extension constructs a group section.

\emph{Homomorphic CCO, without an imported FullWord criterion.}
Take any finite partially specified quotient diversity diagram
whose every specified simple quotient cycle has a homomorphism
into $Q$.  For each specified quotient pair orbital $R$, its
old pullback $D=\widetilde R$ is a nonempty full exact
intersection of represented atomic word profiles, by
\cref{lem:typed-simultaneous-blocks}.  Replace the specified
edge by private old paths spelling every word in one such
presentation.  Delete logical equality factors inside the
words.  No word can reduce entirely to old logical equality,
because the pullback of a quotient \emph{diversity} orbital is
disjoint from old logical equality.  Repeated length-one paths
with the same endpoints impose the same old atom and are merged.

By \cref{lem:typed-private-path-cycle}, each specified
simple cycle of the resulting original typed partial diagram
traverses either two private paths belonging to ONE quotient
edge, or private paths projecting to a \emph{simple} specified
quotient cycle. In the former case choose an arbitrary actual
realization of that nonempty quotient pair orbital; in the
latter choose the assumed quotient homomorphism of the
\emph{entire indicated} simple quotient cycle. In both
cases these choices provide one compatible quotient class
for every marked formal vertex of the raw cycle; there is
no need for a globally coherent choice of quotient images
across DIFFERENT raw cycles.
For each marked original variable choose an arbitrary old real
representative of its quotient image.  Because every pullback
block $E_s;\{a\};E_t$ is saturated, \emph{every} such pair of
representatives satisfies each defining old word profile.
Realize its private word paths independently.  This gives a
homomorphism of the old simple cycle into $M$, even if the
marked representatives of different quotient variables
coincide.  Thus every specified old simple cycle is
homomorphically completable.

Semantic CCO of the original $M$ gives a homomorphism of the
\emph{entire} expanded old partial diagram into $M$.  Each
specified quotient edge's marked endpoint images satisfy
all its defining full word profiles and hence belong to its
exact pullback block.  Projecting marked vertices to their
$E_s$-classes gives the required quotient homomorphic
completion.  This proves quotient semantic CCO without SAP,
without any quotient representative-lifting section, and
without importing an independent typed FullWord$\Rightarrow$CCO
theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Typed full-exact quotient towers]
\label{cor:typed-exact-quotient-towers}
Suppose a finite sequence of homogeneous typed sources is
obtained by simultaneously quotienting each current original
named sort by arbitrary full exact invariant equivalences of
its \emph{current} equality-inclusive represented algebra.
If the initial source satisfies the hypotheses of
\cref{thm:typed-simultaneous-exact-quotients}, so does every
stage.  Furthermore, the composite of any finite number of
these quotient stages is canonically isomorphic as a complete
atomic binary structure to a single simultaneous quotient of
the initial source by full exact invariant equivalences
containing the earlier chosen kernels.  If a stage quotients
some original sort by an equivalence properly above logical
identity, the total number of typed diversity atomic orbitals
(counting converse pairs consistently) strictly decreases.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Each quotient stage is homogeneous, has named transitive
sorts with same-sort symmetric atom orbitals and paired typed
converses, and retains homomorphic CCO by the theorem.
Therefore its hypotheses hold again at the next stage,
without any SAP assumption on the quotient.

For the direct-composite assertion, pull a current quotient
full exact equivalence back through the previous quotient
map.  By \eqref{eq:typed-exact-quotient-product}, every
quotient atomic word profile pulls back to an original full
exact menu; so a finite exact meet and in particular an
exact equivalence pull back to a full exact invariant
original relation containing the previous kernel.  For an
original atom $a:s\to t$, double quotient saturation of its
first block produces the block
$H_s;\{a\};H_t$, where $H_s,H_t$ are the total pulled-back
kernels.  This is precisely the old atomic orbital block of
the direct quotient, including the equality orbital.
Hence both quotient structures have identical underlying
sets of classes and identical named binary atomic orbitals.
No map on automorphism groups is constructed.  Every quotient
atom is one nonempty union of prior original atom blocks, so
distinct old diversity orbitals can only merge, never split.
A nonidentity quotient kernel contains at least one nonidentity
old same-sort atom, which becomes quotient equality; therefore
the typed diversity rank strictly decreases.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Goursat constraints on entire finite named quotient sorts}

The following basic lemma does \emph{not} need CCO, exactness,
SAP, symmetry or transitivity. Its strength derives from the
fact that the finite blocks are \emph{entire named sorts},
not unnamed finite fibres over moving quotient owners.

\begin{lemma}[Strong finite projection of entire named sorts]
\label{lem:typed-finite-entire-axis-projection}
Let $X$ be a homogeneous relational structure of arity at most
two, with a collection of pairwise disjoint \emph{named} finite
sorts $(P_i)_{i\in I}$. Other named sorts may be infinite.
Put $\Gamma=\Aut(X)$,
$R_i=\Aut(X\restriction P_i)$, and for $i\ne j$ let
\[
 P_{ij}=\operatorname{im}\bigl(
   \Gamma\longrightarrow R_i\times R_j\bigr).
\]
Then for every finite $S\subseteq I$,
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \operatorname{im}\!\left(
 \Gamma\longrightarrow\prod_{i\in S}R_i\right)
 =\bigl\{(g_i)_{i\in S}:
               (g_i,g_j)\in P_{ij}\quad(i\ne j)\bigr\}.
 \quad}\tag{TQ3}\label{eq:typed-finite-strong-projection}
\]
In particular $\Gamma\to R_i$ is surjective and each
$P_{ij}\le R_i\times R_j$ is a \emph{subdirect} subgroup.
This is a statement about the \emph{full automorphism group of
$X$}, not about the image of an earlier finer source action.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every automorphism of the induced finite $P_i$ extends to
$X$ by homogeneity, so the coordinate images are all $R_i$.
The left-to-right inclusion of \eqref{eq:typed-finite-strong-projection}
is immediate. Conversely choose a tuple on the right.
The union $g=\bigcup_{i\in S}g_i$ is a bijection of
$P_S=\bigcup_{i\in S}P_i$, a \emph{finite} induced
substructure. It preserves each named unary sort and all
within-sort binary relations because $g_i\in R_i$.
For any two different sorts $i,j\in S$, the
condition $(g_i,g_j)\in P_{ij}$ supplies an actual
$\gamma_{ij}\in\Gamma$ agreeing with $g$ on \emph{all}
of $P_i\cup P_j$. Thus $g$ preserves every cross-sort
binary atom. Since the language has arity at most two,
there are no additional relation instances to check.
Therefore $g$ is a finite partial automorphism of $X$.
Homogeneity extends it to some $\gamma\in\Gamma$,
giving the required tuple. This also covers $S=\varnothing$
and singleton $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite linear Goursat code and flat transport]
\label{cor:typed-finite-linear-goursat}
In \cref{lem:typed-finite-entire-axis-projection}, suppose
each $R_i$ is, as an abstract group, an elementary
abelian $2$-group $V_i=\mathbb F_2^{d_i}$.
For each $i<j$ there exist finite vector spaces
$W_{ij}$ and surjective linear maps
$\pi_{ij}:V_i\to W_{ij}$ and
$\rho_{ij}:V_j\to W_{ij}$ such that
\[
 P_{ij}=\{(x,y):\pi_{ij}(x)=\rho_{ij}(y)\}.
\]
On any finite $S$ form the linear parity-check map
\[
 \Phi_S:\bigoplus_{i\in S}V_i
 \longrightarrow\bigoplus_{\{i,j\}\in[S]^2}W_{ij},
 \quad
 (\Phi_S(x))_{ij}=\pi_{ij}(x_i)+\rho_{ij}(x_j).
\]
The image of the actual full group $\Gamma$ on these
entire sorts equals $\ker\Phi_S$; its cardinality is
\[
 2^{\,\sum_{i\in S}d_i-\operatorname{rank}(\Phi_S)}.
 \tag{TQ4}\label{eq:typed-finite-goursat-rank}
\]
More generally, if on three distinct entire finite
named sorts the pair groups are graphs of isomorphisms
$\theta_{ij}:R_i\to R_j$, these satisfy
$\theta_{ik}=\theta_{jk}\circ\theta_{ij}$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Since each $P_{ij}$ is subdirect, its subgroup kernels
$N_i=\{x:(x,0)\in P_{ij}\}$ and
$N_j=\{y:(0,y)\in P_{ij}\}$ are vector subspaces.
The condition $(x,y)\in P_{ij}$ identifies exactly
the two quotient coordinates in
$V_i/N_i\cong V_j/N_j$; this is the elementary
abelian Goursat presentation. The strong finite
projection equation \eqref{eq:typed-finite-strong-projection}
is now exactly the system $\Phi_S(x)=0$.
Rank--nullity gives \eqref{eq:typed-finite-goursat-rank}.
For graph couplings, choose any $x\in R_i$, extend
it by surjectivity of the full $\Gamma$ action to
one global $\gamma$, and read its three coordinates.
They simultaneously satisfy
$\gamma_k=\theta_{ik}(x)$ and
$\gamma_k=\theta_{jk}(\theta_{ij}(x))$.
This holds for every $x$, proving flatness.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite entire axes after full exact quotient]
\label{cor:typed-exact-finite-axis-goursat}
In \cref{thm:typed-simultaneous-exact-quotients}, take
any selection of ORIGINAL named sorts whose entire
quotient sets $Q_s=M_s/E_s$ are finite. Their full
canonical binary automorphism group $\Aut(Q)$ satisfies
\eqref{eq:typed-finite-strong-projection}, and whenever
its coordinate actions are elementary abelian it
satisfies \eqref{eq:typed-finite-goursat-rank}.
No original SAP is required.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The theorem makes the \emph{actual binary quotient $Q$}
homogeneous, in a finite arity-two named-sorted language.
Apply the preceding two statements to its entire finite
sorts. No assertion is made about the image of
$\Aut(M)$ inside this possibly larger full $\Aut(Q)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Sharp kernel and lifting firewall]
\label{rem:typed-finite-goursat-kernel-scope}
The three preceding statements concern \emph{entire finite
named quotient sorts}. They cannot be applied to the
\emph{pointwise owner kernel} acting on finite fibres above
\emph{unnamed, moving} quotient owners. Naming all those
owners as new constants can destroy homogeneity, so
\cref{lem:typed-finite-entire-axis-projection} need not
hold in the expanded language. The narrower one-original-sort
finite-exact-fibre extension theorem is a genuine
additional structural input, not a formal consequence here.

A small two-original-sort phase control already disproves
the naive stronger intrinsic-binding assertion even with SAP
and CCO. Let two original sorts $U,V$ carry two $\mathbb F_2$
phase classes with infinite pure clones, and name their
cross XOR-difference. Their fine two-point phase quotient
has full automorphism group the \emph{diagonal} $C_2$
translation. Coarsen the $U$ quotient to a singleton but
keep the $V$ phase quotient. The pointwise kernel of the
map of these two fine/coarse quotient automorphism groups
is trivial, although the intrinsic within-$U$ fibre
automorphism group is $C_2$. Both the fine and coarse cuts
are full exact. This does \emph{not} contradict strong
projection for entire named sorts: the latter describes
the \emph{full fine automorphism group} by its actual
cross-sort pair orbitals, not its pointwise kernel.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Scope: joint quotients versus global lifting]
\label{rem:typed-simultaneous-exact-scope}
The common theorem
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting} now supplies
individual finite partial-isomorphism lifting for several
exact quotients of each of several ORIGINAL named sorts.
The historical one-original-sort \cref{thm:joint-exact}
is its immediate special case.  The present theorem additionally
proves full triangle-defined AP and \emph{homomorphic CCO} of
the PURE quotient reduct, which does not follow merely from
mixed joint lifting.  Neither theorem constructs a section
of automorphism groups or a functorial choice of fine
representatives.  Cross-sort orbitals are not assumed
commutative and the original real source need not have SAP.
\end{remark}
