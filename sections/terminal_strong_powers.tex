\section{Terminal strong orbital powers without CCO}
\label{sec:foundational-terminal-strong-powers}

\begin{lemma}[Typed same-sort pure-power saturation without CCO]
\label{lem:typed-terminal-pure-power}
Let $a$ be one named transitive sort of an actual
homogeneous finite complete typed symmetric source
with visible SAP. Assume the FULL original invariant
equivalence lattice on $a$ has no relative-index-two
interval; semantic CCO is NOT assumed. For every old
same-sort diversity atom $t$, let $F_t$ be the diversity
palette of connectivity in its undirected orbital graph.
Then there is $N_t$ such that
\[
                \{t\}^{\starprod n}=F_t
                      \qquad (n\ge N_t).
\tag{TPP}\label{eq:typed-terminal-pure-power}
\]
The left side is the STRONG diversity word profile and
the right side omits literal equality.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every vertex has a $t$-neighbour by transitivity and
nonemptiness of the named atomic orbital. Transitivity
also permutes the connected components of the $t$-graph
transitively. If one component were bipartite, all
would be; its even-walk relation would be an invariant
equivalence of relative index two inside connectivity.
The even-walk equivalence is an ACTUAL original
invariant equivalence and has relative index EXACTLY
two inside the connectivity equivalence, contradicting
the stated FULL original no-index-two hypothesis.
Thus every connected component is nonbipartite.

For each diversity atom $c\subseteq F_t$, choose one
realised $c$-pair $(x_c,y_c)$. Connectedness and
nonbipartiteness give a $t$-walk from $x_c$ to $y_c$
of every sufficiently large length: use an odd closed
walk to change parity and two-edge backtracks to
increase length by two. Since there are only finitely
many relevant old atoms, take a common threshold $N_t$.
Old pair homogeneity transports these walks to all
$c$-pairs. Finally typed SAP injectivisation
\cref{lem:typed-injectivisation}, pinning the two
\emph{distinct} endpoints, converts a represented
walk with a possibly repeated internal vertex into
an injectively realised positive typed path.
Hence every $c\subseteq F_t$ lies in the strong
profile $\{t\}^{\starprod n}$ for $n\ge N_t$.
The reverse inclusion follows because a $t$-path
never leaves its connectivity class. Literal equality
is absent from both diversity palettes.
\end{proof}
