\section{Actual exact fibre products and quotient descent}
For an exact invariant equivalence $E$ the quotient projection is
$\pi_E:M\to M/E$. If $E,J$ are two such equivalences, their meet
$K=E\cap J$ and join $Q=E\vee J=EJ$ are exact: intersection is part of
the definition and product closure follows from
Lemma~\ref{lem:exact-product}. The map
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fibreproduct}
 M/K\longrightarrow (M/E)\times_{M/Q}(M/J),\qquad
 [x]_K\longmapsto([x]_E,[x]_J)
\end{equation}
is a bijection. Injectivity is immediate. Surjectivity says that
an $E$-class and a $J$-class contained in the same $Q$-class
intersect, which holds by $Q=EJ$. The fine colour of a pair
is recovered by the formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:colour-meet}
 Ka=Ea\cap Ja
\end{equation}
for every original atomic support $a$, including equality.
This is the two-coordinate case of
\cref{lem:exact-quotient-colour-meet}, using $EaE=Ea$ and similarly
for $J,K$.
The typed quotient structure therefore remembers all fine pair
orbits of $M/K$.

\begin{lemma}[Exactness descends through exact quotients]
\label{lem:exact-descent}
Suppose $J$ is an invariant exact equivalence and $Q\ge J$ is
an invariant $J$-saturated exact relation (in particular an exact
equivalence). Then its induced relation $Q/J$ on $M/J$ is exact in
the actual quotient orbital language. Conversely the pullback of
every quotient word profile is a represented exact relation on $M$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $Q=\bigcap_iP_i$ with represented word profiles $P_i$.
As $Q=JQJ$, distributivity of exact products over nonempty exact
intersections (Lemma~\ref{lem:exact-product}) gives
\[
 Q=J\left(\bigcap_iP_i\right)J
  =\bigcap_iJP_iJ.
\]
For an original word $P_i=a_1\cdots a_k$, the relation
$JP_iJ=Ja_1J\cdots Ja_kJ$ is the pullback of the actual word
of quotient atomic orbitals $\overline{a_1}\cdots\overline{a_k}$;
the intervening $J$ factors can be inserted because $J^2=J$
and all represented invariant products commute. Hence each
$JP_iJ/J$ is a quotient word profile, and their intersection is
precisely $Q/J$. The converse follows by pulling back each named
quotient orbital $JaJ$, taking represented products and finite
intersections. Identity and $\Top$ obey the same conventions.
\end{proof}


\begin{lemma}[Typed exact descent under intertwining kernels]
\label{lem:typed-intertwining-exact-descent}
Let $N$ be a typed original source as in
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}, and choose for every
original named sort $s$ a full RAW exact invariant
equivalence $E_s$.  Let $D:s\to t$ be a nonempty FULL
exact original relation such that $E_s;D;E_t=D$.
Suppose $D$ has a finite word presentation
$D=\bigcap_{\alpha=1}^m P_{w_\alpha}$, allowing $m=0$
(the typed top), and that every original atomic factor
$a:u\to v$ occurring in these words satisfies
\[
                       E_u;a=a;E_v.
 \tag{TI}\label{eq:typed-intertwining}
\]
Then the induced relation $D/(E_s,E_t)$ on the actual
quotient sorts $N_s/E_s$, $N_t/E_t$ is FULL exact in
their complete binary orbital language.  Conversely,
the old inverse image of every quotient FULL exact
relation is original FULL exact without assuming (TI).
This is a sufficient typed descent criterion, not an
assertion of unrestricted factorwise mixed-sort descent.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $w=a_1;\cdots;a_k$ with intermediate original
sorts $s=s_0,\ldots,s_k=t$.  The inverse image of
the corresponding actual quotient-orbital word is,
by the actual orbit-block calculation of
\cref{lem:typed-simultaneous-blocks},
\[
 E_{s_0};a_1;E_{s_1};a_2;\cdots;a_k;E_{s_k}.
\]
By (TI), $a_i;E_{s_i}=E_{s_{i-1}};a_i$.
Associativity and $E_{s_i}^2=E_{s_i}$ remove each
intermediate equivalence factor, so this expression is
exactly $E_s;P_w;E_t$.  The identity-only empty word
pulls back to $E_s$, and the empty list of word
restrictions presents the quotient top.

Using the ACTUAL full exact convolution and nonempty
meet distributivity of \cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(ii),
\[
 D=E_s;\Bigl(\bigcap_\alpha P_{w_\alpha}\Bigr);E_t
       =\bigcap_\alpha(E_s;P_{w_\alpha};E_t).
\]
Every relation on the right is endpoint-saturated and
is the pullback of ONE quotient word by the previous
paragraph.  Inverse image preserves arbitrary
intersections of such saturated relations, hence
$D/(E_s,E_t)$ is an intersection of actual quotient
words and is FULL exact.  For $m=0$, both sides are
top, by the serial/top absorption law.

Conversely, each ACTUAL quotient atomic pair orbital
pulls back to a block $E_u;a;E_v$ for some original
atomic $a:u\to v$; quotient identity pulls back to
$E_u$.  Each block is nonempty FULL exact by original
word exactness and exact convolution.  The old pullback
of any quotient word is a product of such blocks,
therefore FULL exact, with genuine intermediate
$E$-factors retained.  The inverse image of a
nonempty finite meet of quotient words is their
nonempty full exact intersection.  No mixed-sort
commutation was used in this converse direction.
\end{proof}
