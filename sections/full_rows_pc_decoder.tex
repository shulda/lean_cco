\section{Full-row valuations and invariant path-consistency decoding}
\label{sec:full-rows}

We retain the \emph{actual} finite owner set $B$, the language group
$\Gamma\le\Aut(B)$ containing the induced diagonal thin-owner action,
the finite elementary abelian $2$-group $G$, the
full-owner primitive typed source $U_B$, its strong exact menus
$\mathcal H_{st}$ and the rectangular, pure-$\Gamma$-covariant
readings $h^g_{st}$ from
Proposition~\ref{prop:moving-readings}. The decoder below uses only
the properties exported there: conservativity, survivor heredity,
converse compatibility, pure-owner covariance, switching, and legal
triangles for independently chosen readings on a PC triple.
Any family satisfying this same contract can therefore be used,
including the later orbit-safe readings. By
Theorem~\ref{thm:completable-reservoir}, fix a \emph{single}
finite structural $\Gamma$-EPPA reservoir $B_0$ for a finite
nonempty complete full-$G$-packet source $A\subseteq N$ and for all
the required compatible owner-action requests.  The partial reservoir
has an injective completion in $N$, but its holes remain unrecorded.
No equivariance is required of this chosen completion.

Let $I$ be the finite set of \emph{structural} $G$-packets of $B_0$.
Choose an origin in each such packet and write
\[
 B_0=\{(i,z):i\in I,\ z\in G\},\qquad
 \operatorname{own}(i,z)=z\beta_i,
 \qquad t_g(i,z)=(i,z+g).
\]
Here $\beta_i\in B$ is the actual $E$-owner of the chosen origin.
The action of $G$ on $B$ need not be free; in particular the
stabiliser $H=G\cap E$ is allowed to be nontrivial.  Nevertheless
$G$ acts freely on every \emph{real} packet.  Write
$\mathcal R\subseteq\binom I2$ for the recorded structural pairs.
For $\{i,j\}\in\mathcal R$, all $|G|^2$ fine colours are recorded.
A structural automorphism $s$ with owner-language component
$\gamma\in\Gamma$ therefore has the unique coordinate form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-structural}
 s(i,z)=(\sigma i,z+b_i),\qquad
 \beta_{\sigma i}=b_i\gamma\beta_i,
 \quad \sigma\in\operatorname{Sym}(I),\quad b_i\in G.
\end{equation}
The $b_i$ are determined by the \emph{real} image of the origin,
not merely by the displayed owner equation; the latter does not
uniquely determine them when $H\ne 1$.
All coordinates in this section are additive, and $2g=0$ for $g\in G$.

\subsection{Full-row universe, lifts and output}
\label{subsec:R07-W}

For each $i\in I$ and every function $f:I\to G$, take a fresh
\emph{actual} full packet.  Its vertices are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-W}
 W=\{(i,f,z): i\in I,\ f\in G^I,\ z\in G\},\qquad
 t_g(i,f,z)=(i,f,z+g),\quad
 \operatorname{own}(i,f,z)=z\beta_i.
\end{equation}
Thus the actual packet indices are $p=(i,f)$; the index $i$ by
itself is not an actual packet of $W$.  In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-size}
 |W|=|I|\,|G|^{|I|+1}.
\end{equation}
Define a partial, owner-typed diagram $W^\#$ by putting the
actual thin colours within each packet $p=(i,f)$ and, for every
recorded pair $\{i,j\}\in\mathcal R$ with $i\ne j$, copying the
entire recorded fine matrix of $(i,j)$ between \emph{each} packet
over $i$ and \emph{each} packet over $j$ via the structural projection
\[
 q:W^\#\longrightarrow B_0,\qquad q(i,f,z)=(i,z).
\]
There is no record between distinct actual packets over the same
$i$, nor between packets over an unrecorded pair $\{i,j\}$.
Embed the original source $A$ in the \emph{zero rows} $f=0$ of its
structural indices.  Since $A$ is complete, its distinct packet
indices are recorded in $B_0$.

A correction matrix is any $C=(C_{ij})\in G^{I\times I}$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-correction}
 C_{ij}+C_{ji}=0\qquad
 \text{for every unrecorded }\{i,j\}\subseteq I,\ i\ne j.
\end{equation}
There is \emph{no} correction constraint on recorded pairs and
none on $C_{ii}$.  Given a structural automorphism~\eqref{eq:R07-structural},
set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-lift}
 \alpha_C(i,f,z)=(\sigma i,f',z+b_i),\qquad
 f'(\sigma j)=f(j)+C_{ij}+b_i\quad(j\in I).
\end{equation}

The construction below defines the PC domains $D_{xy}$ and the complete
colouring $d$ in \eqref{eq:R07-decoding} on this fixed universe $W$.
We state its output before proving the completion, row-compatibility and
naturality properties used in the construction.

\begin{theorem}[Relative owner $\Gamma$-EPPA]
\label{thm:R07-relative-eppa}
The colouring~\eqref{eq:R07-decoding}, together with its
original full-packet structure, is a finite member of the
actual owner-typed age of $N$, containing $A$ as an induced
complete full-packet substructure. For \emph{every} structural
$\Gamma$-automorphism $s$ of $B_0$ as in
\eqref{eq:R07-structural} and \emph{every} matrix $C$ satisfying
\eqref{eq:R07-correction}, the map $\alpha_C$ is a
$\Gamma$-automorphism of this \emph{same} completed structure,
with the owner-language component of $s$. It need not preserve
the zero-row source setwise.

For a prescribed source-extension request, choose $s$ extending
its structural map and $C$ satisfying its zero-row conditions.
Then $\alpha_C$ extends that request. Consequently every finite
family of compatible relative requests over the fixed owner set
$B$, including empty-real-domain requests, has one common finite
$\Gamma$-EPPA witness.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Quantifiers, size, and scope of the argument]
\label{rem:R07-scope}
The finite construction uses one $B_0$, one full-row set $W$,
one injective completion certificate for $W^\#$, one greatest
strong-PC fixed family and one decoder.  Only afterwards are
individual correction matrices chosen.  The size is exactly
$|I|\,|G|^{|I|+1}$ and no uniform size bound across varying
finite quotient witnesses is claimed.  We have not assumed
$H=1$, equivariant packet origins, coherent EPPA, equality of PC
menus with full solution spectra, or that HKN faithfulness alone
gives a completable reservoir.  All the statements here depend
on the preceding in-manuscript relative closure, full-owner
transversal, typed gate and reading proofs, together with
published HKN and the CCO-specific RAW-HULL route using Erd\H{o}s.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Completion certificate and compatible row requests}

\begin{lemma}[Actual completion of every full-row clone]
\label{lem:R07-W-completable}
The finite partial diagram $W^\#$ has an injective completion
in $N$ fixing the embedded complete source $A$.  The unrecorded
fine colours of such a completion need not be invariant under
any structural automorphism and will not be treated as records.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Compose the structural projection $q$ with the injective completion
$B_0\hookrightarrow C_0\subseteq N$ from
\cref{thm:completable-reservoir}. This map preserves the owner names,
all $t_g$ and every recorded fine matrix, and is injective on each
actual packet. Its restriction to the zero-row source $A$ is the
prescribed induced embedding. The only possible collisions are
between distinct packets, since $q$ forgets their rows; all recorded
interpacket colours lie outside $F$.
These are exactly the hypotheses of \cref{lem:packet-collision},
with protected base $A$. That lemma separates the packets without
changing any record or the source embedding. The resulting completion
is used only as an existence certificate. Restrict it to one origin in each
actual packet. Since their image packets are pairwise distinct, the definition
in \cref{lem:full-owner-transversal} gives an injective completion of this
origin transversal in $\mathcal U_B$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact finite compatibility criterion for prescribed full rows]
\label{lem:R07-row-compatibility-iff}
Fix one structural map
$s(i,z)=(\sigma i,z+b_i)$ as in
\eqref{eq:R07-structural}. For each $i\in I$
let $T_i\subseteq G^I$ be a finite
set of ACTUAL source rows, possibly
empty. For each $f\in T_i$ prescribe
one target row $r_{i,f}\in G^I$
at structural index $\sigma i$.
For $f\in T_i$ put
\[
 A_{i,f}(j)=r_{i,f}(\sigma j)+f(j)+b_i
                  \qquad(j\in I).
\tag{RC1}\label{eq:R07-row-compatibility-shift}
\]
There is ONE matrix $C$ satisfying
\eqref{eq:R07-correction} such that
$\alpha_C$ sends EACH requested
ACTUAL packet $(i,f)$ to the
packet $(\sigma i,r_{i,f})$ if
and only if BOTH conditions hold:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item for every $i$, the functions
$A_{i,f}$ are identical over
ALL $f\in T_i$ (denote this
common function by $A_i$ when
$T_i\ne\varnothing$);
\item for every UNRECORDED pair
of DISTINCT structural indices
$\{i,j\}$ with
$T_i,T_j\ne\varnothing$,
\[
                         A_i(j)=A_j(i).
\tag{RC2}\label{eq:R07-row-compatibility-edge}
\]
\end{enumerate}
When each touched index has
ONE requested row and the
touched indices form a
recorded clique, both
conditions are automatic.
No condition is imposed
on diagonal entries $C_{ii}$,
nor on RECORDED off-diagonal
pairs. The source rows and
target rows are ACTUAL full
functions on all of $I$,
not owner-stabiliser cosets.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the requested image row
of $(i,f)$, formula
\eqref{eq:R07-lift} gives
\[
 r_{i,f}(\sigma j)
       = f(j)+C_{ij}+b_i
             \qquad(j\in I).
\]
Since $G$ has exponent two,
this holds precisely when
$C_{ij}=A_{i,f}(j)$ for
ALL j. A SINGLE row of C
works for every requested
packet over index i exactly
when (i) holds; all its
entries are then forced to
be $A_i(j)$. For an UNRECORDED
distinct pair $\{i,j\}$ the
only correction equation is
$C_{ij}=C_{ji}$.
If both indices are touched,
this is exactly (RC2);
hence (ii) is necessary.

Conversely assign the entire
row $C_{ij}=A_i(j)$ for
every touched index i.
Condition (i) makes these
assignments consistent inside
each row, and condition (ii)
makes them compatible with
ALL unrecorded constraints
whose two endpoints are
touched. For an unrecorded
pair with exactly one touched
endpoint set the other entry
equal to its already forced
value; with neither touched,
choose both entries zero.
Recorded off-diagonal entries
and all diagonal entries
have no additional equation
and may be chosen freely
unless already prescribed by
a touched row. This constructs
the required one matrix C.
There is no constraint across
two distinct actual packets
of the SAME structural index
except their compulsory
common affine row translation,
which is exactly (i).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Sharp obstructions for independently forced rows]
\label{rem:R07-row-conflict-controls}
Even with $G=\mathbb F_2$,
two different rows $f\ne f'$
over the SAME structural index
cannot both be sent to target
zero rows by one $\alpha_C$:
then the two functions in (RC1)
differ by $f+f'$, violating
condition (i). For two touched
DISTINCT UNRECORDED indices
$i,j$, choose source rows
with $f_i(j)=1$, $f_j(i)=0$,
target both zero, and $b_i=b_j=0$.
This forces $C_{ij}=1$ and
$C_{ji}=0$, contradicting
\eqref{eq:R07-correction}.
These failures explain why
the later unsafe-clique
argument first proves ONE
touched ACTUAL packet over
each structural index and
a RECORDED clique on those
indices, rather than choosing
row corrections independently.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Full-row permutations and zero-row requests]
\label{lem:R07-zero-row}
For every $C$ satisfying~\eqref{eq:R07-correction},
$\alpha_C$ is a permutation of $W$, commutes with every $t_g$,
preserves all the recorded fine colours of $W^\#$ and induces
$\gamma$ on its owner predicates.  Moreover, for every requested
source map that has structural extension $s$, a matrix $C$ can be
chosen such that $\alpha_C$ extends that map on the zero-row
source.  This includes requests with empty real domain.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed $i$, the assignment $f\mapsto f'$ permutes the
coordinates by $\sigma$ and translates each coordinate by a
constant; its inverse exists.  With $z\mapsto z+b_i$ this
proves bijectivity.  It is visibly compatible with all $t_g$.
The owner identity follows from~\eqref{eq:R07-structural}:
\[
 \operatorname{own}(\alpha_C(i,f,z))
 =(z+b_i)\beta_{\sigma i}
 =(z+b_i)(b_i\gamma\beta_i)=\gamma(z\beta_i).
\]
The projection $q$ intertwines $\alpha_C$ and $s$, and the
recorded matrices are precisely pullbacks along $q$; hence all
records are preserved for \emph{every} matrix $C$.

For a source-extension request, each touched index has one requested
row, namely the zero row, and its target is also the zero row.
The criterion \cref{lem:R07-row-compatibility-iff} therefore forces
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-forced-row}
 C_{ij}=b_i\qquad\text{for every }j\in I
\end{equation}
on each touched $i$. The touched indices form a recorded clique
because $A$ is complete, so the criterion supplies one legal matrix
$C$ satisfying all these requests simultaneously. This also covers
empty real domain, when no row is prescribed.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Strong PC on actual packets and gauge naturality}
\label{subsec:R07-PC}

Let
\[
 J=\{(i,f):i\in I,\ f\in G^I\}
\]
be the set of \emph{actual} full $G$-packets in $W$, and choose
their origins $o_p=(i,f,0)$ of owner type $s_p=\beta_i$.
For distinct $p=(i,f)$, $q=(j,k)$ in $J$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-initial-domain}
 D^0_{pq}=\begin{cases}
   \{c_{ij}\},&\{i,j\}\in\mathcal R\ (i\ne j),\\
   P_{\beta_i\beta_j},&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $c_{ij}=\col((i,0),(j,0))$ is the recorded origin
colour of $B_0$, and $P_{st}$ is the full \emph{diversity}
typed palette of $U_B$.  This includes $i=j$ with $f\ne k$:
its initial domain is the full same-sort palette.
Every $D^0_{pq}$ belongs to the corresponding strong exact family
$\mathcal H_{s_ps_q}$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:typed-exact-products}.  The concrete completion
from Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-W-completable} provides a compatible
actual fine colour $d^*_{pq}$ in each domain and a legal typed
triangle on every triple of \emph{distinct actual packets}.

Run all oriented strong path-consistency updates, with converse
symmetry maintained:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-PC}
 D_{pq}\longleftarrow D_{pq}\cap(D_{pr}*D_{rq})
 \quad(p,q,r\in J\text{ pairwise distinct}),
 \qquad D_{qp}=\overline{D_{pq}}.
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[Canonical nonempty PC fixed family]
\label{lem:R07-PC}
The updates~\eqref{eq:R07-PC} terminate at a family
$(D_{pq})_{p\ne q}$ of nonempty strong exact menus, independent
of the fair order of updates.  The final family is
path-consistent on every triple of distinct actual packets,
and any prescribed singleton record remains unchanged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The current $d^*_{pq}$ survives every update: if
$d^*_{pr}\in D_{pr}$ and $d^*_{rq}\in D_{rq}$, the actual
triangle with the third recorded pair witnesses
$d^*_{pq}\in D_{pr}*D_{rq}$.  Hence no update is empty.
Exact-product closure and nonempty exact-meet closure from
Lemma~\ref{lem:typed-exact-products} keep all domains strong
exact.  There are finitely many packets, finitely many menus,
and every update contracts a coordinate, so any fair schedule
stabilises.  Its limit is a common fixed point of all the
contracting updates and hence path-consistent.
For order independence, let $E\le D^0$ be any other common
fixed family of menus.  Initially $E\le D^0$ and it remains
below the current family after each update: multiplication
and intersection are monotone, and the fact that $E$ is fixed
means $E_{pq}\subseteq E_{pr}*E_{rq}$.  The terminal family
is thus the \emph{greatest} common fixed family below $D^0$.
This characterisation does not assert equality with the full
spectra of global completions.
\end{proof}

Choose independent origin shifts $\delta_p\in G$ for $p\in J$.
Transport the endpoint types and domains by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-gauge}
 s'_p=\delta_p s_p,\qquad
 (S_\delta D)_{pq}=(\delta_p+\delta_q)D_{pq}.
\end{equation}
The notation $(g+h)D$ means the \emph{represented endpoint
switch} of a typed menu: its endpoint types move separately as
indicated, and the underlying fine-colour atoms obey the actual
thin transport law.  In particular this is not a pure action
of $\Gamma$ fixing the raw fine names.

\begin{lemma}[PC gauge and pure-owner naturality]
\label{lem:R07-PC-gauge}
The greatest nonempty PC fixed family commutes with all the
independent origin shifts~\eqref{eq:R07-gauge} and with any
pure owner-language permutation $\gamma\in\Gamma$.  More
precisely, for distinct $p,r,q$, typed menus $A_{pr},B_{rq}$ satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-gauge-product}
 ((\delta_p+\delta_r)A_{pr})*((\delta_r+\delta_q)B_{rq})
 =(\delta_p+\delta_q)(A_{pr}*B_{rq}),
\end{equation}
with the \emph{moved endpoint owner types}; the same transport
commutes with intersections and converse.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An atom $c$ lies in $A_{pr}*B_{rq}$ exactly when there is
an actual legal triangle of three \emph{distinct} points of the
indicated owner sorts with its two sides in those menus and
its third side $c$.  Apply $t_{\delta_p},t_{\delta_r},
t_{\delta_q}$ independently to these witnesses, using the
actual translation law on each edge.  This produces a triangle
with colours on the left-hand side of~\eqref{eq:R07-gauge-product}.
The inverse translations prove the reverse inclusion.
The intermediate shift occurs in both incident edges and cancels
because $G$ has exponent two.  The argument does not require
distinct \emph{owner types}, only distinct actual points.
The same bijective transport commutes with intersections and
converses, so every PC update commutes with $S_\delta$.
The greatest-fixed-family characterisation from
Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-PC} gives naturality of the fixed point.
A pure $\gamma\in\Gamma$ fixes all underlying fine-colour
names and permutes only owner tags, realised by the ambient
quotient-owner lift from
Lemma~\ref{lem:full-owner-transversal}; it likewise commutes
with products, meets and PC.
\end{proof}

For any vertices $x=(i,f,z)$, $y=(j,k,w)$ in \emph{distinct
actual packets} $p=(i,f)$, $q=(j,k)$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-moving-domain}
 D_{xy}=(z+w)D_{pq},\qquad
 \operatorname{type}(D_{xy})=(z\beta_i,w\beta_j).
\end{equation}
This is a member of the relevant strong exact family and
satisfies $D_{t_gx,y}=gD_{xy}$ with endpoint types moved.

\begin{lemma}[Naturality under arbitrary full-row lifts]
\label{lem:R07-domain-covariance}
Let $\alpha_C$ be a map~\eqref{eq:R07-lift}, and denote by
$\pi_C(p)=(\sigma i,f')$ its induced permutation of actual
packets.  For all vertices $x,y$ in distinct actual packets,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-raw-domain-invariance}
  D_{\alpha_Cx,\alpha_Cy}=D_{xy}
\end{equation}
as \emph{raw fine-colour sets}; their endpoint owner types
are related by the pure permutation $\gamma$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every structural record is preserved by $s$.
The initial domain for $\pi_C(p),\pi_C(q)$ therefore equals
\[
 D^0_{\pi_C(p),\pi_C(q)}
 =(b_i+b_j)\,\gamma(D^0_{pq}),
\]
where $\gamma$ acts only on the owner tags.  For a hole both
sides are the full typed palette, and for a singleton record
the equality is exactly the structural thin law.
The PC-gauge and pure-$\Gamma$ naturality lemma gives the
same identity for the terminal domains:
\[
 D_{\pi_C(p),\pi_C(q)}=(b_i+b_j)\,\gamma(D_{pq}).
\]
But $\alpha_C(o_p)=t_{b_i}(o_{\pi_C(p)})$, and likewise
for $q$.  Hence for $x=t_zo_p$, $y=t_wo_q$ we have
\begin{align*}
 D_{\alpha_Cx,\alpha_Cy}
  &=(z+b_i+w+b_j)D_{\pi_C(p),\pi_C(q)}\\
  &=(z+w+b_i+b_j)(b_i+b_j)\,\gamma(D_{pq})\\
  &=\gamma((z+w)D_{pq})=D_{xy}
 \quad\text{as raw fine-colour sets}.
\end{align*}
The last equality means that $\gamma$ fixes colour names,
not that it fixes the endpoint owner types.  No equivariant
choice of packet origins is assumed.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Effective rows and verification of the decoder}
\label{subsec:R07-decoder}

For $x=(i,f,z)$ let its effective row be
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-effective}
 \chi_x(j)=z+f(j)\quad(j\in I).
\end{equation}
For $x$ above structural index $i$ and $y$ above index $j$,
put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-reading-parameter}
 u(x,y)=\chi_x(j)+\chi_y(i)\in G.
\end{equation}
This parameter is symmetric in $x,y$.
For vertices $x,y$ in \emph{different actual packets} set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-decoding}
  d(x,y)=
 h^{u(x,y)}_{\operatorname{own}(x),\operatorname{own}(y)}(D_{xy}),
\end{equation}
and for vertices in the same actual packet use their original
thin $G$-colour.  This defines a complete colouring of $W$ by
actual named raw fine colours, once legality is established.

We now prove \cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa} for this colouring.

\begin{proof}
For two distinct actual packets $p,q$, converse
compatibility of the readings
(Proposition~\ref{prop:moving-readings}) ensures
$d(y,x)=\overline{d(x,y)}$.  Within a packet, the original
thin colours are symmetric and satisfy their complete
regular packet equations.

For a triangle with vertices in \emph{three distinct actual}
packets, the transported PC domains form an oriented strong
PC triple by Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-PC-gauge}.  Proposition~
\ref{prop:moving-readings} allows the three reading parameters
$u(x,y)$, $u(y,z)$ and $u(x,z)$ to be chosen independently;
thus the three decoded atoms form a legal fine-colour triangle.

If the triangle has exactly two actual packets, say
$x'=t_gx$ in packet $p$ and $y$ in packet $q\ne p$,
then the thin transport law gives
$D_{x'y}=gD_{xy}$, the owner of $x'$ is
$g\operatorname{own}(x)$, and
$u(x',y)=u(x,y)+g$.  The switching identity
\eqref{eq:reading-switch} therefore says
\[
 d(x',y)=g\,d(x,y),
\]
which is exactly the triangle law forced by the thin edge
$\col(x,x')=g$.  No assumption that the structural indices
$i,j$ differ is needed.  A triangle wholly within one packet
is an original $G$-packet triangle.  The age is triangle-defined,
so all these checks place the complete finite structure in
$\Age(N)$ with the assigned actual owner labels.

On a recorded pair the initial domain was a singleton, and it
remains that singleton by Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-PC}.
In particular every pair of distinct source packets has its
original recorded fine colour matrix.  The internal thin
colours also remain original, so the zero-row embedding of $A$
is an induced embedding.

For the automorphism assertion compute, for
$x=(i,f,z)$ and $j\in I$, using~\eqref{eq:R07-lift},
\[
 \chi_{\alpha_C x}(\sigma j)
 =(z+b_i)+(f(j)+C_{ij}+b_i)
 =\chi_x(j)+C_{ij}.
\]
For $x$ over index $i$ and $y$ over $j$ it follows that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:R07-row-covariance}
 u(\alpha_Cx,\alpha_Cy)=u(x,y)+C_{ij}+C_{ji}.
\end{equation}
The domains are invariant as raw colour sets by
Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-domain-covariance}, and the owner types
transform by $\gamma$.  For $i\ne j$ a recorded pair has a
singleton domain, so the reading is forced independent of
$u$ and no condition on $C_{ij}+C_{ji}$ is needed.  If
$i\ne j$ is unrecorded,~\eqref{eq:R07-correction} makes
that sum zero.  If $i=j$ but $x,y$ lie in different actual
packets, its value is $2C_{ii}=0$, with \emph{no} diagonal
constraint.  The pure-$\Gamma$ covariance of every $h^u$
from Proposition~\ref{prop:moving-readings} therefore yields
\[
 d(\alpha_Cx,\alpha_Cy)=d(x,y)
\]
for all pairs of distinct actual packets.  Within a packet,
$\alpha_C$ commutes with every named translation and hence
preserves its thin colours.  It is bijective by
Lemma~\ref{lem:R07-zero-row} and preserves all owner labels
with the prescribed language component.  Thus it is a $\Gamma$-automorphism of the decoded complete witness
for every structural $s$ and legal $C$. If $s$ is a chosen source
extension, \cref{lem:R07-zero-row} supplies a legal $C$ for which
this automorphism also has the prescribed values on the zero-row
source.

Finally the universe $W$, its PC fixed family and its
completed fine colouring were chosen \emph{before} any of
the correction matrices $C$ associated with the finitely
many requests.  Consequently one and the same finite witness
works for all of them, without a coherent choice of
extensions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Global diagonal thin translations survive the R07 decoder]
\label{lem:R07-global-diagonal-thin}
Let $\rho:G\to\Gamma$
be the ACTUAL homomorphism
induced by the original global
thin translations on the
finite $G$-invariant owner
set $B$. The map $\rho$
is NOT assumed injective:
a nontrivial thin owner
stabiliser $H$ may lie
in its kernel.
On the completed full-row
R07 universe $W$ define,
for every $g\in G$,
\[
          \widehat t_g(i,f,z)=(i,f,z+g).
\tag{RDT}\label{eq:R07-global-diagonal-thin}
\]

\begin{proof}
On the structural reservoir take $s_g(i,z)=(i,z+g)$ with
owner component $\rho(g)$. This is a structural
$\Gamma$-automorphism: it commutes with every $t_h$, preserves
owner names through $\rho(g)$, and preserves each recorded matrix
because simultaneous thin translation changes its fine atoms by
$g+g=0$. It also preserves which packet pairs are recorded.
In \eqref{eq:R07-structural} we therefore have
$\sigma=\mathrm{id}_I$ and $b_i=g$ for every $i$.

Choose the constant correction matrix $C_{ij}=g$. It satisfies
\eqref{eq:R07-correction}, and its lift has
$f'(j)=f(j)+g+g=f(j)$. Thus $\alpha_C=\widehat t_g$.
The universal automorphism assertion of
\cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa} now proves that this permutation
preserves every literal decoded fine colour. Its action on each
actual packet is the original regular $G$-action, while its owner
component is exactly $\rho(g)$, which need not be faithful.
\end{proof}
