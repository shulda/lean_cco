\begin{remark}[Hidden SAP does not force G1-positive quotient diagonals]
\label{rem:typed-hidden-sap-not-G1-positivity}
The CCO-free hereditary SAP criterion
\cref{cor:typed-abstract-relative-kernel-sap-iff}
does NOT imply the positive-orbital
G1 conclusion if the original FULL
no-finite-relative-index hypothesis
is omitted.

Indeed use the ACTUAL homogeneous
visible SAP+semantic-CCO source
\cref{ex:typed-term-visible-index-two-hidden-sap}:
\[
 M=\mathbb N_{\rm block}\times
      \mathbb F_2\times\mathbb N_{\rm clone},
 \qquad
 \Id<K<J<\Top,\quad[J:K]=2,
\]
whose original diversity
atoms are $p$ (same block,
same bit, distinct clones),
$q$ (same block, opposite
bits), and $r$ (different
blocks). Name ONLY the
coarse J-quotient sort
$Q_J=M/J$ and its projection,
with all actual canonical
hidden binary orbitals.
As proved in that example,
its entire hereditary
unary-closed hidden age
HAS SAP, although the
original invariant lattice
has an index-two interval.

The TRUE HIDDEN diagonal
orbital
$\Delta_{Q_J}\subseteq Q_J^2$
has old DISTINCT-real-pair
pullback
\[
            J^\circ=\{p,q\}.
\tag{FG1}\label{eq:typed-finite-index-G1-failure}
\]
But this menu is NOT the
STRONG endpoint profile of
ANY original positive
atomic word in the
three-letter palette
$\{p,q,r\}$. If the word
contains an $r$-letter,
choose its intermediate
block indices from the
infinite common block set
so each r-step changes
block and the final block
differs from the initial
one. The resulting fully
injective real path has
endpoint atom $r$, so
the word profile is NOT
contained in $\{p,q\}$.
If the word contains
NO r-letter, its points
all lie within ONE block,
and the parity of the
number of $q$-letters
uniquely determines the
endpoint bit difference:
for distinct endpoints
its STRONG profile is
$\{p\}$ or $\{q\}$,
never $\{p,q\}$.
The real identity output
cannot be added as a
fictitious positive atom.

The obstruction already
occurs at the equality-
inclusive RAW exact
level. A word containing
NO $r$ has FIXED endpoint
bit parity, so its RAW
relation cannot contain
BOTH original diversity
atoms $p,q$. A word
with EXACTLY one $r$
has all endpoints in
different blocks, so
cannot contain $p,q$
either. A word with
at least TWO $r$ letters
contains the old
different-block atom
$r$: use at least three
distinct block indices
along the successive
$r$-steps, leaving the
first and last blocks
different. Consequently
ANY nonempty finite
intersection of RAW
word profiles containing
BOTH $p$ and $q$
ALSO contains $r$.
The actual invariant
equivalence
$J=\Id\cup p\cup q$
is therefore NOT a
FULL RAW exact
equivalence in this
source.

Thus even the
COMPLETELY NAMED coarse
hidden quotient diagonal
has NO positive old
STRONG word-pullback
in this source, despite
the hidden hereditary SAP
given by the abstract
kernel criterion.
The actual $J$ quotient
is not covered by the
general ORIGINAL FULL
EXACT quotient lifting
theorem, although its
canonical hidden expansion
is homogeneous directly
by explicit block actions.
In the terminal G1 theorem
FULL no-relative-index
two is a substantive
RAW exactness and
positive-path hypothesis,
not an irrelevant
technical consequence
of hidden SAP.
\end{remark}

\begin{construction}[Typed guarded real lift]
\label{con:typed-guarded-lift}
Let \(C\) be a finite UNARY-ADMISSIBLE
(\cref{def:typed-unary-admissible-source}), functionally
closed partial transition-enriched hidden structure.
Before introducing two-root constraints, its full
one-generated induced diagrams are already actual
completed hidden diagrams by the explicit
unary-admissibility hypothesis, and have size at most
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\) by
\cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}. Thus ALL intended
unary function, transition and functional-identity
equations hold literally in \(C\) on every
one-generated closure. An arbitrary partial source
FAILING this hypothesis is NOT covered by this
guarded-lift construction or by the uniform
bounded-CLOSED-obstruction theorem below: its
one-generated closure can be arbitrarily large.
Such sources are excluded by condition (ii) in the
eventual HN local-finiteness test, as checked explicitly
in \cref{thm:typed-direct-irreducible-unary-HN}.
After this initial gate, test all bounded
ACTUALLY joined multi-root semantic-cylinder,
old-tuple and relation patterns as below.
Since every real sort and every
represented quotient sort is transitive and all unary coordinate terms are
\(0\)-definable, every prospective representative of a valid root realises the
same one-root diagram.  In particular, a unary self-equation or a relation
entirely internal to one functional closure needs no artificial two-witness
encoding.

Perform the analogous bounded check on every ACTUAL displayed datum
joining distinct OLD hidden source vertices: take the unary-functional
closure of its complete SOURCE support, which is irreducible because
a displayed relation tuple contains all its specified joining roots,
and require the induced hidden diagram on THIS irreducible
closed source support to occur in a full typed expansion.
This is exactly the valid case of
\cref{lem:typed-joined-source-support}.
In particular a relation only between proper quotient
term values of otherwise unjoined real roots does
NOT license completion of their reducible real two-root diagram
(see \cref{rem:typed-term-join-not-irreducible}).
All constraints below are attached to their ACTUAL old
displayed source endpoint vertices, not to arbitrary pairs
of real roots whose unary terms happen to be related.  For a displayed old relation of arity \(r\), do this on the union
of the \(r\) one-generated closures of its support.  The maximum arity and
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\) are fixed, so every failure again has a uniform bounded
witness.  These checks include reflection: a mandatory cylinder, term
equality, or canonical old tuple type which is absent from the partial source
is a malformed local diagram just as much as a wrong positive symbol is.
In particular, if TWO DISTINCT source vertices \(b\ne c\)
belong to the SAME represented proper quotient sort
\(B_{a,J}\), a displayed relation whose actual
quotient map is the IDENTITY cannot demand
\(b\mapsto c\): such a map would force their
equality in every completed hidden structure,
contradicting injectivity of a hidden
homomorphism-embedding. This is a bounded
two-generated identity/equality defect,
not a positive strong-diversity theta constraint.
When the two occurrences denote the SAME old
hidden vertex, the identity holds inside its
already checked one-generated unary closure.
The reduction
\cref{lem:typed-identity-transport-redundant}
guarantees that all identity-containing
relational transport cases fall into these
two alternatives.

Give every old real vertex its own real witness and every old proper coordinate
vertex a designated prospective real representative in its underlying real
sort.  For displayed data whose endpoint hidden vertices are distinct, impose
on the corresponding pair of witnesses all applicable positive profiles:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item a projection equation to a \(J\)-coordinate imposes \(J\);
\item an upward quotient equation from a \(J\)-coordinate to a
      \(K\)-coordinate imposes \(K\);
\item a NONTRIVIAL named quotient-transition
      equation \(\pi_{R,J}(b)=c\) imposes
      the IDENTITY-FREE reachable graph profile
      \(J^\circ\starprod R\starprod(J^R)^\circ\) from
      \cref{lem:typed-transition-cylinder};
      identity-containing \(R\) is redundant by
      \cref{lem:typed-identity-transport-redundant}
      and is checked as a bounded unary
      functional-identity datum instead;
\item a displayed semantic cylinder imposes its named cylinder profile;
\item a specified old real atomic pair type \(t\) imposes \(\{t\}\).
\end{enumerate}
For each genuinely IRREDUCIBLE
one- or two-generated OLD
source support already joined
by a displayed relation datum,
take the WHOLE locally vetted
COMPLETE induced closed hidden
diagram, not merely the first
primitive joining relation.
Apply the complete two-root
comparison only when the
corresponding induced
\(\cl_C\{b,c\}\) is actually
irreducible; the test is
justified by
\cref{lem:typed-joined-source-support},
not by a mere cross relation
between unary term values.
Any original one-root projection
or unary term identity stays
inside its own checked closure.
For every ordered pair of
unary TERM VALUES from these
two closures, impose on the
two designated real ROOT
representatives the POSITIVE
pullback profile of its
mandated complete named
cylinder, quotient-class
equality, or original
real atomic pair type.
This is a FINITE list:
\cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}
bounds the available unary
term functions and closures,
and the old named sorts/atom
palettes are finite.

Each such term-pair
preimage profile is a
NONEMPTY positive STRONG
reachable spectrum by the
direct complete-orbital
pullback theorem
\cref{lem:typed-term-pair-orbit-pullback}.
This uses that the mandatory
named comparison in the
complete OLD support is a
SINGLE actual hidden
semantic-cylinder orbital
(or a named quotient
transition graph/equality),
and that its term functions
have proper represented
real kernels.
The pullback is exactly
ONE old representative
cylinder
\(\operatorname{Cyl}_{J,K}(t_0)\),
not a complement, not an
uncontrolled union and not
an arbitrary product of
independently selected
representative types.
Its equality-containing
logical-Id exceptional case
is handled as a bounded
OLD real identity defect
or a one-root unary
equation, never by a
synthetic STRONG identity.
This is the explicit
binary positivity interface
required by the guarded
lift construction.

When the local source diagram
has only one root occurrence,
all identities are already
checked in its one-generated
unary closure. For an UNJOINED
pair of closures, do not
insert negative cross
constraints or arbitrarily
choose a completion cylinder:
homomorphism-embeddings are
not required to reflect
absent relations between
different irreducible
components.

Keep every displayed complete old higher-support factor distinguished; its
complete canonical pair shadow is therefore already among the pair constraints
above.  On each constrained OLD witness pair \((b,c)\), the bounded
complete-pattern test has already certified ONE irreducible OLD source
support \(\mathfrak S_{bc}=\cl_C\{b,c\}\), on which ALL imposed term-pair
comparisons hold together.  The direct common-survivor argument of
\cref{lem:typed-one-source-support-per-guarded-pair} (proved immediately
below WITHOUT using this exact-domain intersection) therefore produces
ONE original fine diversity atom belonging to EVERY imposed positive
profile on this pair.  In particular their total intersection is
NONEMPTY, not merely pairwise intersecting.  If the OLD complete-pattern
test fails, its bounded irreducible source support is already a
non-completable witness, so the construction stops before this stage.
Since the imposed spectra are positive typed reachable profiles,
their nonempty finite intersection \(D\) is a typed strong exact domain
by \cref{lem:typed-exact-domain-calculus}.  This common-survivor
existence step does NOT invoke typed 2-Helly; strong Helly remains
essential elsewhere for exact product algebra and theta completion.
Attach a fixed private typed theta bundle realising \(D\), and record
the full transition-closed root signature
from \cref{def:typed-transition-root-signature}.  Denote the resulting
visible guarded network, together with its distinguished higher-support
factors, by \(G_{\mathrm{typ}}(C)\).
\end{construction}

\begin{lemma}[A single irreducible OLD source support for each guarded pair]
\label{lem:typed-one-source-support-per-guarded-pair}
In the BINARY terminal construction
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift},
let \((b,c)\) be a pair of
DISTINCT actual old hidden
source vertices on whose
prospective real witness pair
the construction imposes at
least one positive root
constraint. Then all profiles
imposed on this SAME witness
pair are consequences of
the ONE induced old
functionally closed diagram
\[
                   \mathfrak S_{bc}=\cl_C\{b,c\},
\]
which is irreducible in the
PARTIAL OLD hidden source
language and has at most
\(2m_{\mathrm{typ}}\) old
vertices. The construction's
complete-pattern check
therefore requires \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\)
itself to be a completed
induced hidden member.
Consequently ONE embedding
of \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\) into a full
hidden expansion, followed
by distinct real
representative selection,
provides an old fine atomic
pair \(t\) lying
SIMULTANEOUSLY in EVERY
positive profile imposed on
\((b,c)\).

This is not asserted for
an arbitrary pair of
OLD root ancestors \(b,c\)
whose descendants
\(\tau(b),\sigma(c)\) are
related in \(C\): unless
\(b,c\) themselves are
joined by displayed unary
or binary source data,
the guarded construction
issues NO forced
complete-pair spectrum on
their witness pair.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A root-pair constraint is
issued only from ACTUALLY
DISPLAYED OLD hidden data:
an old unary function value
\(f(b)=c\) (or \(f(c)=b\)),
a displayed binary named
relation on \((b,c)\), or
a complete term-pair
comparison from the induced
closed OLD support generated
by the SAME displayed
endpoint pair. This is the
source-pair restriction
explicit in
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}
and excludes synthesising
a relation on their unjoined
real ancestors.

In the unary case
\(c\in\cl_C\{b\}\), so
\(\cl_C\{b,c\}=\cl_C\{b\}\).
A one-generated
functionally closed source
diagram is irreducible:
every free-amalgam factor
containing its generator
contains all its unary
term values. The reversed
unary case is identical.
In the binary case, the
displayed relation tuple
containing both \(b,c\)
must lie in one factor of
any proposed free
decomposition; closure
under unary functions then
puts ALL of \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\)
in that factor. Hence
\(\mathfrak S_{bc}\) is irreducible.

Every other spectrum that
the guarded lift places on
the SAME witness pair is
the positive old
representative-pair pullback
of a mandatory relation
or equation in the COMPLETE
induced diagram of this
same \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\). In
particular two profiles
from different displayed
data with old endpoint
pair \((b,c)\) are not
verified by unrelated
choices of models: both
are tested against the
ONE completed local
diagram \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\).
The finite complete-pattern
test and
\cref{lem:typed-joined-source-support}
therefore provide an
embedding of \(\mathfrak S_{bc}\)
into one actual full
hidden expansion, or
else reject it as a
bounded noncompletable
irreducible defect.

Use \cref{lem:typed-real-sort-splitting}
on that ONE completed
local factor to choose
pairwise distinct actual
real witnesses for its
old vertices while
preserving all displayed
unary equations and
semantic cylinders. The
original fine atom \(t\)
between the representatives
of \(b,c\) lies in the
positive pullback spectrum
of EACH required comparison,
so lies in their total
intersection. No
cross-support amalgamation
or global completion of
\(C\) was assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Private theta cycles have OLD source endpoints]
\label{lem:typed-private-theta-cycle}
Let \(G_{\mathrm{typ}}(C)\) be the visible
network of \cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.
Every positive root-pair constraint
is issued with an ACTUAL OLD hidden
source endpoint pair and is realised
by a finite theta network whose
interior vertices are disjoint from
all old root witnesses and from
all other private interiors. The
only specified incidences of a
private interior with vertices
outside its own theta are at the
two designated OLD root witnesses.
Consequently, every specified simple
cycle of \(G_{\mathrm{typ}}(C)\) either
is contained in one private theta,
or traverses any theta which it
meets outside its root vertices
along a COMPLETE branch from
one designated OLD root witness
to the other, without repeated
interior vertices. Upon contracting
these branches, the endpoints
and the root-signature anchors of
EVERY macro side belong to the
ORIGINAL partial hidden source.
This is independent of which
completion atom will later be
chosen on any unrecorded root pair.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a theta with designated old
root witnesses \(p,q\).
Its positive specified graph
is the union of finitely many
internally vertex-disjoint
\(p\)-to-\(q\) paths, with no
other incident specified edges
at their interior vertices.
Any simple specified cycle that
uses one such interior vertex
must follow its path until it
reaches a root: it cannot leave
to another private network in
the interior. If the cycle
returns through another branch
of the same theta, it has
closed inside the theta. Otherwise
the cycle visits each of \(p,q\)
at most once and must use one
whole branch between them.
Thus the stated contraction
uses only the two OLD named
root endpoints. All root
signatures were attached to
these endpoints in the
construction, and the
private interiors introduce
no NEW old hidden generators.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Uniform size of complete typed two-root gadgets]
\label{lem:typed-two-root-uniform-size}
There is a finite integer
\(B_{\mathrm{pair}}\), depending
ONLY on the fixed finite typed
hidden language and its original
finite typed strong exact-domain
families, such that each locally
admissible TWO-generated joined
hidden diagram from
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}
has an equivalent guarded
two-real-root constraint
represented by one nonempty
exact-domain theta network
with at most \(B_{\mathrm{pair}}\)
formal vertices.
This includes ALL mandated
named pair relations between
values of unary terms in the
two root closures and
does not presume an unbounded
choice of representative
points or transition words.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(u\) be the uniform number
of possible distinct unary
term values from one hidden
sort, supplied by
\cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}.
A two-generated functionally
closed source diagram therefore
contains at most \(2u\)
term values. It has at most
\((2u)^2\) ordered term-value
pairs and finitely many
possible named atomic,
semantic-cylinder and
functional-identity conditions
per such pair, bounded solely
by the language.

By
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}
and the identity and
RAW/STRONG reductions
\cref{lem:typed-identity-transport-redundant,lem:typed-raw-strong-saturation},
each required cross comparison
reduces to one of the finitely
many nonempty typed STRONG
reachable profiles from the
finite collection
\(\bigsqcup_{a,b}\Rch^{ab}\).
For each reachable profile
fix ONCE an old finite
positive atomic word
presenting it. Such a word
can be chosen of length
at most the size of the
finite typed semigroup
\(S_{\rm typ}\): a shortest
nonzero word cannot
repeat a typed prefix
product state, since
removing the intervening
return loop would otherwise
give a shorter word with
the SAME resulting typed
profile.

A pairwise-compatible
joined diagram therefore
imposes the meet of a
uniformly bounded finite
number of fixed-length
word profiles. That meet
belongs to one of the
finitely many nonempty
typed exact domains
\(\Dat^{ab}\). Choose ONCE
a private theta
presentation for EVERY
such exact domain. The
maximum of their finite
vertex counts, together
with the bounded source
root term anchors, gives
the required
\(B_{\mathrm{pair}}\).
If the local conditions
are incompatible, a
bounded pair of displayed
profiles already witnesses
the empty intersection
by typed 2-Helly, and no
gadget is constructed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Two-generated hidden reflection is an atomic-root test]
\label{lem:typed-two-root-complete-reflection}
Work in the terminal typed hidden signature
with its FINITE family of real and
proper quotient sorts, unary projections,
upward maps, quotient transitions,
and complete named semantic cylinders.
Let \(b,c\) be TWO distinct root
positions of a finite functionally
closed PARTIAL hidden structure.
Assume their one-generated closures
and the full induced diagram on
each ACTUALLY IRREDUCIBLE
two-generated OLD source support
passed the bounded complete-pattern
test of
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift},
as warranted by
\cref{lem:typed-joined-source-support}.
Suppose some displayed datum
joins the two closures in an
IRREDUCIBLE source piece.
Let \(D_{bc}\) be the nonempty
intersection of ALL positive
typed representative-pair profiles
imposed by those data, as in
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.

Then EVERY actual old diversity
atom \(t\in D_{bc}\) on the
two chosen distinct real
representatives interprets
the same COMPLETE named
two-generated hidden diagram
on the source one-generated
closures: it preserves and
reflects all displayed unary
term equalities, projection
and quotient-transition values,
named semantic-cylinder
relations, and complete old
real pair atomic colours.
The same conclusion applies
to each binary pair inside a
distinguished higher-support
complete OLD source factor;
the support-\(\ge3\) tuples
themselves remain conditional
on the separately stated
support-normal completion
interface.

No claim is made that
an UNJOINED pair of old
hidden root closures must
have its absent binary
relations reflected:
homomorphism-embeddings
allow different irreducible
components to gain
new cross relations and,
where permitted, overlap
outside their common base.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A unary term beginning at
a real root is, by
\cref{lem:typed-coordinate-normal-form},
either the identity real
point or a named represented
quotient coordinate followed
by finitely many upward and
transition maps. A term
beginning at a proper
coordinate root has the
same form starting from
that named quotient.
All internal one-root
equations are already checked
by the bounded one-generated
induced diagram.

For two terms based at
different joined roots,
their output equality or
their COMPLETE binary
named relation is determined
by the actual old
representative-pair atomic
orbit:
an equality of quotient
coordinates pulls back to
a named invariant
equivalence-pair palette;
a genuine quotient-transition
graph is an IDENTITY-FREE
typed positive cylinder
\cref{lem:typed-transition-cylinder}
after applying the REAL-Id
reduction
\cref{lem:typed-identity-transport-redundant};
and every other pair of
represented coordinates has
one unique semantic cylinder
from
\cref{lem:typed-cylinder-realisation}.
The RAW-to-STRONG saturation
identity
\cref{lem:typed-raw-strong-saturation}
makes these named cylinders
the ACTUAL off-diagonal
representative-pair spectra,
including repeated sorts
and nonzero quotient kernels.
No independent new value
for a composed unary
transition may be chosen.

On the SOURCE side, the
complete locally vetted
two-generated diagram records
the unique actual cylinder
on EVERY compatible pair
of term values and all
mandatory identities.
The preceding explicit
ALL-TERM comparison step in
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}
places the positive pullback
of EACH such named orbit
among the profiles
intersected to form
\(D_{bc}\). Therefore
\(t\in D_{bc}\) implies
that the actual target
term-value pair has exactly
that recorded cylinder
(or quotient equality)
for EVERY such source
term pair. Since the
completed hidden language
partitions all compatible
off-diagonal coordinate pairs
by their cylinders,
preservation also gives
REFLECTION: no different
named cylinder can hold
for the same pair in the
target. If the recorded
datum is equality, its
actual quotient-class
relation is already forced
by the corresponding
kernel or named unary map;
if the source says two
values are different,
the locally complete
off-diagonal cylinder
prevents their identification.

For old real-root pairs
the named original fine
atomic colours partition
all distinct real pairs.
Because the corresponding
singleton was imposed in
the guarded menu, it is
preserved and reflected.
The same argument works
pairwise inside any
distinguished complete
old higher-support factor.
A separate support-normal
assumption, not this binary
pair calculation, handles
the remaining higher-arity
tuple data. Thus the
full joined two-generated
hidden diagram is
reconstructed from ANY
\(t\in D_{bc}\), as claimed.
