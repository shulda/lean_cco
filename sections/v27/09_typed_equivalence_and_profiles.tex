\begin{lemma}[Nonzero typed equivalence reproduction]
\label{lem:typed-equivalence-reproduction}
Let $E$ be the diversity palette of a nonzero invariant
same-sort equivalence relation in a finite-named-sorted
homogeneous symmetric SAP source. If $C\subseteq E$ is
a nonempty set of same-sort diversity atoms, then
\[
                   E\starprod C=E=C\starprod E,
              \qquad E\starprod E=E.
 \tag{TER}\label{eq:typed-equivalence-reproduction}
\]
The assertion is about STRONG off-diagonal products,
not about an equality-labelled edge.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The inclusion $E\starprod C\subseteq E$
is transitivity of the actual equivalence.
Choose any old atom $c\in C$ and any
old diversity atom $s\in E$.
Realise an $s$-edge $xz$ and a $c$-edge
$xy$ with their old $x$-endpoints aligned
by source homogeneity. Apply SAP to
amalgamate these two complete old
two-vertex structures over $\{x\}$ so
that $y\ne z$. Both $y,z$ are in the
$E$-class of $x$, hence the old
diversity colour of $yz$ belongs to $E$.
The genuine three-distinct-points
triangle proves $s\in C\starprod E$.
The same argument with the factors reversed
gives $s\in E\starprod C$.
Taking $C=E$ proves idempotence.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Typed no-return into a same-sort equivalence]
\label{lem:typed-terminal-no-return}
In a finite named-sorted homogeneous symmetric
terminal SAP+CCO source, let $J$ be a nonzero
represented full exact same-sort equivalence
palette on a sort $a$. For any two nonempty
SAME-SORT STRONG reachable profiles
$P,Q\in\Rch^{aa}$,
\[
       P\starprod Q\subseteq J
          \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
       P\subseteq J,\quad Q\subseteq J.
 \tag{TNR}\label{eq:typed-no-return}
\]
The same conclusion holds for any finite positive
product contained in $J$. Internal path vertices
of $P,Q$ may belong to other named sorts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $J^\circ=J\setminus\Id_a$ and saturate the
two menus by this NONZERO original equivalence:
\[
 \widehat P=J^\circ\starprod P\starprod J^\circ,
 \qquad
 \widehat Q=J^\circ\starprod Q\starprod J^\circ.
\]
Nonzero equivalence reproduction
\cref{lem:typed-equivalence-reproduction},
same-sort STRONG commutativity and associativity
give
\[
 \widehat P\starprod\widehat Q
 =J^\circ\starprod(P\starprod Q)\starprod J^\circ
 \subseteq J^\circ.
\]
The actual RAW/STRONG comparison
\cref{lem:typed-raw-strong-saturation}
shows that $\widehat P,\widehat Q$ are
the DIVERSITY parts of the FULL OLD
$J$-bi-saturated relations $J;P;J$ and
$J;Q;J$ respectively. Thus they induce
well-defined nonempty, symmetric and SERIAL
invariant binary relations $\bar P,\bar Q$
on the actual quotient $M_a/J$.

We justify the quotient-composition implication
without an unproved simultaneous representative
choice. Suppose quotient classes $u,v,w$ satisfy
$u\bar P v$ and $v\bar Q w$, with $u\ne w$.
Choose one ACTUAL real representative $y\in v$.
Full OLD $J$-saturation permits representative
pairs $x\in u,z\in w$ with
$x\widehat P y\widehat Q z$.
All $J$-classes are infinite by visible SAP
and $J\ne\Id_a$, so the representatives may
be chosen pairwise distinct even if $u=v$
or $v=w$. The resulting old STRONG
two-edge triangle has its endpoint diversity
atom in $\widehat P\starprod\widehat Q
\subseteq J^\circ$, while $u\ne w$ puts
the endpoints in different $J$-classes.
Contradiction. Hence
\[
                \bar P\circ\bar Q\subseteq\Delta_{M_a/J}.
\]

For clarity, the remaining quotient argument
is purely about symmetric SERIAL relations.
Take $u\bar P v$. Every $\bar Q$-neighbour
of $v$ must be $u$, by the displayed
composition inclusion, and there is such
a neighbour by seriality; hence
$\bar Q(v)=\{u\}$. By symmetry
$v\bar Q u$. Every $\bar P$-neighbour
$w$ of $v$ must therefore equal $u$,
and symmetry supplies the neighbour $u$.
Consequently
\[
                 \bar P(v)=\bar Q(v)=\{u\}.
\]
For every quotient class $v$ this defines
ONE map $\iota(v)=u$. Symmetry gives
$\iota^2=\mathrm{id}$; group invariance gives
equivariance. Thus $\bar P$ and $\bar Q$
are the graphs of the SAME equivariant
involution on the transitive quotient $M_a/J$.

If $\iota$ were nonidentity, transitivity
and equivariance would make it FIXED-POINT-FREE.
The union of its graph with the quotient
diagonal would then be an invariant
equivalence of class size exactly two.
Its actual old inverse image defines an
invariant original equivalence strictly
above $J$ with relative index two,
contrary to terminality (indeed this is
a strict Hasse cover). Thus $\iota$
is the identity. Both saturated profiles
are contained in FULL $J$, and since
$P\subseteq\widehat P$, $Q\subseteq\widehat Q$
by nonzero same-sort equivalence reproduction,
both original profiles lie in $J$.

For a positive product of more than two
factors, apply the two-factor assertion
first to its initial factor and the
remaining nonempty STRONG reachable
product, then iterate. No intermediate
logical equality factor is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Eventual typed powers of a same-sort profile]
\label{lem:typed-profile-pure-power}
Let $W\in\Rch^{aa}$ be a nonempty STRONG
same-sort diversity word profile in a
terminal typed SAP+CCO source, and let $F_W$
be the diversity palette of connectedness in
the undirected old invariant $W$-graph.
There exists $N_W$ such that
\[
                  W^{\starprod n}=F_W
                            \quad(n\ge N_W).
 \tag{TWP}\label{eq:typed-profile-pure-power}
\]
In particular, $F_W$ is a nonempty strong
reachable idempotent equivalence palette.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose one actual old atomic $t\subseteq W$.
Every vertex of the named transitive sort $a$
has a $t$-neighbour. Hence every connected
component of the $W$-graph contains a
$t$-orbital subgraph and therefore an odd
$t$-cycle, by
\cref{lem:typed-terminal-pure-power};
in particular it is nonbipartite.
In a connected nonbipartite undirected graph
there is, between any two distinct connected
vertices, a $W$-walk of every sufficiently
large length. For one pair in each of the
finitely many old atomic diversity orbitals
contained in $F_W$, choose a common threshold.
Old homogeneity transports those witnesses
to all pairs of the same atom, and typed SAP
injectivisation \cref{lem:typed-injectivisation}
fixes both distinct endpoints while making
all path occurrences distinct. Thus every old
diversity atom in $F_W$ belongs to the
STRONG word profile $W^{\starprod n}$
for all sufficiently large $n$.
The reverse inclusion follows from connectivity.
Since $W$ is a positive reachable profile,
its positive powers are reachable; taking
$n$ and $2n$ beyond the threshold gives
$F_W\starprod F_W=F_W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact two-edge return test for complete $J$-cylinders]
\label{lem:typed-complete-J-cylinder-return}
Let $J>\Id_a$ be an invariant equivalence on
one original named transitive REAL sort of a
homogeneous symmetric visible SAP source.
Let $T,S$ be TWO nonempty, J-BISATURATED,
COMPLETE semantic same-sort DIVERSITY
cylinders, each DISJOINT from $J$.
Then the following are equivalent:
\[
        (T\starprod S)\cap J^\circ
                \ne\varnothing
             \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad T=S.
\tag{JRT}\label{eq:typed-complete-J-cylinder-return}
\]
No CCO, NR2, MI, exact-domain Helly
or reset theorem is needed for this
purely complete-orbital return test.
The COMPLETENESS of the two cylinders
is indispensable: it is not a claim
for arbitrary J-saturated unions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose some ACTUAL three-distinct-point
old triangle witnesses
$x\,T\,y\,S\,z$ with $xJz$.
Since $S$ is left/right J-saturated,
$ySz$ and $zJx$ imply $ySx$.
Same-sort symmetry gives $xSy$.
But $xTy$ as well, so the
single OLD fine diversity atom
of $(x,y)$ lies in BOTH complete
J-semantic cylinders $T,S$.
By DEFINITION these cylinders
are inverse images of individual
complete $\Aut(M)$-pair orbitals
on $(M_a/J)^2$; such pair orbits
are DISJOINT. Hence the cylinders
are equal: $T=S$. This uses only
the actual equivariant G-action,
not the CCO-dependent positive-word
representation of those cylinders.

Conversely suppose $T=S$.
Choose any ACTUAL old diversity
T-edge $xTy$.
The J-class of $x$ is INFINITE
by original visible SAP, since
$J>\Id_a$. Choose $z\in[x]_J$
with $z\ne x$. Since $T$ is
J-saturated, $xTy$ and $xJz$
give $zTy$, hence $yTz$
by symmetry. Because $T$ is
disjoint from $J$, $y$ lies
outside $[x]_J$, so the three
vertices $x,y,z$ are distinct.
Their triangle witnesses a
diversity atom in
$(T\starprod T)\cap J^\circ$,
proving the converse.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Exact STRONG lifting of original quotient relation composition]
\label{lem:typed-quotient-strong-semigroup}
Let $M$ be an original finite-complete homogeneous
binary source with a transitive named real sort $a$
and visible SAP. Let $J>\Id_a$ be an ACTUAL
invariant equivalence on that sort, and
$Q=M_a/J$. For EVERY $\Aut(M)$-invariant
quotient binary relation $R\subseteq Q^2$,
define the ORIGINAL diversity pullback
\[
 \Phi(R)=\{(x,y)\in M_a^2:x\ne y,\
             ([x]_J,[y]_J)\in R\}.
\]
Then for any nonempty invariant $R,S$,
\[
                 \Phi(R)\starprod\Phi(S)
                       =\Phi(R;S).
\tag{QS}\label{eq:typed-quotient-strong-semigroup}
\]
All OLD original $J$-classes are infinite by SAP.
Therefore $\Phi$ is injective on quotient relations,
preserves finite unions/intersections, and sends
every nonempty quotient relation to a nonempty
OLD DIVERSITY palette (also for the quotient
diagonal). The product equality is on DISTINCT
old real endpoints, even when the intermediate
QUOTIENT class equals one endpoint's quotient
class; no old logical identity is inserted
into the STRONG palette. Finite concatenations
satisfy the analogous identity by choosing
ALL formal intermediate OLD representatives
pairwise distinct.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Project any actual three-distinct-point OLD
STRONG $R,S$-path to quotient classes to get
one inclusion. Conversely suppose
$qRrSs$ on the quotient and choose ANY
two DISTINCT old real endpoints $x\in q$,
$z\in s$. Since $J>\Id_a$ and SAP holds,
the ORIGINAL class $r$ is infinite.
Choose $y\in r\setminus\{x,z\}$.
The FULL-preimage definition gives
$x\,\Phi(R)\,y\,\Phi(S)\,z$;
the OLD fine endpoint colour of $x,z$
thus belongs to the STRONG product.
For longer quotient walks, choose each
intermediate original representative
successively outside the finitely many
already chosen points. This proves all
assertions, including quotient-diagonal
returns with OLD distinct endpoints.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original positive external layer at every nonzero full cover]
\label{thm:typed-original-full-cover-layer-word}
Let $M$ be a finite-complete homogeneous
binary source with a transitive named
original real sort $a$, finitely many
symmetric SAME-SORT fine atomic orbitals,
original SAP, and semantic HOMOMORPHIC CCO.
Let $\Id_a<J\prec K$ be a cover in the
FULL original invariant-equivalence lattice.
Suppose $J^\circ$ is the STRONG diversity
endpoint profile of ONE positive ORIGINAL
atomic word on $a$. Then
\[
               K\setminus J
       \quad\text{is the STRONG endpoint profile
                   of ONE positive old atomic word.}
\tag{OWL}\label{eq:typed-original-full-cover-layer-word}
\]
No FULL NR2, distributivity, original exact
theta presentation, typed exact-domain
Helly, or hidden SAP is assumed.
The index-two cover case is allowed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $Q=M_a/J$ and let $C_1,\ldots,C_r$
be ALL complete external $\Aut(M)$-orbitals
on quotient pairs lying in $K/J$.
Each original diversity pullback
$T_i=\Phi(C_i)$ is a positive STRONG word
profile: for one fine atom $t_i\in T_i$,
old SAP and complete J-saturation give
$T_i=J^\circ\starprod\{t_i\}
                        \starprod J^\circ$.
Concatenate the assumed positive word
for $J^\circ$ on both sides of $t_i$.
These complete palettes partition
$U=K\setminus J$. If $r=1$, then
$U=T_1$ and we are done; this includes
EVERY $[K:J]=2$ cover. Thus assume $r\ge2$,
which forces $[K:J]\ne2$.

By the CCO-free full-cover dichotomy
\cref{lem:typed-quotient-full-cover-powers},
a sufficiently high positive power of
$T_1$ is $K^\circ$. Hence $K^\circ$
ALSO has a single positive old word
representation. All hypotheses of
the one-word original reset
\cref{thm:typed-original-full-cover-reset-single-path}
now hold. Choose its COMPLETE external
orbital $M_0=C_k$ with
$M_0;M_0=K/J$ on the QUOTIENT, using
the product identity and the INJECTIVITY of $\Phi$
from \cref{lem:typed-quotient-strong-semigroup}
to translate the strong reset identity.
We henceforth work with actual invariant
relations on $Q$. Their orbitals are all
symmetric (original fine same-sort
atomic orbitals are symmetric and old
homogeneity swaps two real representatives);
thus EVERY invariant quotient binary relation
is symmetric, and represented quotient
products commute. Nonempty such relations
are serial by transitivity of $Q$.

We record two elementary QUOTIENT facts.
First, if nonempty invariant symmetric
serial $P,S\subseteq K/J$ satisfy
$P;S\subseteq\Delta_Q$, then $P,S$
are graphs of the SAME equivariant
involution: all neighbours of any
vertex are forced to be unique by
seriality and the diagonal inclusion,
and symmetry makes the resulting
map involutive. If $P$ is external,
a nonidentity involution would define
a two-point invariant equivalence
between $\Delta_Q$ and $K/J$.
FULL cover would then give
$[K:J]=2$, impossible. Consequently
NO finite nonempty quotient product
of external COMPLETE orbitals lies
in $\Delta_Q$: group its first factor
against the remaining nonempty
symmetric invariant product.

Second, if nonempty invariant
$W\subseteq K/J$ is NOT contained
in $\Delta_Q$, its quotient graph has
connectedness equivalence strictly
above $\Delta_Q$ and inside $K/J$,
hence EQUAL to $K/J$ by FULL cover.
If W has quotient loops, all its
components are aperiodic. Otherwise
a bipartite component would yield an
even-walk invariant equivalence strictly
below $K/J$ and hence, by FULL cover,
equal to $\Delta_Q$; this forces a
two-vertex $K/J$-component, i.e.
$[K:J]=2$, impossible. Every component
is therefore nonbipartite/aperiodic.
The finite number of quotient pair
orbitals gives one uniform threshold:
\[
                       W^{;n}=K/J
                    \quad(n\text{ sufficiently large}).
\tag{OWL-P}\label{eq:typed-old-cover-quotient-powers}
\]

Suppose for a contradiction no two
DISTINCT complete external orbitals
$C,D$ have $C;D=\bar U:=(K/J)\setminus\Delta_Q$.
For each external complete C put
$P_C=M_0;C$. Since
$C\subseteq M_0;M_0$ by reset,
quotient Peirce rotation gives
$P_C\cap M_0\ne\varnothing$.
As $P_C$ is invariant and $M_0$
one COMPLETE orbit, $M_0\subseteq P_C$.
We claim
\[
                          \bigcap_C P_C=M_0.
\tag{OWL-I}\label{eq:typed-old-cover-intersection}
\]
Choose C DISTINCT from $M_0$.
Then $P_C$ contains no quotient
diagonal: a two-edge return via
two disjoint complete symmetric
external orbitals is impossible.
Hence the intersection lies
outside $\Delta_Q$.
If an external complete orbital S
meets every $P_C$, quotient
Peirce yields $C\cap(M_0;S)\ne
\varnothing$ for ALL external C.
By invariance $M_0;S$ therefore
contains the ENTIRE $\bar U$.
If $S\ne M_0$, it cannot contain
the quotient diagonal either;
so $M_0;S=\bar U$, contradicting
our supposition. Thus S=$M_0$,
proving the claim.

Start with one external COMPLETE
$R\ne M_0$, so $R\cap M_0=\varnothing$.
If $R$ met EVERY $P_C$, the
family $\{R\}\cup\{P_C:C\text{ external}\}$
would be pairwise intersecting
(ALL $P_C$ contain $M_0$)
with empty total intersection,
by \eqref{eq:typed-old-cover-intersection}.
Its OLD STRONG diversity pullbacks
are positive word profiles by
\cref{lem:typed-quotient-strong-semigroup},
since $R,M_0,C$ have positive
single-word presentations.
The direct ORIGINAL positive-word
2-Helly follows from CCO+SAP:
put private old paths between two
formal roots, merge duplicate
length-one specified edges,
use pairwise overlapping diversity
profiles to complete every
specified simple cycle homomorphically,
then use SAP
\cref{lem:typed-injectivisation}
to split ALL vertices after one
global CCO completion. Hence
the assumed family cannot exist.
Choose C with $R\cap(M_0;C)=\varnothing$;
Peirce gives $(R;C)\cap M_0=\varnothing$.
The new state $R;C$ remains nonempty,
invariant, OLD-positive on lifting,
and inside $K/J$, but need no
longer be a COMPLETE orbital or
avoid the QUOTIENT diagonal.
Repeat this escape for every new state
$R$ disjoint from $M_0$.

There are FINITELY many invariant
binary quotient relations. Some state
repeats, so we obtain nonempty
invariant $R\subseteq K/J$,
$R\cap M_0=\varnothing$, and a
NONEMPTY product $W$ of external
complete orbitals with
\[
                           R;W=R.
\]
The quotient no-return fact above
makes $W\not\subseteq\Delta_Q$.
The eventual-full-power fact
\eqref{eq:typed-old-cover-quotient-powers}
gives $W^{;n}=K/J$ for large n.
Iterating the state equation gives
$R=R;(K/J)=K/J$, since every
nonempty invariant quotient relation
is serial and is contained in $K/J$.
This contradicts $R\cap M_0=\varnothing$.

Hence some two distinct external
COMPLETE orbitals $C,D$ satisfy
$C;D=\bar U$. Lift via
\cref{lem:typed-quotient-strong-semigroup}:
\[
             \Phi(C)\starprod\Phi(D)
                =\Phi(\bar U)=K\setminus J.
\]
Both factors have a single positive
OLD atomic word, so their concatenation
is ONE positive old word for $U$.
This proves \eqref{eq:typed-original-full-cover-layer-word}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reachable typed relative cover layer]
\label{thm:typed-cover-layer-reachable}
Under the same terminal typed SAP+CCO
hypotheses, let $J$ be a nonzero represented
proper meet-irreducible FULL EXACT same-sort
equivalence on $a$ and $K=J^+$ its cover
in the finite represented exact-equivalence
lattice. Then the STRONG diversity palette
\[
                         U=K\setminus J
\]
is a nonempty reachable typed $a$--$a$
word profile (even when its witnessing
positive words visit other named sorts).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A SHORT original-source proof is now available
independently of typed exact theta geometry.
By \cref{lem:typed-all-invariant-equivalences-raw-word},
the terminal source's FULL NR2 makes every
original invariant equivalence FULL RAW word
representable; the represented exact cover
$J\prec K$ is therefore a FULL invariant cover.
By visible SAP injectivisation, $J^\circ$
has ONE positive original STRONG atomic word.
The direct quotient-semigroup theorem
\cref{thm:typed-original-full-cover-layer-word}
now gives ONE positive word profile exactly
$K\setminus J$, as claimed.
This does not use a separate typed hidden
SAP or mixed-sort private theta construction.

\end{proof}
