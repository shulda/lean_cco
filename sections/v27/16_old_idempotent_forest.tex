\begin{lemma}[OLD boundary coordinate certificate for one deleted idempotent gap]
\label{lem:typed-old-gap-boundary-certificate}
Let \(C\) be a finite
UNARY-FUNCTIONALLY CLOSED
partial terminal typed
hidden source whose
one-generated diagrams
and every ACTUALLY
joined irreducible
two-root source support
have passed the COMPLETE
bounded local test of
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.
Suppose
\[
 b_0 \ \xrightarrow{P_1}\
 b_1\ \xrightarrow{P_2}\
 \cdots\ \xrightarrow{P_m}\ b_m
\]
is one original
source-supported
composable macro
subpath with distinct
formal OLD root
vertices \(b_i\).
Each \(P_i\) is the
NONEMPTY positive
STRONG profile of the
ACTUAL traversed
private branch, and
the original source
exact pair menu on
\((b_{i-1},b_i)\)
is \(D_i\subseteq P_i\).
Assume
\[
       P_1\starprod
           \cdots\starprod P_m=e,
       \qquad e\ne\varnothing,
       \qquad e\starprod e=e
\]
with endpoints of the
same original sort \(a\).
Let
\(E_e=\Id_a\cup e\).

For EVERY represented
proper meet-irreducible
\(J\supseteq E_e\),
there are named unary
term functions
\(\tau_{0,J},\tau_{m,J}\)
on the OLD source
endpoints \(b_0,b_m\)
whose values are their
represented \(J\)-coordinates
and satisfy
\[
             \tau_{0,J}(b_0)
                        =\tau_{m,J}(b_m)
\tag{OGC}\label{eq:typed-old-gap-certificate}
\]
as LITERAL equality of
vertices of the ORIGINAL
partial source \(C\).
This equality survives
in the induced closed
substructure
\(\cl_C\{b_0,b_m\}\).

Consequently, for ANY
homomorphism-embedding
completion of that
retained boundary
substructure, the
chosen original real
representatives of
\(b_0,b_m\) have an
actual pair relation
in the FULL RAW exact
equivalence \(E_e\).
If the representatives
are distinct, their
original fine diversity
atom belongs to the
STRONG profile \(e\).
The same conclusion
holds when \(E_e=\Top_a\),
with the empty family
of \(J\)-equalities
interpreted as the
universal relation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(w\) be the
concatenated ACTUAL
original branch word.
By typed SAP
injectivisation,
the equality-inclusive
RAW relation of \(w\)
is contained in
\(E_e=\Id_a\cup e\).
For any represented
MI \(J\supseteq E_e\),
apply
\cref{lem:typed-return-transport}
to the actual RAW
relations of the macro
factors \(P_i\).
It gives represented
proper transported
kernels
\(J=J_0,J_1,\ldots,
J_m=J\), named quotient
maps \(\pi_i\), and
\[
              \pi_m\circ
                 \cdots\circ\pi_1
                                      =\mathrm{id}_{a/J}
\]
as ACTUAL quotient
functions.

For each original
source-supported pair
\((b_{i-1},b_i)\),
\cref{lem:typed-branch-root-visibility,lem:typed-old-source-branch-equation}
proves the LITERAL OLD
term equation
\[
 \pi_i(\tau_{i-1,J_{i-1}}(b_{i-1}))
       =\tau_{i,J_i}(b_i).
\]
The source check is
essential: these are
not equalities inferred
from arbitrary extra
representatives selected
after a completion.
At each intermediate
root the outgoing and
incoming readings denote
the SAME canonical
represented \(J_i\)-term
of that actual source
vertex, by
\cref{lem:typed-old-seam-telescope}.
Because C is closed under
all named unary functions,
the equations compose
INSIDE C, giving
\[
 (\pi_m\circ\cdots\circ\pi_1)
       (\tau_{0,J}(b_0))
                         =\tau_{m,J}(b_m).
\]
The quotient-function
identity is an identity
of finite named unary
terms, not a
completion-dependent
coincidence. It already
holds in the valid
ONE-generated OLD
diagram on \(b_0\);
this is exactly why the
bounded one-root test
precedes the guarded lift.
Substitution yields
the literal equality
(OGC) in C.

Each side of (OGC) is
a value of a unary term
ON ITS OWN boundary OLD
vertex. Hence both
equal term-value vertices
remain in
\(\cl_C\{b_0,b_m\}\),
regardless of deletion
of all intermediate old
roots. Every hidden
homomorphism preserves
the named unary functions
and this literal
equality.

For any completion and
any chosen actual real
representatives of the
two boundary hidden
vertices, equality of
their \(J\)-coordinates
for ALL represented
MI \(J\supseteq E_e\)
means their relation
belongs to the meet
of these equivalences.
By
\cref{lem:typed-idempotent-raw-exact},
this meet is EXACTLY
the FULL RAW \(E_e\).
If there are no such
proper MIs then the
finite distributive
exact lattice says
\(E_e=\Top_a\), so
the conclusion is
vacuous but correct.
Finally, if the two
representatives are
distinct, their fine
pair atom cannot be
logical identity, and
membership in
\(E_e=\Id_a\cup e\)
places it in \(e\).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Typed compressed guarded macro path]
\label{lem:typed-factor-path}
Work in the binary terminal guarded-lift setting after the bounded
one-/two-root source tests of \cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.
Let \(P_1\cdots P_n\), with \(n\ge1\), be a source-supported positive
macro path with pairwise distinct formal OLD root vertices, as obtained from the
complementary path of a specified simple cycle. Put
\(h=3|S_{\rm typ}|\).

There is a compression in the typed hidden language with at most \(2^h\) retained original macro letters
and at most \(2^{h+1}\) OLD root vertices. Their induced unary closure
retains the complete source support of each retained letter and the
literal boundary certificates of all deleted idempotent intervals.
Every hidden completion of this retained source permits reconstruction
of the original macro path after choosing distinct real representatives
of the retained roots by \cref{lem:typed-real-sort-splitting}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Apply the factorisation-forest theorem~\cite{Kufleitner} to the word in
\(S_{\rm typ}\), with height at most \(h\).
Every contiguous subword is nonzero by
\cref{lem:typed-composable-products-nonzero}. Thus any node with at least
three children has a common \emph{nonzero} idempotent value \(e\).
Retain both children at binary nodes. At a node with at least three
children, retain the first and last children and record the whole
middle interval as one deleted gap; do not recurse inside that gap.
The pruned tree has at most \(2^h\) leaves. Let \(B\) be the endpoints
of their retained original macro letters. Then \(|B|\le 2^{h+1}\).
Both endpoints of each retained subtree persist, so \(B\) also contains
the two boundary roots of every deleted gap and the endpoints of the
original path. Deleted gaps may separate retained letters; the
endpoint count is therefore twice the leaf count, not leaf count plus one.

For each deleted gap the product of its macro profiles is \(e\).
Its roots are distinct OLD source vertices, its traversed branches are
positive, and every original pair menu is nonempty and contained in
the corresponding branch profile, by
\cref{lem:typed-private-theta-cycle} and the guarded-lift source tests.
These are precisely the hypotheses of
\cref{lem:typed-old-gap-boundary-certificate}. That lemma gives literal
OLD endpoint term equalities for every represented proper MI
\(J\supseteq E_e=\Id\cup e\). They survive in
\(\cl_C(B)\), and every completion of this retained source forces the
boundary pair into the full RAW exact equivalence \(E_e\).
This includes \(E_e=\Top\), when the family of equalities is empty.
The certificate supplies this entire source-to-boundary implication;
no new equation is inferred from coincidences in a completion.

The complete OLD support of each retained macro letter is generated
by its two endpoints, by
\cref{lem:typed-one-source-support-per-guarded-pair}, and is contained
in \(\cl_C(B)\). Consequently
\cref{lem:typed-two-root-complete-reflection} preserves its original
pair menu in any hypothetical completion. The retained source has
uniformly bounded size, since unary admissibility and
\cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound} give
\( |\cl_C(B)|\le m_{\rm typ}2^{h+1}\).

Given such a completion, choose pairwise distinct real representatives
of all retained roots using \cref{lem:typed-real-sort-splitting} and
take their complete finite visible induced structure as the common
base. Each retained letter has its original endpoint profile. At a
deleted gap, membership in \(E_e\) now gives a diversity atom in \(e\),
\emph{because the boundary representatives are distinct}.
Thus \cref{lem:typed-idempotent-reinsert} applies to the entire original
word of that gap. Finally
\cref{lem:typed-simultaneous-word-insertion} realises all retained
branch words and deleted whole words over the same complete base,
with mutually disjoint new interiors. They partition the original
path, so this reconstructs it with its original endpoints and labels.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[No zero-valued composable positive typed macro interval]
\label{lem:typed-composable-products-nonzero}
In an actual homogeneous
named-sorted complete binary
SAP source, let
\(P\subseteq M_a\times M_b\)
and \(Q\subseteq M_b\times M_c\)
be NONEMPTY invariant
DIVERSITY pair palettes
of the indicated types.
Then their genuine STRONG
three-distinct-vertex
product is nonempty:
\[
                    P\starprod Q\ne\varnothing .
\]
Consequently the product of
EVERY nonempty contiguous
subword of a composable
positive typed macro word
is nonzero. In the typed
semigroup
\(S_{\rm typ}=\{0\}
 \sqcup\bigsqcup_{a,b}\Rch^{ab}\),
the adjoined zero can occur
on NONCOMPOSABLE typed inputs,
but NEVER at a node of a
factorisation forest built on
one actual composable positive
macro path.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose any actual point \(y\)
of the common middle sort
\(b\). By transitivity of
the original named sorts,
nonemptiness and invariance
make both \(P\) and \(Q\)
serial/surjective. Thus choose
\(xP y\) and \(yQ z\).
The two old pairs are
complete finite structures
with common induced
one-point substructure
\(\{y\}\). Strongly amalgamate
them over \(\{y\}\), so their
outside points remain
DISTINCT even if their
original representatives
happened to coincide.
The resulting three old
real vertices \(x,y,z\)
are pairwise distinct
(the two input pair
relations are diversity),
and their complete
triangle has a genuine
old diversity atom
\(\col(x,z)\in
 P\starprod Q\).
This proves nonemptiness.
Induction on the length of
a composable positive word
proves the interval
assertion, using that the
strong product stays an
actual nonempty invariant
palette of its endpoint
sort pair. A zero value
can therefore arise only
when the semigroup
multiplication is applied
to mismatched endpoint
sorts, which never occurs
in the given macro word.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Partition and simultaneous reconstruction of a pruned OLD cycle]
\label{lem:typed-pruned-cycle-reconstruction}
Consider a SPECIFIED SIMPLE cycle
\(Z\) of a binary guarded lift which
is not confined to one private theta.
Choose one original complete atomic
theta branch as a fixed anchor;
the complementary side contracts to
a typed macro word
\(P_1\cdots P_n\) with ACTUAL OLD
source root witnesses
\(v_0,\ldots,v_n\), pairwise distinct
along the complementary path.
For each macro letter \(P_i\),
remember the ONE ORIGINAL selected
positive atomic branch word \(w_i\)
traversed by \(Z\); thus
\(\Prof(w_i)=P_i\).

Apply a factorisation forest to
\(P_1\cdots P_n\).
By
\cref{lem:typed-composable-products-nonzero},
EVERY subtree value of this
composable positive word
is NONZERO; in particular
no unbounded-arity node
can have the adjoined
zero as its idempotent.
Recursively keep
both children of every binary node,
and the first and last children of
every node with at least three
children of one idempotent value.
When middle children are discarded,
STOP recursion on those children,
and record the corresponding whole
contiguous original interval word
with its two OLD boundary roots.

Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The retained original macro
letters and the maximal recorded
deleted intervals form a DISJOINT
ordered partition of the original
complementary macro path. The two
boundary roots of every deleted
interval occur among endpoints of
the retained macro letters. No
boundary vertex is introduced
after taking an OLD source induced
substructure.
\item Every maximal deleted
interval has nonzero STRONG
idempotent product \(e\), so its
whole ORIGINAL concatenated
atomic branch word has the
strong endpoint profile \(e\).
\item Let \(A\) be ANY finite
COMPLETE old visible structure
containing pairwise distinct
representatives of ALL retained
OLD source root vertices, with
the fine atomic pair on each
retained macro side lying in its
ORIGINAL \(P_i\), and with the
boundary pair of each deleted
interval of value \(e\) lying in
\(E_e=\Id\cup e\).
ALSO assume that the fine atom
of the two anchor endpoints
belongs to the STRONG endpoint
profile of the EXACT fixed
original anchor branch word.
Then the cycle \(Z\) can be
recreated as a specified
SIMPLE positive atomic cycle
inside one finite complete old
visible extension of \(A\),
provided each root pair of \(Z\)
is represented by the same
chosen root points in \(A\).

Only the last implication uses
visible SAP; it imposes NO
condition on newly chosen fine
cross colours between interiors
of distinct inserted words.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every node of the forest
represents a contiguous interval
of the original macro letters,
with its endpoint root positions
among \(v_0,\ldots,v_n\).
Prove (i) by induction down the
tree. At a leaf retain its one
original macro letter. At a
binary node concatenate the two
child partitions at their SHARED
OLD boundary root. At a node
with \(k\ge3\) idempotent
children, partition its interval
into the recursively pruned
FIRST child, the ONE original
contiguous middle interval
consisting of children
\(2,\ldots,k-1\), and the
recursively pruned LAST child.
Do NOT recurse into the deleted
middle interval: this prevents
overlapping deletion records.
Its two old boundary root
positions are respectively
the final root of child one
and the initial root of child
\(k\). They persist under
recursive pruning because
BOTH endpoints of every
retained subtree persist.
All recorded intervals are
therefore pairwise disjoint
and, together with retained
leaves, cover every original
macro edge EXACTLY ONCE.

At such a high-arity node all
children have one common nonzero
idempotent value \(e\).
The product of any positive
number of these values is
again \(e\). In particular
the deleted middle interval
has STRONG profile \(e\).
Associativity and the
definition of \(P_i\) as the
profile of the EXACT original
atomic word \(w_i\) show that
the concatenation of its
original \(w_i\)'s has that
same STRONG endpoint profile.
This proves (ii).

For (iii), membership in
\(E_e\) and distinctness
of the boundary root points
imply their actual fine
DIVERSITY atom belongs to
\(e\). Therefore the entire
original atomic word of
each deleted interval has
a simple-path realisation
between that specified
pair. The retained letters
have the same property by
the assumed membership of
their fine endpoint atoms
in \(P_i\). The ORIGINAL fixed anchor
branch has an admissible
endpoint atom by the
EXPLICIT last hypothesis
of (iii). In the guarded
source application this
hypothesis follows because
its two OLD endpoints and
their complete original
joined two-root support
belong to the retained
induced functionally
closed OLD substructure;
their exact menu is
reflected in any
hypothetical completion
by
\cref{lem:typed-two-root-complete-reflection}.
Apply
\cref{lem:typed-simultaneous-word-insertion}
ONCE to the finite list
consisting of all these
atomic words and the
anchor branch, over the
SAME complete old base
\(A\). It preserves every
old root pair atom and
gives all new interiors
pairwise disjoint.

The original macro root
positions of the simple
cycle were pairwise
distinct; their retained
representatives in \(A\)
are pairwise distinct by
hypothesis. The erased
macro roots reappear only
as NEW internal positions
of the whole deleted words,
and no such old position is
used in another retained
segment, by (i).
Consequently the
concatenated inserted words
and the fixed anchor form
one SIMPLE atomic cycle
with EXACTLY the old
specified sequential edge
labels. This proves (iii).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Uniformly bounded ORIGINAL hidden anchor support]

Work in the BINARY guarded-lift
setting after the bounded
one-/two-root OLD source tests
of \cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.

\(h=3s\), and let
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\) be the
one-generated hidden
unary-closure bound of
\cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}.
For any specified simple
bad cycle in the guarded
visible network, choose one
original private atomic
branch as a fixed anchor
and contract the complementary
private branch traversals
to a typed macro path.
After the idempotent-node
pruning used in
\cref{lem:typed-factor-path},
there is a set \(B\) of
ACTUAL OLD hidden source
vertices such that:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \(B\) contains the
two original root positions
of the fixed anchor branch,
the endpoints of every
retained macro side, and
both boundary roots of
every deleted idempotent

unary term values of

\(\cl_C(B)\).
\item Every retained
macro-side spectrum is
supported inside the

diagram on \(\cl_C(B)\),
including its complete
irreducible old pair

the same endpoint
represented-MI term

LITERALLY in that

\item One may choose
\[
 \bigl|\cl_C(B)\bigr|
   \le m_{\mathrm{typ}}
               (2^{h+3}+4).
\tag{OAB}\label{eq:typed-forest-old-anchor-bound}
\]
No term-value vertex newly
created by a subsequent
visible completion is included
among these OLD anchors.
\end{enumerate}

The claim concerns this
retained OLD hidden support
and its literal partial
relations; it does NOT
assert that this partial
support is itself a
completed hidden member.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A factorisation forest over
the finite typed profile
semigroup has height at most
\(h=3s\). At a node with
at least three idempotent
children retain only its
first and last children,
remembering the deleted
middle interval and its
two boundary OLD roots.
At binary nodes retain both
children. The pruned tree is
binary with height at most
\(h\), and therefore has at
most \(2^h\) retained leaves
and \(2^{h+1}-1\) nodes.
Every retained leaf is an
original macro side with at
most TWO old source endpoint
vertices. Every deleted
idempotent interval is
attached to one pruned
internal node and needs
at most TWO boundary old
root positions, already
among adjacent retained
child endpoints. Add the
two roots of the unpruned
anchor branch. Thus the
number of OLD generating

conservatively by
\(2^{h+3}+4\).

By
\cref{lem:typed-one-source-support-per-guarded-pair},
the entire complete OLD
irreducible support behind
each retained binary
macro side is generated
by its TWO endpoints;
there are no additional
old generators hidden
behind an independent
choice of representatives.

\cref{lem:typed-private-theta-cycle},
each macro side indeed
has these ACTUAL OLD
endpoints. The full
OLD boundary-certificate
lemma
\cref{lem:typed-old-gap-boundary-certificate}
(which imports
\cref{lem:typed-old-source-branch-equation,lem:typed-old-seam-telescope})
shows that every represented
quotient identity needed at

to a LITERAL equality of
named unary terms of its
two boundary OLD roots.
All such term values
belong to their OLD
unary closures and remain
identical on restriction
to the induced diagram.
There are no arbitrary
intermediate real
representatives to retain.

Finally every one-generated
unary closure has at most
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\)
vertices, and the closure
of a finite set under
UNARY functions is the
union of its
one-generated closures.
Multiplying the
generator count by
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\)
gives (OAB). All relations
are taken by induced
restriction from the
original OLD partial
source; no new relation
is filled in before a
hypothetical completion.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Typed bounded hidden obstructions]
\label{cor:typed-bounded-hidden}
There is a constant \(N_{\mathrm{typ}}\), depending only on the
finite terminal typed hidden language, such that every
NON-COMPLETABLE UNARY-ADMISSIBLE
(\cref{def:typed-unary-admissible-source})
finite functionally closed PARTIAL transition-enriched
hidden structure contains a non-completable induced
functionally closed substructure on at most
\(N_{\mathrm{typ}}\) vertices.

The qualifier is ESSENTIAL: the assertion is FALSE for
arbitrary total-unary partial sources, which may contain
arbitrarily long malformed cycles of named mutually
inverse quotient-transition functions.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By UNARY-ADMISSIBILITY every one-generated induced
diagram of \(C\) is already an actual completed hidden
member and hence has size at most \(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\)
by \cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}. In particular
ALL intended unary function and universal
quotient-transition identities hold in \(C\);
there is NO malformed one-generated unary diagram
to dispose of by an unjustified uniform-closure claim.
Any malformed atomic two-root, semantic-cylinder,
old-tuple or joined functional-support pattern has
a bounded witness because its CLOSED OLD support
is generated by at most two roots (or the fixed
maximum relation arity), hence has size uniformly
bounded by a corresponding multiple of \(m_{\mathrm{typ}}\).
Assume those bounded multi-root defects have been
excluded and apply \cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}.  If two imposed
profiles are disjoint, the hidden data supporting that conflict already form
a bounded non-completable substructure, and we are done.  Otherwise the
construction reaches \(G_{\mathrm{typ}}(C)\), and by
\cref{lem:typed-guarded-lift-equivalence} non-completion of the hidden
fragment implies non-completion of this guarded visible lift.  In the BINARY case the guarded visible network contains
only genuine old DIVERSITY atomic edges. If several length-one
private branches assign an atom to the SAME formal root pair,
their labels coincide because the already checked nonempty exact
root menu lies in each singleton profile; merge those duplicate
edges, rather than treating them as artificial parallel edges.
Its canonical quotient root-signature equations are CONSEQUENCES
of those positive pair menus, not additional old equality edges.
The terminal original visible source has SAP, so
\cref{lem:typed-strong-cycle-completion} upgrades semantic
HOMOMORPHIC CCO to an INJECTIVE completion criterion.
As the visible guarded diagram has NO injective completion,
SOME specified simple cycle has no colour-preserving
homomorphism, hence is genuinely non-completable.
In the support-normal finite-arity case the corresponding
conclusion still requires the separately stipulated
frozen-higher-support completion interface: binary SAP
injectivisation alone does NOT preserve higher-arity factors.

If the cycle lies inside one fixed private template, its hidden anchor
projection is bounded.  Otherwise \cref{lem:typed-private-theta-cycle}
proves that every maximal traversal of a
private template has two ACTUAL OLD hidden
source endpoint witnesses. Contract it
to its typed reachable macro profile,
retaining those two old root anchors. In
\cref{con:typed-guarded-lift}, each OLD hidden vertex has one designated
witness; since the specified visible cycle is simple, its complementary
macro path therefore has pairwise distinct OLD root vertices.
Apply \cref{lem:typed-factor-path}
to one complementary macro path.  Retain its
bounded set of macro vertices, the old hidden anchors generating their root
signatures, and, for every deleted idempotent interval and every represented
meet-irreducible above its idempotent value, the full quotient-transition
closure prescribed in the proof of that lemma.  Close under all unary
functions.  Because the transition language is
finite, the complete joined
two-root signature and its
private exact theta network
have the UNIFORM vertex
bound from
\cref{lem:typed-two-root-uniform-size}.
The explicit ORIGINAL
support-count lemma
\cref{lem:typed-forest-old-anchor-bound}
now supplies a uniformly
bounded induced
functionally closed OLD
hidden source, with a
binary bound
\(m_{\mathrm{typ}}
(2^{3|S_{\rm typ}|+3}+4)\)
for the long-cycle
compression branch.
Every retained fine pair
spectrum comes from one
old irreducible pair support,
and every deleted-interval
quotient equation remains
a literal OLD term equality;
no additional original
generators or arbitrary
completion-only
representatives are retained.
Enlarge the fixed bound
if necessary for the
finite list of malformed
local atomic/term diagrams
and single-template cycles,
and call the resulting
constant \(N_{\mathrm{typ}}\).
It is independent of the
original cycle length
and of the finite source
\(C\).

Suppose this retained substructure had a hidden completion.  Apply
\cref{lem:typed-real-sort-splitting} to obtain pairwise distinct designated
real roots and representatives while preserving every displayed hidden
function, transition, semantic-cylinder relation and specified old real
relation.  The retained root signatures also contain all old anchors defining
each retained exact-domain theta side, so after splitting the corresponding
endpoint colour still lies in every defining branch profile.  Reinsert each
retained private template.  At every deleted idempotent interval
of value \(e\), the LITERAL
OLD source equality certificate
\cref{lem:typed-old-gap-boundary-certificate}
survives in the induced
retained unary-closed substructure,
forcing its two boundary
root occurrences to have equal
\(J\)-coordinates for EVERY
represented meet-irreducible
\(J\supseteq E_e\) in ANY
hypothetical hidden completion. By \cref{lem:typed-idempotent-raw-exact},
the same coordinate equalities, taken
over every represented meet-irreducible
above the FULL RAW exact envelope,
give their relation in \(E_e=\Id\cup e\),
\emph{not yet} a diversity colour in \(e\).
The exact-envelope hypothesis is
essential here: arbitrary nonexact
invariant equivalences need not equal
the meet of represented exact
meet-irreducibles above them.
The roots are distinct formal vertices of the
original simple cycle. The preceding
\cref{lem:typed-real-sort-splitting}
keeps their actual representatives distinct,
so their old pair colour lies in \(e\).
Apply \cref{lem:typed-idempotent-reinsert}
to each deleted interval's ENTIRE
original atomic word, not to a
newly invented factor word.
The retained branches are likewise
the EXACT original positive atomic
words with their original endpoint
profile memberships.
