\begin{lemma}[Commutation of same-sort invariant binary relations]
\label{lem:typed-symmetric-orbital-commutation}
Let \(a\) be one named original
transitive sort of a homogeneous
complete SYMMETRIC binary source.
For any two invariant (equivalently,
unions of old named atomic orbitals)
relations \(P,Q\subseteq M_a^2\),
their equality-inclusive RAW
relational products commute:
\[
                    P;Q=Q;P.
\]
No assertion is made here about
relations with different source
and target sorts.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take \(xP yQ z\).
On the old two-point induced
substructure \(\{x,z\}\), the map
interchanging \(x,z\) is an
isomorphism, because every
same-sort atomic orbital is
symmetric. By homogeneity it
extends to an automorphism
\(g\) of the actual source.
Then \(xQ g(y)Pz\), since
\(g(z)=x\), \(g(x)=z\),
and both \(P,Q\) are invariant
and symmetric. Thus
\(P;Q\subseteq Q;P\);
interchanging \(P,Q\)
gives the reverse inclusion.
The argument includes
all equality cases, because
the atomic diagonal and
the symmetric invariant
relations are fixed by \(g\).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Modularity of the original same-sort invariant equivalence lattice]
\label{lem:typed-invariant-equivalence-modularity}
Let $a$ be a transitive original named real sort
of a homogeneous COMPLETE binary source, whose
same-sort ORIGINAL atomic orbitals are symmetric.
The FULL invariant-equivalence lattice on $a$
is MODULAR, without assuming SAP, CCO,
terminal NR2, RAW exactness, or distributivity.
Precisely, for original invariant equivalences
$E,F,G$ with $E\le G$,
\[
             (E\vee F)\cap G
                    =E\vee(F\cap G).
\tag{MOD}\label{eq:typed-invariant-modularity}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every original invariant same-sort binary relation
is a union of symmetric old atomic orbitals, hence
symmetric. Its represented product with another
invariant relation is again invariant and symmetric.
Taking converses therefore shows that all invariant
same-sort relations COMMUTE under ACTUAL RAW
relational composition, as in
\cref{lem:typed-symmetric-orbital-commutation}.
In particular for invariant equivalences
$E,F$, the product $E;F$ is an equivalence
and equals their join $E\vee F$.
Fix $E\le G$.  Take actual real points
$x,y$ with
$x\,(E\vee F)\,y$ and $xGy$.
Choose $z$ with $xEzFy$.
Since $E\le G$, we have $xGz$;
together with $xGy$, transitivity
gives $zGy$. Hence
$z(F\cap G)y$ and
\[
                   x\bigl(E;(F\cap G)\bigr)y .
\]
The latter product is an equivalence
and equals $E\vee(F\cap G)$.
This proves one inclusion of
\eqref{eq:typed-invariant-modularity}.
The reverse follows immediately since
$E\subseteq G$ and $F\cap G\subseteq G$,
while both are contained in $E\vee F$.
No original CCO or SAP entered.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An original exact $M_3$ forces failure of homomorphic CCO]
\label{lem:typed-exact-m3-cco-obstruction}
Suppose a homogeneous COMPLETE binary
same-sort symmetric original source has
three invariant equivalences
$E_1,E_2,E_3$ generating an actual
sublattice $M_3$:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 E_i\cap E_j=L<E_i\quad(i\ne j),\\
 E_i\vee E_j=H\quad(i\ne j),
 \qquad L<H .
 \end{gathered}
\]
Assume each $E_i$ is the ACTUAL represented
RAW endpoint relation of at least one
finite original atomic word, including
true equality on the diagonal.
Then the original source does NOT have
semantic HOMOMORPHIC simple-cycle CCO.
No SAP or NR2 is needed for this implication.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual $x,y$ with
$xE_1y$ but $\neg xLy$, possible
since $L<E_1$.  Put
\[
 A=E_1[x],\quad
 B=E_2[x],\quad
 C=E_3[y].
\]
The pairwise intersections are NONEMPTY:
$x\in A\cap B$,
$y\in A\cap C$, and
$B\cap C\ne\varnothing$ because
$xE_1y$, $E_1\le H=E_2\vee E_3
=E_2;E_3$ (the last identity uses
original same-sort symmetry and RAW
commutation).  On the other hand,
\[
           A\cap B=L[x],
           \qquad A\cap C=L[y],
\]
and $L[x]\cap L[y]=\varnothing$.
Thus $A\cap B\cap C=\varnothing$.

Choose one ACTUAL old atomic word
representing each $E_i$.  Construct
a finite specified original PARTIAL
atomic graph with two actual marked
centres having the COMPLETE old
atomic pair type of $(x,y)$
and one new formal old root $z$.
Attach PRIVATE old atomic word paths
from $z$ to the centre $x$
representing $E_1$ and $E_2$,
and from $z$ to the centre $y$
representing $E_3$; keep all
three path interiors disjoint,
also from the two marked centres.
Delete genuine zero-length
equality factors (the represented
$E_i>L$ are nonidentity, so
each word has a positive path).
If two direct length-one paths
coincide, merge duplicate old
specified edges; their singleton
atom would have to agree because
the corresponding endpoint classes
meet, and in fact no
equality-inclusive nonidentity
equivalence is represented by
a single diversity atom.

Every specified SIMPLE cycle
uses exactly TWO distinct private
root-to-centre paths, with either
an EMPTY route at the common
centre or the specified old
$x$--$y$ edge between distinct
centres. Its two RAW endpoint
requirements have a common
ACTUAL realization, by the
three verified pairwise
intersections of $A,B,C$.
Align the complete old centre
pair by old binary homogeneity
and realize the private word
walks independently, allowing
nonadjacent path vertices to
collapse under a homomorphism.
Thus EVERY specified simple cycle
has an original homomorphism.

A global homomorphism of the
entire partial network would
map the two marked centres to
the original fine pair type
of $x,y$, which old homogeneity
moves back to the ACTUAL
$x,y$. The image of $z$ would
then lie simultaneously in
$E_1[x]\cap E_2[x]\cap E_3[y]$,
which is EMPTY. Hence the
network has NO global original
homomorphism. This contradicts
semantic homomorphic CCO.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A finite modular $M_3$ diagnostic for non-CCO]
\label{cor:typed-terminal-nondistributive-noncco}
Let $M$ be a homogeneous finite-complete
binary symmetric-on-each-sort source with
NO relative-index-two interval in the FULL
invariant-equivalence lattice on one original
transitive sort $a$; visible SAP is NOT assumed.

on $a$ is NONDISTRIBUTIVE, then the
original source does NOT have semantic
homomorphic simple-cycle CCO.
In fact three nonidentity original
invariant equivalences in an $M_3$
sublattice yield an explicit finite
bad partial atomic network built from
their ACTUAL RAW atomic word presentations.
No converse is claimed:
a DISTRIBUTIVE invariant lattice
alone does not imply CCO.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:typed-invariant-equivalence-modularity}
the full invariant-equivalence lattice
is MODULAR without CCO.  A finite
modular nondistributive lattice
contains a sublattice $M_3$ (the
standard finite forbidden-sublattice
criterion: modularity excludes $N_5$).
By the standalone CCO- and SAP-FREE
\cref{lem:typed-all-invariant-equivalences-raw-word},
EVERY original invariant equivalence
on $a$ is represented by ONE ACTUAL
equality-inclusive old atomic word.
This uses only symmetry, homogeneity,
finite old orbitals and the absence
of full invariant relative index two;
no STRONG diversity interpretation
or original SAP is needed.
Apply
\cref{lem:typed-exact-m3-cco-obstruction}
to the three $M_3$ elements.
It gives a specified old simple-cycle
CCO obstruction, as asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[CCO-free quotient-orbital power dichotomy at a full invariant cover]
\label{lem:typed-quotient-full-cover-powers}
Let $M$ be a homogeneous finite-complete binary structure
with finitely many named transitive original real sorts,
symmetric old same-sort orbitals, and visible SAP.
Fix an original sort $a$ and let
$\Id_a<J\prec K$ be a cover in the FULL original
invariant-equivalence lattice, with $J>\Id_a$.
Let $T\subseteq K\setminus J$ be the full OLD diversity
preimage of ONE non-diagonal complete $\Aut(M)$-orbital
on $(M_a/J)^2$. Exactly one of the following holds:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $[K:J]=2$, and for EVERY $n\ge1$ the actual
STRONG diversity powers alternate:
$T^{\starprod n}=T$ for odd $n$ and
$T^{\starprod n}=J^\circ$ for even $n$;
\item $[K:J]\ne2$, and there is $N_T$ such that
\[
                   T^{\starprod n}=K^\circ
                   \quad\text{for every }n\ge N_T.
\tag{QPP}\label{eq:typed-quotient-full-cover-powers}
\]
\end{enumerate}
Thus local exclusion of THIS index-two cover suffices;
no global NR2 or semantic CCO, exact-ball Helly,
distributivity, RAW exactness, or mixed-sort
quotient transport is needed for (ii).
The FULL-cover condition is essential.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $Q=M_a/J$, with quotient action of
$G=\Aut(M)$, and let $\Theta$ be the non-diagonal
complete $G$-orbital whose full preimage is $T$.
Since old same-sort atomic orbitals are symmetric,
$\Theta$ is a symmetric irreflexive graph relation
on $Q$. Its connectedness equivalence $\sim$ is
$G$-invariant, and its inverse image under
$M_a\to Q$ is an ORIGINAL invariant equivalence
$H$ satisfying $J\le H\le K$.
Because $T$ is nonempty and external to $J$, we
have $H>J$. The FULL invariant cover $J\prec K$
therefore gives $H=K$. Thus the connected
components of the $\Theta$-graph are EXACTLY
the actual $K/J$-classes.

If a component is bipartite, transitivity of
the original named sort (and hence of $Q$)
makes EVERY component bipartite. The relation
of admitting an even $\Theta$-walk is then
a $G$-invariant equivalence on $Q$ splitting
each component into exactly TWO nonempty
parity classes. Its inverse image on $M_a$
is an ORIGINAL invariant equivalence $E$
with $J\le E<K$ and $[K:E]=2$.
Because $J\prec K$ is a FULL invariant cover,
necessarily $E=J$. Thus each parity class
contains exactly ONE quotient $J$-vertex,
and each connected component has exactly
TWO quotient vertices: $[K:J]=2$.
Conversely, if $[K:J]=2$, every component
consists of two vertices with their unique
external edge, so it is bipartite.
In that case the quotient $\Theta$-walks
alternate: for odd length the endpoint
relation is exactly $\Theta$, and for
positive even length it is the quotient
diagonal on each $J$-class. Since every
old $J$-class is infinite by SAP, choosing
distinct representatives at every formal
position lifts these quotient walks to
injective OLD STRONG paths. Their exact
old endpoint menus are respectively
$T$ and $J^\circ$, proving (i).

Hence for case (ii), $[K:J]\ne2$, no
component can be bipartite. Every
connected component is NONBIPARTITE.

For any two quotient vertices $q,r$ in one
connected nonbipartite component, there is a
$\Theta$-walk of EVERY sufficiently large length
from $q$ to $r$: concatenate a route to an odd
cycle, an odd detour when parity must be changed,
and two-edge backtracks. This argument includes
$q=r$ and TRUE even and odd closed walks; no
synthetic identity edge is introduced.
There are only FINITELY many $G$-orbitals on
$Q^2$, because $(M_a)^2$ has finitely many
old fine atomic pair orbitals and its surjective
projection to $Q^2$ is $G$-equivariant.
Choose one witness pair from each quotient
orbital contained in $K/J$; by equivariance
its walk threshold applies to EVERY pair of
that orbital. Taking the maximum of finitely
many thresholds gives one $N_T$ such that
\[
                 \Theta^{;n}=K/J
                 \qquad(n\ge N_T)
\]
as actual quotient binary relations, INCLUDING
the true quotient diagonal.

Fix now ANY two DISTINCT old real points
$x,y\in M_a$ with $xKy$, and any $n\ge N_T$.
Choose a quotient walk
$[x]_J=q_0\,\Theta\,q_1\,\Theta\cdots
        \Theta\,q_n=[y]_J$.
Every ORIGINAL $J$-class is INFINITE by SAP:
choose a proper $J$-neighbour and strongly
amalgamate arbitrarily many copies of the
complete two-point extension over its anchor;
homogeneity and sort transitivity cover all
classes. Hence select pairwise DISTINCT old
representatives $x_1\in q_1,\ldots,
x_{n-1}\in q_{n-1}$, disjoint from the fixed
$x_0=x$ and $x_n=y$, even when a quotient
vertex occurs more than once along the walk.
By the definition of COMPLETE cylinder as
the FULL PREIMAGE of $\Theta$, each adjacent
old real pair $(x_i,x_{i+1})$ belongs to $T$.
The lifted old walk is fully injective, so
the fine diversity colour of $(x,y)$ belongs
to the STRONG product $T^{\starprod n}$.
This proves $K^\circ\subseteq
T^{\starprod n}$ for all $n\ge N_T$.
The reverse inclusion follows from
$T\subseteq K$ and transitivity of the
actual ORIGINAL equivalence $K$.

In particular, a quotient walk MAY return to
its initial $J$-class: different old real
representatives are chosen for different formal
positions. This is why $J>\Id_a$ matters,
and why a quotient diagonal walk is not
confused with an OLD STRONG identity edge.
The argument for (ii), together with the
bipartite alternative already proved,
establishes the claimed dichotomy.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Typed relative pure powers at a nonzero exact cut]
\label{lem:typed-relative-pure-power}
Work in an actual finite-named-sorted, homogeneous, symmetric
on each sort, SAP and semantic homomorphic-CCO
\emph{terminal} source. Fix one original named sort $a$,
a \emph{nonzero} represented proper meet-irreducible
full exact equivalence $J$ on $a$, and its unique
represented exact cover $K=J^+$. Let
$T\subseteq K\setminus J$ be one complete
$J$-semantic same-sort cylinder. Then for some
$N_T$, in the guarded STRONG typed diversity product,
\[
                         T^{\starprod n}=K^\circ
                   \qquad(n\ge N_T).
 \tag{TRP}\label{eq:typed-relative-pure-power}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:typed-all-invariant-equivalences-raw-word},
the terminal source's FULL original NR2 hypothesis
already makes EVERY original invariant same-sort
equivalence an ACTUAL represented FULL RAW exact
relation, without invoking CCO or distributivity.
Thus the represented exact cover $J\prec K$
is also a cover in the FULL invariant lattice.
The terminal FULL NR2 hypothesis in particular
excludes the LOCAL index-two cover $[K:J]=2$.
The complete $J$-semantic cylinder is precisely
the FULL old diversity preimage of one
non-diagonal quotient $\Aut(M)$-orbital on
$(M_a/J)^2$, by its definition and
\cref{lem:typed-cylinder-realisation}.
Therefore \cref{lem:typed-quotient-full-cover-powers}
applies verbatim and proves (TRP).

In particular NO use of terminal RAW exactness is
needed INSIDE the quotient-graph power argument:
RAW exactness is used here only to identify the
represented exact cover with a FULL invariant
cover. Mixed original-sort private witnesses,
if any, are irrelevant to this same-sort
quotient-graph proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original one-word-path relative reset at a full cover]
\label{thm:typed-original-full-cover-reset-single-path}

symmetric-on-one-original-transitive-sort $a$
binary source with original visible SAP and
semantic HOMOMORPHIC simple-cycle CCO.
Let $\Id_a<J\prec K$ be a cover in the FULL
original invariant-equivalence lattice,
with $J>\Id_a$ and $[K:J]\ne2$.
Suppose the old diversity palettes
$J^\circ$ and $K^\circ$ EACH have one
genuinely positive original atomic WORD
with exactly that STRONG endpoint profile.
Then there is ONE COMPLETE non-diagonal
$J$-semantic same-sort quotient orbital
$\mathfrak m\subseteq K\setminus J$ with
\[
 \mathfrak m\starprod\mathfrak m=K^\circ,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak m;\mathfrak m=K
 \quad\text{(ACTUAL equality-inclusive RAW).}
 \tag{OWR}\label{eq:typed-original-full-cover-reset}
\]
The proof uses ONE private OLD positive word
per graph/spoke edge, NOT private exact theta
presentations, typed Helly, distributivity,
a mixed-sort quotient groupoid or a hidden
SAP assumption. It does use original CCO
for ONE global homomorphic completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $T_1,\ldots,T_r$ be all COMPLETE non-diagonal
$\Aut(M)$-quotient pair orbitals on $(M_a/J)^2$
inside $K/J$, pulled back to OLD pair palettes.
These pairwise disjoint menus partition
$K\setminus J$. Each is the COMPLETE
$J$-bi-saturation of an old diversity
atomic colour $t_i\in T_i$. Thus visible
SAP and the RAW/STRONG identity
\cref{lem:typed-raw-strong-saturation}
give
\[
 T_i=(J;\{t_i\};J)^\circ
       =J^\circ\starprod\{t_i\}
                       \starprod J^\circ .
\]
The assumed positive word for $J^\circ$
therefore yields ONE positive atomic
word $w_i$ with STRONG profile EXACTLY $T_i$;
write $w_K$ for the positive word with
profile EXACTLY $K^\circ$.
The CCO-free full-cover graph theorem
\cref{lem:typed-quotient-full-cover-powers}
and $[K:J]\ne2$ give a common $N$
such that $T_i^{\starprod n}=K^\circ$
for EVERY $i$ and $n\ge N$.

We use two OLD SAP facts about NONZERO
invariant equivalences, not typed exactness.
First, for every nonempty same-sort STRONG
profile $D\subseteq K^\circ$,
\[
                 K^\circ\starprod D=K^\circ.
\tag{OW-Abs}\label{eq:typed-old-reset-absorption}
\]
For distinct $xKz$, choose any fine atom
$d\in D$. By original transitivity $z$
has a $d$-neighbour in its $K$-class,
and by SAP it has arbitrarily many such
distinct neighbours. Choose $y\ne x,z$
with $y\,d\,z$; then $xK^\circ yDz$.
The reverse inclusion is K-transitivity.
Second, SAME-SORT STRONG positive
products commute and associate:
the RAW old invariant products commute
by symmetry and two-point homogeneity,
and original SAP injectivisation
\cref{lem:typed-injectivisation}
realises their diversity slice by fully
distinct positive word vertices.

Choose the sparse labelled graph
\cref{lem:sparse-labelled-graph} with
edge labels $1,\ldots,r$, vertex colours
the finitely many OLD atoms in $K^\circ$,
and girth $>r(N-1)+1$.
Attach from a new original-sort centre
$o$ to every graph vertex $v$ ONE private
path spelling $w_K$. Replace every
$i$-labelled graph edge by ONE private
path spelling $w_i$. All path interiors
are pairwise disjoint and carry the ORIGINAL
named sorts prescribed by the typed words
$w_K,w_i$; only the centre and graph roots
are required to have original sort $a$.
Every specified edge is an ACTUAL old
DIVERSITY atom.

Every specified SIMPLE cycle of this
partial old network traverses WHOLE
private paths. Contracting these
paths gives a simple cycle in the
graph plus its star at $o$.
A cycle THROUGH $o$ contains TWO
$K^\circ$-spokes and intervening
nonempty $T_i$-factors; close one
spoke against the other and use
\eqref{eq:typed-old-reset-absorption}
to obtain a common endpoint atom
in $K^\circ$. A cycle AVOIDING $o$
is a graph cycle of length
$>r(N-1)+1$, so some label $i$
occurs at least $N$ times.

DIFFERENT label if possible;
otherwise any edge leaves at
least $N$ copies of label $i$.
By STRONG commutativity, positive
powers and absorption, the
complementary word profile is
$K^\circ$ and meets the closing
edge's nonempty $T_j$.
Thus EVERY specified simple cycle
admits a colour-preserving old
HOMOMORPHISM, with no invented
identity factor or simultaneous
choice of root atomic colours.

Semantic CCO now yields ONE
homomorphism of the WHOLE partial
old network. Use original SAP
injectivisation
\cref{lem:typed-injectivisation}
to separate ALL its formal vertices,
preserving the displayed atoms.
For each graph vertex $v$, the

$d_v=\col(o,v)$ belongs to

are distinct. The labelled

       (\{d\}\starprod\{d\})\cap T_i
                      \ne\varnothing
              \qquad\text{for every }i.
\tag{OW-Tri}\label{eq:typed-old-reset-triangle}

external $J$-quotient orbital

 \qquad

       =\mathfrak m\starprod J^\circ.
\]

For the remaining $J^\circ$,

\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Typed relative reset for one named-sort cut]

      \mathfrak m\starprod\mathfrak m=K^\circ.\quad}
 \tag{TR}\label{eq:typed-relative-reset}

Equivalently, for the equality-INCLUSIVE

\[

 \tag{TR-RAW}\label{eq:typed-relative-reset-raw}

\end{theorem}

\end{proof}
