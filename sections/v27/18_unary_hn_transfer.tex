\begin{theorem}[Terminal typed Ramsey expansion]
\label{thm:internal-ramsey-terminal}
Let $\mathcal C$ be a finite-sorted homogeneous complete binary
triangle-constrained class with SAP and semantic homomorphic CCO, such that
every proper same-sort invariant-equivalence cover has infinite relative
index. Then $\mathcal C$ admits a finite relational precompact Ramsey
expansion. Its added comparison predicates record a linear order on the
point-generated transition-enriched hidden coordinate system.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let \(\mathcal H_{\rm typ}\) be the hereditary envelope of all
finite induced unary-closed substructures of the actual terminal
transition-enriched hidden structure. We use its full hidden signature:
original real sorts, proper MI quotient sorts, named unary projections,
upward maps and quotient transitions, and the complete binary
semantic-cylinder relations. This is the hereditary envelope, not just
its point-generated subfamily or a reduct to selected cylinders.

We apply \cref{thm:typed-direct-irreducible-unary-HN} to this age.
The signature is finite and its functions are total unary maps on their
prescribed source sorts. The remaining hypotheses have the following
providers:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The envelope is hereditary by its definition and has SAP by
      \cref{thm:typed-hidden-sap}.
\item Every completed member is irreducible already in the original
      hidden signature, by \cref{lem:typed-terminal-hidden-irreducible}.
\item If every one-generated induced closure of a partial source is a
      completed hidden member, the source is unary-admissible in exactly
      the sense of \cref{def:typed-unary-admissible-source}.
      The uniform bound \(N_{\rm typ}\) from
      \cref{cor:typed-bounded-hidden} therefore supplies the required
      induced unary-closed noncompletable substructure.
\end{enumerate}
The third item uses the bounded-obstruction theorem only on its stated
unary-admissible domain. Its proof, including the uniform closure bound
of \cref{lem:typed-unary-closure-bound}, is independent of the abstract
HN transfer. Conversely, the abstract theorem already verifies that
HN's irreducible-substructure condition enforces this admissibility;
there is no further local-finiteness argument to repeat here.
It follows that \(\mathcal H_{\rm typ}^{<}\), with arbitrary linear
orders, is Ramsey. No auxiliary universal relation \(E_*\) is needed.

Finally \cref{lem:typed-finite-ordered-real-readout} transfers these
ordered hidden Ramsey arrows to the original real sorts. Its finite
list of binary term-comparison predicates records the entire order on
each point-generated hidden closure, including diagonal comparisons
between different term values of one real root. The lemma supplies
precompactness, reasonableness and the embedding correspondence, so the
resulting finite relational real expansion is Ramsey as claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[General SAP symmetric CCO Ramsey expansion]
\label{thm:internal-v27-general-ramsey}
Every homogeneous triangle-defined complete finite symmetric binary
SAP+homomorphic-CCO age has a finite relational precompact Ramsey expansion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The safe exact-complement split theorem
\cref{thm:safe-exact-split} and its terminating iteration
\cref{thm:finite-cover-elimination} yield a finite reasonable unary
terminal expansion which retains SAP and CCO. Apply
\cref{thm:internal-ramsey-terminal} to this finite-sorted terminal age.
The composite is finite relational and precompact. No expansion-property
or coherent-EPPA claim is made here.
\end{proof}
