\section{A simultaneous finite master for exact owner quotients}
\label{sec:acf-master}

A major difficulty of rooted gluing is that compatible
automorphisms of several finite quotient witnesses need
not have a common owner component.  Ordinary EPPA of a
joint owner diagram is not in itself enough: a chosen
factor-owner stabiliser must lift while fixing the
\emph{entire} joint root owner tuple.  We now construct
one finite witness with this strengthening for \emph{all}
exact quotients simultaneously.  This theorem is \emph{conditional on ordinary EPPA providers
for the actual exact quotient sources}; it does not invoke any ACF
induction hypothesis.  In the global induction below these providers
are supplied in a later phase, after rooted ACF has been proved at
the same diversity rank.

For an invariant quotient \(Q\), retain
\(F_Q=\Sigma_Q(\Id)\), the largest finite-class
equivalence.  If \(\pi:Q\to O\) is an exact
quotient, the image of an \(F_Q\)-packet is
contained in an \(F_O\)-packet: the quotient
\((F_Q\vee\ker\pi)/\ker\pi\) has finite
classes and is hence below \(F_O\).
Consequently the full \(F_Q\)-closure of
a finite real set over an \(F_O\)-closed
owner set stays over that owner set.

\begin{lemma}[Finite-base EPPA and point homogeneity]
\label{lem:acf-owner-provider}
Let \(\pi:Q\to O\) be an actual exact quotient
of a standing source.  Assume \(\Age(Q)\) has ordinary EPPA.
Let \(B\subseteq O\)
be finite, nonempty, induced and \(F_O\)-closed.
For any finite \(A\subseteq\pi^{-1}(B)\) there
is a finite induced \(D\subseteq Q\), containing
\(A\), which is \(F_Q\)-closed, projects
precisely onto \(B\), and has:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every compatible pair \((p,\gamma)\),
  with \(p\) a partial automorphism of \(A\)
  and \(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\), extended by an
  automorphism of \(D\) inducing \(\gamma\);
\item for arbitrary \(x,y\in D\) and
  \(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\) satisfying
  \(\gamma(\pi x)=\pi y\), an automorphism
  of \(D\) inducing exactly \(\gamma\)
  and carrying \(x\) to \(y\).
\end{enumerate}
In particular \(\Aut(D)\to\Aut(B)\) is onto
and its kernel is transitive on each fibre.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Enlarge \(A\) to \(A^+=F_Q[A\cup S]\),
where \(S\) contains one actual representative
of every \(B\)-owner.  The preliminary
packet observation keeps \(A^+\) over \(B\).
There are finitely many compatible pairs
\((p,\gamma)\) for partial automorphisms
of \(A^+\) and \(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\);
include all compatible singleton maps and
all empty-real requests. We verify the
AMBIENT-LIFT premise rather than
assuming that mere owner compatibility
is sufficient in an arbitrary source.
For one such $(p,\gamma)$, form the
finite partial map $p$ on original
real points together with $\gamma$
on EVERY actual quotient owner in
$B$. Its real-real restrictions
preserve fine atomic colours
because $p$ is a partial
automorphism; the quotient-quotient
restrictions preserve the ACTUAL
complete owner pair orbitals
because $\gamma\in\Aut(B)$.
For each real point $x$ in
$\operatorname{dom}(p)$ and each
owner class $c\in B$, the mixed
real/owner orbit of $(x,c)$
depends ONLY on the quotient
pair orbital of $(\pi x,c)$:
in the symmetric ORIGINAL
equality-inclusive RAW algebra,
\[
       \Id_Q;a;E
           =a;E=E;a;E,
       \qquad E=\ker\pi,
\]
by \cref{lem:nested-mixed}.
The compatibility
$\pi p=\gamma\pi$ and
preservation of owner pair
orbitals therefore preserve
EVERY mixed real/owner
orbital simultaneously.
Consequently this union is
a genuine finite partial
isomorphism of the COMPLETE
joint real/exact-owner
orbital structure. By
\cref{thm:typed-joint-exact-lifting}
it extends to ONE original
ambient automorphism inducing
the prescribed $\gamma$ on
ALL of B and extending $p$.
The same argument includes
$p=\varnothing$, with no
real-root constraint; no
owner group section is chosen.
The finite-base restriction
Lemma~\ref{lem:finite-base}, using ordinary
EPPA of \(Q\), yields one finite \(D_0\)
over \(B\) extending all these requests.

Take the finite full-packet closure
\(D_1=F_Q[D_0]\), which remains over
exactly \(B\) by the preliminary
finite-packet projection observation.
Every CHOSEN automorphism of \(D_0\)
extends to some ambient automorphism
of \(Q\) by homogeneity. Since \(F_Q\)
is invariant, that extension preserves
\(D_1\) setwise, and its induced owner
permutation on the ENTIRE \(B\) is still
the prescribed \(\gamma\).
Choose one such extension per request,
without imposing any coherence, and let
\(G_0\) be the finite group generated by
their restrictions to \(D_1\). Put
\[
                         D=G_0 A^+.
\]
Because \(A^+\) is a union of full
\(F_Q\)-classes and every \(G_0\)-element
preserves \(F_Q\), each translate
\(g(A^+)\) is again a union of such
classes. Their union \(D\) is therefore
FULL-\(F_Q\)-closed, finite, induced
and \(G_0\)-invariant, and contains \(A\).

The original \(A^+\) already contains
a representative over EVERY \(b\in B\).
Thus \(\pi(D)=B\) even without any
extra group orbit argument; inclusion
\(\pi(D)\subseteq B\) follows because
all \(G_0\)-elements induce automorphisms
of the fixed owner set \(B\).
All selected source extensions restrict to the $G_0$-invariant
set $D$, with their original real values and their full owner
components unchanged, since $\pi(D)=B$. This proves (1).

Apply \cref{lem:acf-point-homogeneity} with its designated
original set $A_0=A^+$. This set is nonempty because it contains
a representative of every owner in the nonempty $B$.
Every point of $D=G_0A^+$ can be moved into $A^+$ by an element
of $G_0$, and all compatible singleton requests on $A^+$ retain
their prescribed-owner extensions in $D$. The lemma gives (2).
No rooted ACF property of $A^+$ is used.
\end{proof}
