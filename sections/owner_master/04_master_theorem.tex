\begin{theorem}[Finite exact-quotient owner master]
\label{thm:acf-owner-master}
Let \(X\) be a nonempty transitive standing
source, and assume ordinary EPPA for \(\Age(X/E)\) for
\emph{every} exact quotient \(X/E\), including \(E=\Id\).
Let
\(\mathcal E_{\rm ex}(X)\) be its full finite
lattice of invariant exact equivalences.
For every finite nonempty \(A\subseteq X\)
there is \emph{one} finite induced \(B\subseteq X\)
containing \(A\), with
\(B_E=\pi_E(B)\subseteq X/E\) for every exact \(E\),
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(M1)] each \(B_E\) is an ordinary EPPA
  witness for \(\pi_E(A)\);
\item[(M2)] each \(B_E\) is \(F_{X/E}\)-closed
  in its actual ambient quotient;
\item[(M3)] for \(E\le H\) exact, the natural
  \(\Aut(B_E)\to\Aut(B_H)\) is onto, and
  its kernel acts transitively on \emph{each}
  fibre of \(B_E\to B_H\);
\item[(M4)] for every finite family
  \(E_1,\ldots,E_m\) of exact equivalences,
  \(B_{\cap_iE_i}\) is precisely the set of
  tuples from \(\prod_iB_{E_i}\) whose images
  agree in every \(B_{E_i\vee E_j}\).
\end{enumerate}
The construction uses only finitely many
ordinary EPPA-provider steps and no safe
palette or ACF induction hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Corollary~\ref{lem:acf-exact-distributive}
and Birkhoff's finite representation theorem,
write the exact lattice as \(\operatorname{Down}(P)\)
for a finite poset \(P\).  Let \(E_d\)
denote the exact equivalence corresponding
to downset \(d\subseteq P\).  For each upset
\(U\subseteq P\), put
\[
             X_U=X/E_{P\setminus U},
        \qquad A_U=\pi_U(A).
\]
For \(V\subseteq U\) upsets there is the actual
exact projection \(X_U\to X_V\).
The empty upset gives a singleton quotient
and \(U=P\) gives the original \(X\).

Process the elements of \(P\) in an order
with larger elements first.  At an intermediate
upset \(U\), suppose finite induced
\(B_V\subseteq X_V\) have been constructed
compatibly for all upsets \(V\subseteq U\),
with properties (M1)--(M4) within this
subsystem.  For \(U=\varnothing\) the
singleton system plainly satisfies them.
Choose \(p\notin U\) whose strict successors
\(O=\{q:q>p\}\) already lie in \(U\);
let \(U_p=O\cup\{p\}\).

The distributive lattice identities and the
A32 exact-coordinate gluing formula give, for
every new upset \(V\subseteq U\cup\{p\}\)
containing \(p\), with \(V^-=V\setminus\{p\}\),
the \emph{actual fine-colour} identification
\[
        X_V=X_{V^-}\times_{X_O}X_{U_p}.
        \tag{\(\ast\)}\label{eq:acf-master-step}
\]
Take the already prepared finite \(B_O\),
which is closed under \(F_{X_O}\), and apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-owner-provider} to
\(X_{U_p}\to X_O\) with original input
\(A_{U_p}\).  Obtain a finite \(D_p\)
over exactly \(B_O\), full finite-packet
closed, with relative EPPA and prescribed
owner point homogeneity.  Keep the old
\(B_V\) unchanged and define for each
new \(V\ni p\)
\[
              B_V=B_{V^-}\times_{B_O}D_p.
              \tag{\(\ast\ast\)}
\]
This is the \emph{full} finite fibre product
inside~\eqref{eq:acf-master-step}; both
coordinates project onto \(B_O\), so the
new quotient projects onto each of its
two factors.  All original \(A_V\) embed.

We check the four properties simultaneously.

\emph{Finite-packet closure (M2).}
Fix a new upset \(V\ni p\), a point
\(w=(b,x)\in B_V\), and another point
\(w'\in F_{X_V}[w]\). Write \(\pi^-,\pi^p\)
for its two ACTUAL exact quotient
projections. The images of the finite
invariant \(F_{X_V}\)-packet under
either projection belong to a finite-class
equivalence of the corresponding
quotient source: if \(K=\ker\pi\)
then \((F_{X_V}\vee K)/K\) has finite
classes, since every one of its
\(K\)-classes is reached by the finite
set \(F_{X_V}[w]\) after commuting
the invariant equivalences
\(F_{X_V}\) and \(K\).
Therefore
\[
 \pi^-(w')\in F_{X_{V^-}}[b],
 \qquad
 \pi^p(w')\in F_{X_{U_p}}[x].
\]
By the old induction and the
local owner-provider lemma, these
two images belong to
\(B_{V^-}\) and \(D_p\),
respectively. As \(w'\) is an
ACTUAL real point of \(X_V\),
its coordinate images agree
over \(X_O\); their common owner
lies in \(B_O\).
The FULL finite fibre product
\eqref{eq:acf-master-step} therefore
contains \(w'\). Thus \(B_V\)
is closed under every full finite
\(F_{X_V}\)-packet, without
requiring \(F_{X_V}\) itself
to be full exact or assuming that
all automorphisms of a quotient
lift globally.

\emph{Full automorphism images (M3).}
For every new $V\ni p$, \cref{lem:acf-fibreproduct-aut} applies to
\eqref{eq:acf-master-step}: the actual exact coordinate kernels,
the full induced product and the common surjective owner image
$B_O$ give
\[
 \Aut(B_V)=
       \Aut(B_{V^-})\times_{\Aut(B_O)}\Aut(D_p).
       \tag{\(\ast\ast\ast\)}
\]
Apply \cref{lem:acf-fibreproduct-owner-kernels} to the largest new
product $L=B_U\times_{B_O}D_p$, with $D=B_U$, $C=D_p$ and
owner set $B_O$. The old (M3) supplies the quotient-action and
kernel-transitivity hypotheses, and
\cref{lem:acf-owner-provider} supplies prescribed-owner point
homogeneity of $D_p$.
For an old target $B_V$, use part (a) with $T=B_V$.
Here $V$ need not contain $O$, so no map $B_V\to B_O$ is required.
For a new target $B_V$, use part (b) with $T=B_{V^-}$ and
$W_T=B_V$: now $O\subseteq V^-$ supplies the required
factorization through $T$, and the displayed group identity
applies to $W_T$ as well.
Thus $\Aut(L)\to\Aut(B_V)$ is onto with fibre-transitive
kernel for every old or new $V$.

This also gives (M3) for every nested pair $B_V\to B_{V'}$.
For surjectivity, lift an automorphism of $B_{V'}$ first to $L$
and then project to $B_V$. For kernel transitivity, lift two
points of $B_V$ with the same $B_{V'}$-image to $L$, move them
by its kernel over $B_{V'}$, and project the resulting
automorphism to $B_V$.

\emph{Ordinary EPPA (M1).}
Old quotients retain (M1). For a new $V\ni p$, apply the EPPA
clause of \cref{lem:acf-fibreproduct-owner-kernels} with
\[
 (D,C,O,A_W)=(B_{V^-},D_p,B_O,A_V).
\]
Take $T=D$, so the identity quotient satisfies condition (i).
The two input projections are exactly $A_{V^-}$ and $A_{U_p}$.
The first has the old EPPA witness $B_{V^-}$; the second has
all prescribed-owner extensions in $D_p$ by
\cref{lem:acf-owner-provider}. The projection kernels and
quotient pair orbitals are unions of the original fine colours,
as required by that clause. It therefore extends every partial
automorphism of $A_V$, including those not induced by a partial
map of the original $A$. This proves (M1) without choosing any
owner-group section.

\emph{Full compatibility (M4).}
It is convenient to state the
assertion DUAL to the exact-equivalence
indexing: for every finite nonempty
family of available upsets
\(V_1,\ldots,V_m\), the finite system
\(B_{\bigcup_i V_i}\) is exactly the
set of tuples \((b_i)_{i\le m}\)
with \(b_i\in B_{V_i}\) which agree
in every \(B_{V_i\cap V_j}\).
This is the CRT written for the
upset presentation \(X_V\);
complementing upsets converts its
unions/intersections to the
meets/joins in M4.

If \(p\notin \bigcup_iV_i\), this
is the old induction hypothesis.
Otherwise, at least one \(V_i\)
contains \(p\), and ANY such
upset contains the WHOLE principal
upset \(U_p=O\cup\{p\}\).
For two indices \(i,j\) containing
\(p\), the intersection \(V_i\cap
V_j\) still contains \(U_p\).
Their agreement in the projected
\(B_{V_i\cap V_j}\) therefore forces
the SAME \(D_p\)-coordinate
\(x\in D_p\) in all \(p\)-containing
members.

For each \(i\), put \(V_i^-=V_i
\setminus\{p\}\) and project its
coordinate \(b_i\) to the already
constructed old \(B_{V_i^-}\).
Because
\[
 (V_i\cap V_j)\setminus\{p\}
    =V_i^-\cap V_j^- ,
\]
the given pairwise compatibility
implies compatibility of EVERY
old coordinate in the finite old
system, even when one of the
original \(V_i\) did not contain
\(p\). By the inductive full
CRT there is one UNIQUE
\(b\in B_{\bigcup_i V_i^-}\)
inducing all \(b_i^-\).
For a \(p\)-containing index
\(i\), the coordinate \(b_i\)
itself belongs to the FULL
fibre product
\(B_{V_i^-}\times_{B_O}D_p\).
Consequently its two projections
have the same \(B_O\) owner.
Since \(b\) induces \(b_i^-\),
the new \(b\) and the common
\(x\) are compatible over \(B_O\).
Thus
\[
          (b,x)\in
          B_{\bigcup_iV_i^-}
                    \times_{B_O}D_p
          =B_{\bigcup_iV_i}.
\]
This tuple realizes ALL given
\(b_i\). It is unique, since
the old coordinate \(b\) and
the common new coordinate \(x\)
are each uniquely determined.
The empty family corresponds
to the singleton top-equivalence
quotient and causes no additional
compatibility condition.
This proves M4 at the new stage.

All checks are performed at each
of the finitely many elements of
\(P\).  At the end \(B=B_P\)
is a finite induced original source
containing \(A\), and its complete
quotient system satisfies
(M1)--(M4).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the master is not a coloured product assumption]
The finite \(\operatorname{Down}(P)\)
coordinatisation is applied to the lattice
of \emph{actual exact equivalences}.
The intermediate sources \(X_U\) retain
all their original fine binary orbitals.
They need not be pure sets, Cartesian
Hamming products, or freely coloured
primitive structures.  The new local
\(D_p\) is obtained by ordinary EPPA
on its actual quotient source.
The sole padding occurs in an ordinary
EPPA construction, not in a rooted
ACF target; this is essential when
owner-relative closure is larger than
the absolute real algebraic closure.
\end{remark}
