\begin{lemma}[Full-fibre-product prescribed-owner kernels and EPPA]
\label{lem:acf-fibreproduct-owner-kernels}
Let $D\to O$ and $C\to O$
be finite induced, SURJECTIVE
actual exact quotient systems,
and let $W=D\times_O C$ be
their FULL induced fine-coloured
fibre product. Assume the ACTUAL
group identity
\[
   \Aut(W)=\Aut(D)\times_{\Aut(O)}
                                \Aut(C)
\tag{FK}\label{eq:acf-fibreproduct-kernel-action}
\]
from \cref{lem:acf-fibreproduct-aut}.
Let $T$ be an actual quotient of $D$, with its induced
automorphism action. The quotients $T$ and $O$ need not be nested.
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $\Aut(D)\to\Aut(T)$
is ONTO and its kernel acts
TRANSITIVELY on each fibre
of $D\to T$;
\item $C$ has prescribed-owner
point homogeneity: for ALL
$x,y\in C$ and EVERY
$\delta\in\Aut(O)$ with
$\delta(\pi_Cx)=\pi_Cy$,
there exists $h\in\Aut(C)$
inducing EXACTLY $\delta$
and satisfying $hx=y$.
\end{enumerate}
Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item $\Aut(W)\to\Aut(T)$
is ONTO and its kernel is
TRANSITIVE on each fibre
of $W\to T$;
\item if the projection $D\to O$ factors through $T$ and
$W_T=T\times_O C$
is also a FULL induced
actual exact fibre product
with its corresponding
automorphism-group identity,
then $\Aut(W)\to\Aut(W_T)$
is ONTO and its kernel is
TRANSITIVE on every fibre
of $W\to W_T$.
\end{enumerate}
In either case no group section
is assumed.

Moreover let $A_W\subseteq W$
be an actual finite induced
source whose quotient
projections $A_D,A_C$
are as displayed.
Suppose $D$ has EPPA for
$A_D$, and $C$ extends
EVERY compatible partial
automorphism of $A_C$
with EVERY prescribed
owner permutation
$\delta\in\Aut(O)$.
Then $W$ has ordinary
EPPA for $A_W$, provided
the two projection kernels
and quotient atomic orbitals
are ACTUAL invariant old
fine-colour unions so that
partial automorphisms of
$A_W$ descend to genuine
partial maps of $A_D,A_C$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $C=\varnothing$, surjectivity gives $O=\varnothing$;
the maps from $D$ and its quotient $T$ then force both to be empty.
All displayed groups are trivial and the assertions follow.
Assume henceforth that $C\ne\varnothing$.

By \cref{lem:prescribed-action-point-lifting}, condition (i)
is equivalent to prescribed-action point lifting for $D\to T$.
We prove that same property for both displayed quotient maps of $W$;
the lemma will then give their onto actions and fibre-transitive kernels.
The full product $W$ projects onto $D$ and hence onto $T$;
these sets are nonempty.

For (a), fix $(d,x),(d',y)\in W$ and $\eta\in\Aut(T)$
with $\eta(\pi_Td)=\pi_Td'$. Choose $g\in\Aut(D)$ inducing
$\eta$ and sending $d$ to $d'$. Its induced $O$-action $\delta$
satisfies $\delta(\pi_Cx)=\pi_Cy$. By (ii), choose
$h\in\Aut(C)$ inducing exactly $\delta$ and sending $x$ to $y$.
Then $(g,h)\in\Aut(W)$ by (FK), induces $\eta$ on $T$,
and sends the first point to the second. This proves (a), without
requiring a map $T\to O$.

For (b), $W\to W_T$ is onto: a point $(t,x)\in W_T$ lifts
by choosing $d$ above $t$, since the $O$-projection factors through $T$.
Fix $(d,x),(d',y)\in W$ and an automorphism of $W_T$
carrying their images to one another. By its assumed full-product
group identity, that automorphism is a pair $(\eta,h)$ with
$\eta\in\Aut(T)$, $h\in\Aut(C)$ and matching $O$-components.
It satisfies $\eta(\pi_Td)=\pi_Td'$ and $hx=y$.
Choose $g\in\Aut(D)$ inducing $\eta$ and sending $d$ to $d'$.
Since $D\to O$ factors through $T$, its $O$-component equals
that of $h$. Thus $(g,h)\in\Aut(W)$ lifts the given automorphism
and carries the first point to the second. This proves (b).

Finally fix any partial
automorphism $f$ of
the original source
$A_W$. Its two
coordinate projections
$f_D,f_C$ are
well-defined partial
automorphisms of the
projected induced sources
by the ACTUAL named
equivalence-kernel and
quotient-colour hypothesis.
Choose a $g\in\Aut(D)$
extending $f_D$ by
ordinary EPPA and write
$\delta$ for its EXACT
O-owner component.
Both f-projections have
identical action on the
common old owner parameters
over their original
domains; hence
$(f_C,\delta)$ is a
compatible request.
Choose $h\in\Aut(C)$
extending $f_C$ with
EXACT component $\delta$.
The pair $(g,h)$
acts as an automorphism
of the FULL product W,
by (FK), and extends
the ORIGINAL f.
No choice of a common
owner section is made.
\end{proof}
