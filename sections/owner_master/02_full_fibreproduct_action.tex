\begin{lemma}[Automorphisms of a full exact fibre product]
\label{lem:acf-fibreproduct-aut}
Let \(X\) be a source in the standing one-sort symmetric
homomorphic CCO setting, and let \(E,J\) be invariant full exact
equivalences on \(X\). Put
\(K=E\cap J\) and \(H=E\vee J\), and
regard \(X/K\) as the ACTUAL fine-coloured
fibre product
\[
       X/K=(X/E)\times_{X/H}(X/J).
\]
Let \(D\subseteq X/E\) and \(C\subseteq X/J\)
be finite induced structures whose images
in \(X/H\) are one and the same finite
induced set \(B\), and assume both
\(D\to B\), \(C\to B\) are surjective.
Let \(W=D\times_B C\) be the FULL finite
fibre product, with its original induced
fine-colour structure from \(X/K\).
Then restriction to the two coordinates
gives a natural group isomorphism
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
  \operatorname{Aut}(W)\cong
  \operatorname{Aut}(D)
       \times_{\operatorname{Aut}(B)}
  \operatorname{Aut}(C).\qquad}
\tag{FPA}\label{eq:acf-fibreproduct-aut}
\]
In particular, the formula concerns
automorphisms of the FULL finite induced
fibre product; it does not hold merely
from a set-theoretic bijection for an
arbitrary coloured subset of that product.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The projections \(W\to D,C\) are onto,
because every owner in \(B\) has a preimage
in each coordinate. Their kernels are
the restrictions of actual exact
equivalences on \(X/K\), hence are
finite unions of named original atomic
colours. Consequently every automorphism
of \(W\) induces well-defined
automorphisms \(g_D,g_C\) of both
coordinate images, and their restrictions
to \(B\) agree. The pair determines the
automorphism of \(W\), because the map
\(W\to D\times C\) is injective.

Conversely suppose \(g_D\) and \(g_C\)
are automorphisms with the SAME induced
action on the actual finite owner set
\(B\). Their coordinatewise map sends
each \((d,c)\in W\) to
\((g_Dd,g_Cc)\in W\), since compatibility
over \(B\) is preserved. It is a bijection
of the full fibre product. To verify
that it preserves the ORIGINAL fine
binary orbitals, fix a pair of points
of \(W\) of old \(K\)-quotient atomic
colour \(a_K\). Choose an old atomic
representative \(a\) of that block.
Its two coordinate quotient orbitals
pull back to \(E;a;E\) and \(J;a;J\).
In the original symmetric RAW represented
exact algebra,
\[
  (E;a;E)\cap(J;a;J)
     =(E\cap J);a;(E\cap J)
     =K;a;K ,
\]
by \cref{lem:exact-quotient-colour-meet}. Thus
the two quotient binary orbitals
determine EXACTLY the fine
\(K\)-quotient binary orbital, even
if one coordinate pair lies on its
quotient diagonal. Both coordinate
automorphisms preserve their
respective orbitals, so their
product map preserves the original
fine atomic colour on \(W\).
This proves \eqref{eq:acf-fibreproduct-aut}
without assuming SAP, independent
cross-colouring choices, or a
group-theoretic section.
\end{proof}
