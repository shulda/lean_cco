\section{Terminal original invariant equivalences: RAW words and CRT}
\label{sec:foundational-terminal-invariant-exactness}

\begin{lemma}[CCO-free RAW word presentations for all original invariant equivalences]
\label{lem:typed-all-invariant-equivalences-raw-word}
Let $M$ be a homogeneous finite-complete binary
structure with a finite old atomic pair palette
and individually transitive named original sorts;
assume SAME-SORT old atomic orbitals are symmetric.
Fix one original real sort $a$. Suppose the FULL
original invariant-equivalence lattice on $a$
has NO relative-index-two interval. Then EVERY
original $\Aut(M)$-invariant equivalence $E$
on $M_a$ is the ACTUAL equality-inclusive RAW
relation of ONE old atomic word on sort $a$.
Thus EVERY invariant equivalence is FULL RAW
exact (already as one word profile). Neither
visible SAP nor semantic CCO, exact-ball Helly
or distributivity is assumed. In particular
this lemma does NOT claim that the FULL invariant
lattice is distributive without CCO.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The case $E=\Id_a$ is the ACTUAL RAW empty
word; if the sort is a singleton this is
the only invariant equivalence.
Otherwise list all old same-sort diversity
atoms $t_1,\ldots,t_m$ contained in $E$.
For each $t_i$ let $F_i$ be the ACTUAL
equivalence of connectedness in its
undirected old orbital graph, including
TRUE logical real equality. Since
$t_i\subseteq E$, we have $F_i\le E$.

If a component of the $t_i$-graph were
bipartite, original transitivity would
make ALL its components bipartite. The
even-walk relation then defines an ACTUAL
$\Aut(M)$-invariant equivalence of relative
index two in $F_i$, contradicting the FULL
original hypothesis. Hence every component
is connected NONBIPARTITE. Between every
two vertices of one such component,
INCLUDING equal endpoints, $t_i$-walks
exist of all sufficiently large lengths:
an odd-cycle detour changes parity and
two-step backtracks increase length by two.
There are finitely many OLD fine pair
orbitals in $F_i$, including its diagonal;
choose one witness pair for each, and by
old homogeneity and transitivity obtain
one UNIFORM $N_i$ for all pairs.
Thus for every $n\ge N_i$ the ACTUAL
equality-inclusive RAW relational product
of $n$ copies of the SINGLE atomic $t_i$
equals $F_i$. This is a RAW old word
realisation, not an assertion that a
STRONG diversity word contains identity.

Same-sort invariant RAW relations commute:
each original invariant same-sort relation is symmetric,
its represented product with another invariant relation
is again invariant and symmetric, whereas
\((R;S)^\smile=S;R\). Therefore
\(R;S=S;R\) without any terminal or CCO input.
Consequently the represented RAW product
$F_1;\cdots;F_m$ equals their equivalence
join. It is contained in $E$ and contains
the ACTUAL diagonal and EVERY diversity
atom $t_i$ in $E$. Therefore
\[
                       E=F_1;\cdots;F_m.
\]
Concatenate the finitely many actual RAW
atomic words representing the $F_i$.
This is ONE old atomic word representing
$E$ (and the empty list case is handled
by the actual empty word). By the
original definition of FULL exact menus
as nonempty finite intersections of
RAW represented word profiles, $E$
is itself FULL RAW exact. No binary
CCO or localised hidden SAP was used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Terminal typed invariant equivalences are RAW exact]
\label{lem:typed-terminal-all-equivalences-exact}
Fix an original named transitive sort $a$ of a finite
complete homogeneous typed symmetric SAP+semantic-CCO
source which has no proper finite relative-index interval
of invariant equivalences on $a$. Then EVERY
$\Aut(M)$-invariant equivalence $E$ on $M_a$
is an actual RAW equality-inclusive represented
atomic word profile. In particular, the FULL finite
sortwise invariant-equivalence lattice is distributive.
This does not assert commutativity of mixed-sort arrows.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The RAW word representation follows ALREADY under
the weaker no-relative-index-two hypothesis
from \cref{lem:typed-all-invariant-equivalences-raw-word};
it does not itself require CCO or SAP.
In the present terminal semantic-CCO source,
\cref{thm:full-typed-exact-balls}(iii) applies
to classes of these ACTUAL FULL exact
equivalences. Their finite pairwise-compatible
families have common original real points.
The commuting-equivalence CRT criterion
\cref{thm:abstract-exact-crt} therefore gives
distributivity of the FULL invariant lattice.
This latter step, unlike RAW word representation,
does require the original CCO exact-ball theorem.
\end{proof}
