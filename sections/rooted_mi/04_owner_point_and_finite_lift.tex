\begin{lemma}[Prescribed owner action on one point]
\label{lem:acf-point-homogeneity}
Let \(D\subseteq Q\) be a finite induced structure whose image
under an invariant owner quotient is a finite set \(B\), and
suppose every automorphism of \(D\) induces an automorphism of \(B\).
Suppose every point of \(D\) is moved into the original
nonempty \(A_0\subseteq D\) by some relative
automorphism, and every compatible singleton request
\(a\mapsto a'\) on original points \(a,a'\in A_0\),
with \emph{every} prescribed compatible
\(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\), extends in \(D\).
Then for arbitrary \(x,y\in D\) and
\(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\) with
\(\gamma(\operatorname{own}(x))=\operatorname{own}(y)\),
there exists \(\beta\in\Aut(D)\) inducing precisely
\(\gamma\) and sending \(x\) to \(y\).
Consequently it sends any subset of \(F_Q[x]\)
into \(F_Q[y]\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose \(g,h\in\Aut(D)\) with
\(g(x)=a\in A_0\) and \(h(y)=a'\in A_0\).
The required owner component on an automorphism
taking \(a\) to \(a'\) is
\(\operatorname{own}(h)\gamma
          \operatorname{own}(g)^{-1}\).
It is compatible with \(a\mapsto a'\).
Extend this singleton request by \(k\), with
that exact component.  Then \(h^{-1}kg\)
does the job.  Invariance of \(F_Q\) gives
the packet conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Finite exact reconstruction preserves a root]
\label{lem:acf-finite-lift}
Let \(K\) be a finite-class \emph{exact} invariant
equivalence of a standing source \(Q\), and write
\(\pi:Q\to\overline Q=Q/K\).
For any finite real \(A_0\) one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:acf-finite-acl}
 \operatorname{acl}_Q(A_0)=
 \pi^{-1}\bigl(\operatorname{acl}_{\overline Q}(\pi A_0)\bigr).
\end{equation}
The strong closed-\(S_Q\) hypothesis descends to
\(\overline Q\) for the full-matrix palette
\[
 \overline S=\{q\in\mathcal D(\overline Q):
                    \Lambda_K(q)\subseteq S_Q\},
\]
where \(\Lambda_K(q)\) denotes the set of original fine
colours occurring in full \(K\times K\) cross matrices over \(q\).
For nonempty \(A_0\), if \(\overline D\) is an ordinary EPPA
witness with rooted \(\overline S\)-ACF over \(\pi(A_0)\),
then \(D=\pi^{-1}(\overline D)\) is an ordinary EPPA witness
with rooted \(S_Q\)-ACF over \(A_0\).

For the following STRONGER RELATIVE
assertion, assume explicitly that the
original owner quotient is an ACTUAL
invariant FULL EXACT quotient \(Q/H\).
Its corresponding coarse owner quotient
after factoring out \(K\) is
\[
 \overline Q/\overline H=Q/(K\vee H),
 \qquad \overline H=(K\vee H)/K .
\]
Suppose a finite relative rooted ACF
witness \(\overline D\) for \(\pi(A_0)\), with safe palette
\(\overline S\), is available in \(\overline Q\) over a finite
coarse owner set
\(\overline B\subseteq Q/(K\vee H)\).
Let \(B\subseteq Q/H\) be the
\emph{complete finite inverse owner
packet} over \(\overline B\), and suppose
the induced map
\(\Aut(B)\to\Aut(\overline B)\) is
surjective with kernel transitive on
each owner-cover fibre. Then
\(D=\pi^{-1}(\overline D)\) is a rooted
relative ACF witness over \(B\) for
\(A_0\), with all compatible source
requests and rooted targets preserved.
The explicit exactness of \(H\)
is needed here to invoke joint
exact-sort homogeneity; no claim is
made for an arbitrary nonexact owner
quotient under these hypotheses alone.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We prove the algebraic-closure identity
\eqref{eq:acf-finite-acl} with both directions
and with the stabiliser groups distinguished.
Let \(x\in Q\) and \(\bar x=\pi(x)\).
If \(\bar x\in\operatorname{acl}_{\bar Q}(\pi A_0)\),
its orbit under \(\Aut(\bar Q/\pi A_0)\)
is finite. The image of \(\Aut(Q/A_0)\)
acts on \(\bar Q\) inside this pointwise
stabiliser; therefore the \(\Aut(Q/A_0)\)
orbit of \(x\) lies in finitely many
\(K\)-classes, each finite by hypothesis.
Thus \(x\in\operatorname{acl}_Q(A_0)\).

Conversely suppose
\(\bar x\notin\operatorname{acl}_{\bar Q}(\pi A_0)\).
There are infinitely many DISTINCT
quotient classes \(\bar x'\) in its orbit
under \(\Aut(\bar Q/\pi A_0)\).
For each such \(\bar x'\), the finite
joint sort map fixing EACH actual
real point of \(A_0\), fixing the
named \(K\)-classes of \(\pi A_0\),
and sending the ONE additional
\(K\)-class \(\bar x\) to \(\bar x'\)
is a partial isomorphism of the
actual real/\(K\)-quotient binary
language: its within-quotient
orbitals are preserved by the chosen
quotient automorphism, the real
diagram on \(A_0\) is fixed, and
EVERY mixed orbital between a real
point \(a\in A_0\) and \(\bar x\)
depends only on
\((\pi(a),\bar x)\), by
\cref{lem:nested-mixed}.
The case \(\bar x\in\pi A_0\)
is excluded by its infinite orbit.
Joint exact-sort homogeneity
\cref{thm:joint-exact} extends this
finite map to some automorphism of
\(Q\) fixing \(A_0\) pointwise and
sending \(\bar x\) to \(\bar x'\).
These infinitely many target
\(K\)-classes force an infinite
\(\Aut(Q/A_0)\) orbit of the
actual point \(x\). Hence
\(x\notin\operatorname{acl}_Q(A_0)\).
This proves \eqref{eq:acf-finite-acl}
without presuming a section
\(\Aut(\bar Q)\to\Aut(Q)\).

For safety, lift closed quotient factors to
\emph{full finite} \(K\)-preimages, apply the
original strong closed-\(S_Q\) amalgamation, and
project back.  The preimages are full
\(K\)-classes, so a real strong amalgam cannot
identify two distinct off-base \(K\)-classes;
all entries of each new cross matrix are safe.

Both witness assertions use the same finite joint-map lifting
observation. Suppose \(\overline\beta\in\Aut(\overline D)\)
and \(p_0\) is a finite original partial isomorphism with domain
and range in \(D=\pi^{-1}(\overline D)\), satisfying
\(\pi p_0=\overline\beta\pi\) on its domain. The union of
\(p_0\) and \(\overline\beta\) on all named \(K\)-classes
of \(\overline D\) is a partial isomorphism of the joint
real/\(K\)-quotient language. Indeed, within-sort orbitals are
preserved by the two maps, and every mixed orbital factors
through the \(K\)-projection by \cref{lem:nested-mixed}.
Thus \cref{thm:joint-exact} gives an ambient lift preserving
\(D\) setwise and extending \(p_0\).

In the relative situation, the owner image of \(D\) is exactly
\(B\): every \(K\)-class with \((K\vee H)\)-owner \(c\)
meets every \(H\)-class in \(c\), by \(K;H=K\vee H\), and
\(\overline D\) projects onto \(\overline B\). The same lifting
observation allows an additional prescribed
\(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\), provided it agrees with \(p_0\) on
real owners and induces the same coarse owner action as
\(\overline\beta\). To check the enlarged joint map, mixed
real/\(H\) pairs use \cref{lem:nested-mixed}; mixed
\(K\)/\(H\) pairs use \cref{lem:acf-mixed-exact-join}, namely
\[
                  K;a;H=(K\vee H);a.
\]
Their pair orbit is determined by the common quotient
\(Q/(K\vee H)\), where the two actions agree. Hence
\cref{thm:joint-exact} also lifts this enlarged finite map,
with its exact prescribed action on all of \(B\).
No global section of quotient automorphism groups is used.

For source extension, project the original partial map \(p\)
to \(\overline p\), extend it in \(\overline D\), and apply
the preceding observation with \(p_0=p\). In the relative case,
use the quotient witness extension with the coarse action
induced by the prescribed \(\gamma\). This proves ordinary EPPA
and its stated relative version on the same full preimage \(D\).

For rooted transport, an original unsafe clique \(Z\) projects
to an \(\overline S\)-unsafe set of distinct \(K\)-classes:
any original unsafe edge between different classes witnesses
that their full cross matrix is not contained in \(S_Q\).
A singleton projection is unsafe vacuously. Choose a quotient
rooted automorphism \(\overline\alpha\) carrying \(\pi(Z)\)
into \(\operatorname{acl}_{\overline Q}(\pi A_0)\) and taking
\(\pi(z)\) to \(\pi(a)\) for one actual \(a\in A_0\).
Without owners, the lifting observation with \(p_0:z\mapsto a\)
already gives the required automorphism of \(D\).

In the relative case let \(\overline\gamma\) be the coarse
owner component of \(\overline\alpha\). By
\cref{lem:prescribed-action-point-lifting}, the onto owner action
with fibre-transitive kernel supplies a lift
\(\gamma\in\Aut(B)\) of \(\overline\gamma\) satisfying
\(\gamma(H[z])=H[a]\). Apply
the enlarged lifting observation to
\(\overline\alpha,\gamma,z\mapsto a\).
In either case the resulting automorphism sends the selected
root literally to \(a\), and its whole clique into
\[
 \pi^{-1}\bigl(\operatorname{acl}_{\overline Q}(\pi A_0)\bigr)
                  =\operatorname{acl}_Q(A_0)
\]
by \eqref{eq:acf-finite-acl}. No independently chosen
permutations of finite \(K\)-fibres are required.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The finite owner hypotheses]
The finite owner-packet and kernel-transitivity
hypotheses in Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-finite-lift}
are substantial.  They will be furnished
simultaneously for all canonical MI factors by
the finite exact-quotient master below, rather
than by choosing incompatible finite owner
sets independently.
\end{remark}
