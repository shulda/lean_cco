\section{Rooted relative witnesses on unary-closure factors}
\label{sec:acf-relative-mi}

We next strengthen the \(\Gamma\)-relative R07 construction,
without changing its valuation rows.  It is useful to keep
separate the two sorts of requirements: an extension of a
\emph{source partial map} may prescribe its owner permutation,
whereas an automorphism transporting an unsafe clique is
allowed to choose its owner component.  Rooting the latter
transport is what will make the eventual components compatible.

Let \(Q\) be an actual homogeneous exact quotient from the
previous section whose greatest finite-class equivalence
\(F_Q\) is meet-irreducible.  Its real algebraic closure is
unary, \(\operatorname{acl}_Q(A)=F_Q[A]\).  Let
\(H\) be an invariant exact owner equivalence containing
the unique minimal infinite direction of \(Q\).
For a finite induced \(B\subseteq Q/H\), write
\(\Gamma_B=\Aut(B)\).
A relative automorphism of a finite structure over \(B\)
is a pair consisting of an original fine-colour
automorphism and its induced \(\gamma\in\Gamma_B\).
There is \emph{no} implicit splitting of the induced
homomorphism onto \(\Gamma_B\).

\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous full-row zeroing on a recorded packet clique]
\label{lem:acf-recorded-row-zeroing}
Use the R07 full-row packet universe
\(W=\{(i,f,z):i\in I,\ f\in G^I,\ z\in G\}\)
and a structural packet automorphism
\(s(i,z)=(\sigma i,z+b_i)\).
Let \(J\subseteq I\) be a set of DISTINCT
structural indices such that EVERY two
different indices of \(J\) are a
RECORDED pair in \(\mathcal R\).
For each \(i\in J\), specify one actual
row \(f_i\in G^I\). Then some correction
matrix \(C\in G^{I\times I}\) satisfies
the R07 equations
\(C_{ij}+C_{ji}=0\) on all UNRECORDED
\(i\ne j\), and the associated
automorphism \(\alpha_C\) sends
\[
     \{(i,f_i,z):z\in G\}
        \quad\text{onto}\quad
     \{(\sigma i,0,z):z\in G\}
\]
up to its fixed internal translation \(b_i\),
SIMULTANEOUSLY for every \(i\in J\).
This is a clique-transport correction,
not an assertion that the automorphism
also fixes all original zero-row source packets.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{lem:R07-row-compatibility-iff} with
\(T_i=\{f_i\}\) for \(i\in J\), \(T_i=\varnothing\)
otherwise, and every requested target row equal to zero.
Its first condition is automatic because at most one row is
requested at each index. Its second is vacuous because no two
touched indices form an unrecorded pair. It therefore supplies
one legal correction matrix, with
\(C_{ij}=f_i(j)+b_i\) on each selected row. The universal
lift assertion of \cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa} makes the resulting
\(\alpha_C\) an automorphism of the fixed decoder; it need not
extend any separately prescribed source request.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Unsafe multi-packet cliques descend to the padded source]
\label{lem:acf-multipacket-rooted-transport}
Let \(A^+\) be a finite complete full-packet
source obtained from \(F_Q[A_0]\) by adding
padding packets with EVERY new cross matrix
\(S_Q\)-safe, including all padding-to-original
pairs. Build the finite R07 full-row decoder
from a completable HKN structural reservoir
\(B_0\supseteq A^+\), using the orbit-safe
selector \(h_{S^G}\) on its originally
unrecorded packet pairs.

For every all-non-\(S_Q\) clique \(Z\)
in the decoded finite witness that
meets at least TWO distinct actual packets,
there is one decoded \(\widetilde\Gamma\)-
automorphism taking \(Z\) into
\(F_Q[A_0]\). More precisely, its
structural component moves all touched
structural packet indices into those of
the padded source \(A^+\), and one
simultaneous full-row correction moves
the selected actual packet of each such
index into its zero row.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \cref{lem:orbit-safe-all-row-interface}, every originally
unrecorded pair of distinct actual packets has an entirely
\(S_Q\)-safe cross matrix, including two packets with the same
structural index and different rows. Thus the unsafe clique
\(Z\) meets at most one actual packet \((i,f_i)\) at each
structural index \(i\), and its set \(J\) of touched indices
is a recorded clique.

The full structural packets on \(J\), together with their
complete recorded cross matrices, form an irreducible
substructure of \(B_0\): each packet is unary-generated,
and recorded fine edges join every two packets.
The irreducible-faithfulness conclusion of
\cref{thm:completable-reservoir} supplies one structural
\(\widetilde\Gamma\)-automorphism
\(s(i,z)=(\sigma i,z+b_i)\) carrying this whole substructure
into \(A^+\). Apply \cref{lem:acf-recorded-row-zeroing} to
\(s\) and the rows \(f_i\), \(i\in J\). Its single legal
correction sends every touched actual packet into the zero row
over \(\sigma i\), so the resulting decoded automorphism
\(\alpha_C\) carries \(Z\) into the literal copy of \(A^+\).

This use of \(s\) concerns only the partial structural reservoir.
It need not preserve a chosen completion of its holes; the
universal lift assertion of \cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa}
ensures that \(\alpha_C\) preserves the completed decoder and
every original fine colour. Hence \(\alpha_C(Z)\) is still
unsafe and still meets at least two actual packets. Any point
in a padding packet would then have a safe edge to a point in
another touched packet, by the padding hypothesis. This is
impossible, so \(\alpha_C(Z)\subseteq F_Q[A_0]\).
\end{proof}
