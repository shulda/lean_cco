\begin{lemma}[Normalised relative rooted MI construction]
\label{lem:acf-relative-mi-normal}
Let \(Q\) be an infinite standing homogeneous source with no
nonidentity finite-class exact equivalence, and let
\(S_Q\subseteq\mathcal D(Q)\) have strong closed amalgamation.
Write \(F_Q\) for its greatest finite-class invariant equivalence
and \(E\) for its unique minimal infinite direction. Let
\(H\ge E\) be an actual invariant FULL EXACT owner equivalence,
and assume \(\Age(Q/E)\) has ordinary EPPA.
Let \(A_0\subseteq Q\) be finite
nonempty and let \(B\subseteq Q/H\) be finite, induced and
satisfy
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(B\) contains the owner projection of \(A_0\),
  is an ordinary EPPA witness for that projection,
  and the union of their owner orbits covers \(B\):
  \(\Gamma_B\cdot\operatorname{own}_H(A_0)=B\);
\item \(B\) is closed under the largest finite-class
  equivalence of the actual owner quotient \(Q/H\).
\end{enumerate}
Here \(\Gamma_B=\Aut(B)\) in the actual quotient language, and
\(\operatorname{own}_H(x)=[x]_H\). A source request \((p,\gamma)\)
is \emph{compatible} precisely when
\[
 \operatorname{own}_H(p(x))=\gamma(\operatorname{own}_H(x))
 \qquad(x\in\operatorname{dom}p).
\]
This includes every \((\varnothing,\gamma)\).
There exists one finite, full-\(F_Q\)-closed, original-language
\(D\subseteq Q\), projecting onto exactly \(B\) and
containing \(F_Q[A_0]\), such that
\begin{enumerate}
\item every compatible source partial automorphism
  \((p,\gamma)\), \(p:A_0\rightharpoonup A_0\),
  \(\gamma\in\Gamma_B\), extends to a relative
  automorphism of \(D\) with owner component precisely
  \(\gamma\); this includes all compatible singleton
  and empty-real-domain requests;
\item for every nonempty all-non-\(S_Q\) clique
  \(Z\subseteq D\) and \(z\in Z\), some relative
  automorphism \(\alpha\) satisfies
  \[
       \alpha(Z)\subseteq F_Q[A_0],
                   \qquad \alpha(z)\in A_0.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the normalised MI case, \(R_E=F_Q\): otherwise the global
correspondence lemma would supply another infinite exact direction
transverse to \(E\). The bottom \(F_Q\) is a thin regular packet
torsor for the central elementary abelian group \(G\), by
\cref{lem:thin-bottom}.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{1. A common fine-owner provider.}
Start with the finite \(G\)-invariant set of \(E\)-classes
meeting \(F_Q[A_0]\). For each source request \((p,\gamma)\),
including \((\varnothing,\gamma)\) for every
\(\gamma\in\Gamma_B\), \cref{thm:joint-exact} supplies an
ambient lift of the real/\(H\)-owner request. Its restriction
to \(F_Q[\operatorname{dom}p]\) induces a partial map
\(\pi_E(F_Q[\operatorname{dom}p])\to
\pi_E(F_Q[\operatorname{ran}p])\) within the chosen fine-owner
set, compatible with \(\gamma\). Apply
\cref{lem:finite-base} in the actual exact quotient
\(Q/E\to Q/H\) to this finite list. Its EPPA hypothesis is
precisely the assumed ordinary EPPA of \(\Age(Q/E)\).
We obtain a finite fine-owner structure \(U_1\) projecting onto
\(B\), with a selected extension for every request.

Put \(U=G[U_1]\). Each selected automorphism of \(U_1\),
together with its prescribed action on the ENTIRE \(B\), is a
finite partial isomorphism of the actual \(E\)- and \(H\)-quotient
sorts: every mixed pair factors through the \(H\)-projection by
\cref{lem:nested-mixed}. Apply \cref{thm:joint-exact} in the
ORIGINAL \(Q\) to lift this joint finite map. That original
automorphism commutes with \(G\) by \cref{lem:thin-bottom},
so it preserves \(G[U_1]\) and restricts to an automorphism
of \(U\). No centrality assertion about a larger quotient
automorphism group or global lifting section is needed.
The finite-class closure of \(B\) ensures that no new
\(H\)-owner appears. Let \(\widetilde\Gamma\) be the finite
group of automorphisms of \(U\) inducing automorphisms of
\(B\). It contains the actual thin owner translations and
has a compatible lift for every prescribed \((p,\gamma)\).
The same joint-lifting argument on \(U\) together with \(B\)
applies to EVERY element of \(\widetilde\Gamma\). Its original
lift commutes with \(G\), so \(\widetilde\Gamma\) commutes
with the induced thin action on \(U\), as required by the
safe palettes and moving readings below.
In particular, the empty-real-domain requests prove that
\(\widetilde\Gamma\to\Gamma_B\) is onto. No section is chosen,
and the thin action on owner labels need not be faithful.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{2. Safe padding and the fixed decoder.}
The normalised relative-closure theorem
\cref{thm:relative-real-closure} gives closure \(F_Q[-]\) in
the finite fine-owner source, and \cref{cor:relative-closed-sap}
gives its full-packet closed SAP. Thus
\cref{lem:orbit-safe-strong} applies to the assumed absolute
strong closed-\(S_Q\) amalgamation: its full-owner transversal
has strong orbit-safe palettes \(S^G\), equivariant under
\(\widetilde\Gamma\) and unilateral thin translation.
The augmented safe algebra of
\cref{lem:safe-trace-product,lem:augmented-safe-algebra}
provides a pure-owner covariant selector on the safe traces;
\cref{lem:orbit-safe-readings} supplies its compatible moving
readings. These are separate inputs: the original exact selector
alone does not handle intersection with a safe palette, nor is
\(S_Q\) assumed invariant under unilateral translation.

Pad \(F_Q[A_0]\) to a complete full-packet source \(A^+\)
meeting every fine owner in \(U\). To add a missing owner,
strongly \(S^G\)-amalgamate the existing origin transversal
with a fresh singleton of that owner over the empty base,
then inflate the new origin to its full \(G\)-packet. Repeat
for the finitely many missing owners. Every new cross matrix
is entirely \(S_Q\)-safe, including padding-to-original and
padding-to-padding pairs, while the original matrices remain
unchanged.

For each prescribed \((p,\gamma)\), choose its fine-owner lift
\(\widetilde\gamma\in\widetilde\Gamma\) from Step~1.
The joint map \(p\cup\widetilde\gamma\) lifts by
\cref{thm:joint-exact}; restricting to \(F_Q[\operatorname{dom}p]\)
gives a full-packet partial map of \(A^+\), with range in
\(F_Q[A_0]\) and with that exact language component.
Apply \cref{thm:completable-reservoir} to \(A^+\), including
these requests and every empty-real-domain
\(\widetilde\Gamma\)-request. Decode its full-row reservoir
with the selector from Step~2. By
\cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa,lem:orbit-safe-all-row-interface}
this gives one finite complete \(D_0\), containing \(A^+\)
in its zero rows, extending all prescribed source requests,
and with every originally unrecorded actual interpacket matrix
safe. Its universe, PC menus and colouring are fixed before
any request-specific or clique-specific correction matrix.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{3. Transport and invariant trimming.}
The padding just constructed and the all-row safety conclusion
are exactly the hypotheses of
\cref{lem:acf-multipacket-rooted-transport}. Consequently every
unsafe clique in \(D_0\) meeting at least two actual packets is
carried into \(F_Q[A_0]\) by an automorphism of this same decoder.
The structural component alone need not preserve the chosen
completion of the HKN holes; the decoded full-row lift does.

Let \(K\) be the group of underlying original-language
permutations of \(\Aut_{\widetilde\Gamma}(D_0)\) and set
\[
                   D=\bigcup_{\beta\in K}\beta(F_Q[A_0]).
\]
By \cref{lem:R07-global-diagonal-thin}, \(K\) contains the
actual global group \(G\), acting regularly within each packet.
Since the zero-row \(A^+\) realises every fine owner,
\cref{lem:acf-full-owner-language-faithfulness} identifies the
semilinear group with \(K\) and gives an onto owner map
\(K\to\widetilde\Gamma\to\Gamma_B\). This does not require
faithfulness of \(G\)'s owner action.

We may now apply \cref{lem:acf-invariant-packet-orbit-trim}.
It makes \(D\) finite, induced and full-\(F_Q\)-closed, containing
\(F_Q[A_0]\) and invariant under every selected source extension
and clique transport. Its owner image is all of \(B\), since
\(K\to\Gamma_B\) is onto and
\(\Gamma_B\cdot\operatorname{own}(A_0)=B\).
The same lemma gives selected-root transport for every
single-packet clique. For a multi-packet unsafe clique, the
transport from the preceding paragraph supplies its more general
hypothesis, so it again gives the whole clique in \(F_Q[A_0]\)
and the selected root in \(A_0\). In both cases the final root
correction is one global thin translation.

All source extensions restrict to this one \(D\), with their
prescribed owner actions unchanged because its owner image is
\(B\). Forgetting auxiliary owner predicates, unary translation
functions, rows and structural indices leaves an induced
original-language witness: each selected semilinear automorphism
preserves every literal original fine colour. The actual quotient
map \(D\to B\) remains available for owner gluing. No coherent
choice of extensions, independent packet permutations or group
section is asserted.
\end{proof}
