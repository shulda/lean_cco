\section{Orbit-safe full-packet completion under thin translations}
\label{sec:orbit-safe-packets}

The pure-owner safe-trace selector and the moving-type readings
must be connected through a $G$-stable safe palette.  Merely
knowing $h_{st}(D)\in S$ does \emph{not} imply that every
$h^u_{st}(D)$ belongs to $S$: unilateral thin translations
may permute literal fine colours.  The following strengthening
uses the fact that our original $S$ amalgamates \emph{full}
finite algebraic packets, not arbitrary incomplete sets.

Fix a finite $G$-invariant set $U$ of actual fine $E$-owners
in a normalised one-direction source $Q$, its full-owner
primitive transversal $\mathcal U_U$, and let $\Gamma\le\Aut(U)$
be a finite group of genuine quotient automorphisms.  The group
$G$ acts on fine colours by unilateral endpoint translation:
$a\in P_{st}$ is sent to $ga\in P_{gs,t}$, and
$\gamma(ga)=g\gamma(a)$, where the $\Gamma$-action fixes
literal raw colour names but moves their owner types.

\begin{definition}[Orbit-safe typed palettes]
\label{def:orbit-safe}
For a diversity colour $a\in P_{st}$ set
\[
  a\in S^{G}_{st}\ \Longleftrightarrow\
           ga\in S\quad\text{for every }g\in G.
\]
Equivalently, if $x,y$ are origins of two distinct full
$G$-packets and $\col(x,y)=a$, then $a\in S^G_{st}$
precisely when \emph{every} entry of their full
$G\times G$ colour matrix belongs to $S$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[One full thin packet covers one whole owner orbit]
\label{lem:orbit-safe-owner-helper}
Let $G$ be the ACTUAL finite regular
thin translation group on every
real full $F_Q$-packet, acting on
the named fine $E$-owner set $U$.
For any actual packet $\Pi=F_Q[x]$
whose origin has owner
$s=\operatorname{own}(x)\in U$,
\[
 \{\operatorname{own}(y):y\in\Pi\}=G s,
 \quad
 |\{y\in\Pi:\operatorname{own}(y)=t\}|
        =|\operatorname{Stab}_G(s)|
       \quad(t\in Gs).
\tag{OH}\label{eq:orbit-safe-owner-helper}
\]
Thus for finite $G$-invariant
$U$, exactly ONE actual full
packet from EACH $G$-orbit
of owner labels suffices for
a full-packet helper containing
a representative of EVERY
named owner in $U$. This
does NOT assume the $G$-action
on owners is free; it only uses
the regular action on REAL
packet vertices. Given any
finite full-packet closed
old real set $V$, the helper
can be realised packetwise
DISJOINT from $V$ in $N_U$
by relative full-packet
closed SAP.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the actual full packet
as $\Pi=\{t_gx:g\in G\}$.
The thin action is free on
real points, so every group
element supplies a distinct
point. Its owner is
$\operatorname{own}(t_gx)=gs$.
These owners form exactly
the orbit $Gs$, and the map
$g\mapsto gs$ has all fibres
of size
$|\operatorname{Stab}_G(s)|$.
This proves (OH), including
nontrivial owner stabilisers.

Choose one actually realised
full packet with origin owner
in each $G$-orbit of U;
their owner sets cover all U
and are disjoint as labels
between different orbits.
To make the chosen packets
disjoint from an existing
finite closed V, apply original
owner-named closed SAP to
the finite union of these
complete packets and V
over the empty closed base.
The resulting finite full
packet diagram lies in
the actual owner-typed age
and embeds into $N_U$,
with all packets distinct
and their original literal
owner labels preserved.
No packet representative
section or group-action
splitting on owner labels
has been constructed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Closed strong $S$ supplies strong orbit-safe transversal palettes]
\label{lem:orbit-safe-strong}
Assume original strong $S$-amalgamation over finite
\emph{absolute real acl-closed} spans and normalised unary
closure $\operatorname{acl}_{Q}(A)=F_Q[A]$.
Then the tagged palettes $S^G_{st}$ are nonempty and
strongly amalgamate \emph{all} finite spans in the
primitive all-owner transversal $\mathcal U_U$.
They are converse compatible, pure-$\Gamma$-covariant,
and equivariant under each unilateral endpoint translation:
\[
     gS^G_{st}=S^G_{gs,t}=S^G_{s,gt}
       \quad\text{(as raw fine-colour subsets, with moved types).}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
First fix any finite transversal span $A\to L,R$.  Inflate
every transversal vertex to its full $F_Q$ packet.  By the
full-owner closed-SAP theorem, first realise both inflated
factors \emph{strongly} over the full-packet inflation of
$A$, producing a finite union $V$ with no extra overlap.
Then, using closed SAP once more, adjoin a finite union $T$
of full packets meeting \emph{every} fine $E$-owner in $U$,
disjoint from $V$. By
\cref{lem:orbit-safe-owner-helper},
one whole packet for each ACTUAL $G$-orbit
of fine owner labels suffices, even if
$H=\operatorname{Stab}_G(s)\ne1$.  In $V\cup T$ take the induced full-packet
structures on the common base $F_Q[A]\cup T$ and the two
extended factors $F_Q[L]\cup T$ and $F_Q[R]\cup T$.
These factors intersect \emph{exactly} in that base and
retain every old typed fine colour.  They are finite
\emph{absolute} acl-closed structures because
$\operatorname{acl}_Q=F_Q[-]$.
Apply original strong $S$-amalgamation to these two abstract
acl-closed factors over their common base.

Each old vertex stays in its prescribed $E$-owner: it has a
named $E$-incidence to a common anchor of that owner, and
these incidences are preserved by the real-language amalgam.
Two full off-base packets from opposite factors cannot
collapse to the same $F_Q$-class: each is a complete
$F_Q$-class, so such a collision would identify at least
one off-base point from each factor, contradicting strongness.
Hence selecting the origins gives a \emph{strong typed}
transversal amalgam over $A$.

For every new cross-origin pair $x,y$ all pairs
$t_gx,t_hy$ are distinct off-base real pairs from
opposite factors, and therefore have original colours in
$S$.  Their fine colour is $(g+h)\col(x,y)$ by the actual
thin law.  Consequently the origin colour lies in
$S^G_{\operatorname{own}(x),\operatorname{own}(y)}$.
This proves the required typed strong amalgamation;
taking a span of two singleton factors proves each
compatible $S^G_{st}$ nonempty.

Commutativity of $G$ and its exponent two show that
$a\in S^G_{st}$ iff $ga\in S^G_{gs,t}$ for each $g$;
changing the second endpoint gives the same raw set.
Converse is immediate from symmetry.  Genuine owner
permutations $\gamma$ commute with the global thin
translations and fix raw fine atom names, so they preserve
all tagged orbit-safe palettes.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[All-row orbit-safe R07 interface]
\label{lem:orbit-safe-all-row-interface}
Work in the actual full-owner source of
\cref{lem:orbit-safe-strong}, with the ORIGINAL
fine-colour palette \(S\), its thin-saturated
orbit-safe palettes \(S^G_{st}\), and a
pure-\(\Gamma\)-covariant augmented selector
\(h=h_{S^G}\). Assume that \(\Gamma\) contains the
owner action of the global thin translations, as required for
\cref{prop:moving-readings}. Let \(W^\#\) be one fixed
finite R07 partial packet reservoir, and
suppose the origin transversal has an
INJECTIVE completion in the owner-typed
SAP source. Run the greatest strong-PC
procedure ONCE on the actual packet
indices \(p=(i,f)\), and denote its
terminal exact menus by \(D_{pq}\).

Then for EVERY pair of DISTINCT actual
packets \(p=(i,f)\ne q=(j,k)\)
the following exhaustive alternatives hold.

\smallskip
\noindent\textup{(R)} If \(\{i,j\}\) is an
originally RECORDED structural pair
(\(i\ne j\)), then \(D_{pq}\) is the
literal original singleton \( \{c_{ij}\}\).
For every \(z,w\in G\) the decoded
old fine atom of \((i,f,z),(j,k,w)\)
is precisely the original recorded
structural fine colour
\[
       \operatorname{col}_{B_0}((i,z),(j,w))
                          =(z+w)c_{ij}.
\]

\smallskip
\noindent\textup{(U)} Otherwise the pair
is originally UNRECORDED. This includes
the case \(i=j\) with \(f\ne k\).
Then \(D_{pq}\cap S^G_{\beta_i,\beta_j}
\ne\varnothing\), and for ALL
\(z,w,u,g,h\in G\), the reading
\[
 a=h^u_{z\beta_i,w\beta_j}
             \bigl((z+w)D_{pq}\bigr)
\]
lies in \(S^G_{z\beta_i,w\beta_j}\).
Moreover its entire translated
interpacket matrix
\[
             (g+h)a
\]
consists of ORIGINAL fine atoms in
\(S\), with the properly shifted owner
tags. No old singleton record is
recoloured to achieve this property.

All these conclusions hold in ONE
preselected finite PC fixed family,
independently of which permitted
R07 structural extension and correction
matrix will be chosen later.
The completed structure has no extra
assumption that the structural
automorphism of the INCOMPLETE
reservoir preserves its arbitrary
completion certificate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For (R), the injective completion certificate ensures that no
strong-PC domain becomes empty. Thus a recorded singleton remains
$\{c_{ij}\}$, and every conservative reading on its transported
singleton returns $(z+w)c_{ij}$. This is its original full matrix,
independently of the reading parameter.

For (U), apply \cref{lem:safe-global-completion} to the injectively
completable origin transversal and the strong typed palette $S^G$
from \cref{lem:orbit-safe-strong}. The resulting same-vertex
completion preserves every record and colours every original hole
in $S^G$. Its atoms survive every PC update. Hence every terminal
unrecorded domain $D_{pq}$ meets $S^G_{\beta_i,\beta_j}$.

Translate its two endpoints separately by $z,w$. The original exact
families and the tagged orbit-safe palettes are preserved, giving
\[
 (z+w)D_{pq}\cap S^G_{z\beta_i,w\beta_j}\ne\varnothing.
\]
The tags here are $(z\beta_i,w\beta_j)$, not
$((z+w)\beta_i,\beta_j)$. A moving reading first translates this
menu by $u$, chooses from its nonempty safe trace at the moved
type, and translates the chosen atom back. Unilateral invariance
of $S^G$ therefore puts its output in $S^G$ for every $u$.
By the definition of $S^G$, all its further translates $(g+h)a$
lie in the original palette $S$. This proves full-matrix safety,
including distinct packets with the same structural index.

The augmented selector restricted to the original exact families
has the conservativity, survivor heredity, converse compatibility,
pure-owner covariance and full-PC property used in
\cref{prop:moving-readings}. Its moving readings therefore satisfy
that proposition's switching and independent-triangle identities.
Apply \cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa} with these readings: the same
preselected PC family and decoder are legal, and every structural
map with a legal correction matrix acts on the completed witness.
Neither PC naturality nor this automorphism assertion requires an
invariant choice of the auxiliary completion certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Safe moving readings preserve every missing matrix]
\label{lem:orbit-safe-readings}
Assume $\Gamma$ contains the induced diagonal thin-owner action.
Let $h=h_{S^G}$ be the pure-$\Gamma$-covariant,
converse-compatible, survivor-hereditary full-PC selector
from Lemmas~\ref{lem:augmented-safe-algebra} and
\ref{lem:orbit-safe-strong}, and let
\[
      h^u_{st}(D)=u\,h_{us,t}(uD)
\]
be its moving-type readings as in
Proposition~\ref{prop:moving-readings}.
Whenever an original exact $D\in\mathcal H_{st}$ meets
$S^G_{st}$, then for \emph{every} $u\in G$
\[
                 h^u_{st}(D)\in S^G_{st}\subseteq S.
\]
Suppose an R07 full-row reservoir has an injective
completion of its \emph{origin transversal} in
$\mathcal U_U$.  After greatest strong-PC and the safe
selector on the original packet-pair domains,
\emph{every} originally unrecorded actual interpacket
pair receives a fine $S$-colour under the entire R07
row decoder, for \emph{all} values of the reading
parameter $u(x,y)$ and \emph{all} endpoint translations.
All structural source extensions and their row corrections
continue to preserve the decoded fine colours.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $D$ meets $S^G_{st}$, unilateral invariance makes $uD$ meet
$S^G_{us,t}$. The safe-trace selector chooses there, and translating
back by $u$ returns an atom of $S^G_{st}$. This proves the first
assertion for every original exact menu, independently of any
reservoir.

For a reservoir, apply \cref{lem:orbit-safe-all-row-interface}:
its alternative (U) supplies the nonempty traces and safety for all
rows and endpoint translates, while (R) retains every original
singleton matrix. The same interface verifies the moving-reading
hypotheses of \cref{thm:R07-relative-eppa}, so all structural maps
and legal row corrections remain automorphisms of this one decoder.
\end{proof}
