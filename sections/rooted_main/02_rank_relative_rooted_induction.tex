\begin{lemma}[Rank-relative rooted ACF construction]
\label{lem:acf-rank-step}
Let \(M\) be a nonempty standing symmetric binary source,
and assume the rooted theorem, ordinary EPPA and the all-exact
finite owner-master theorem for \emph{every strictly smaller
actual exact quotient} of \(M\).  Let
\(S\subseteq\mathcal D(M)\) have strong closed
amalgamation as in Definition~\ref{def:rooted-acf}.
The local normalised MI safe-selector/R07 construction is the
explicit direct provider at this rank.

For every finite nonempty original real input
\(A_0\subseteq M\), there exists a finite induced
\(W\subseteq M\) containing \(\operatorname{acl}_M(A_0)\)
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every partial automorphism of \(A_0\) extends to
  an automorphism of the same \(W\);
\item for every nonempty \(Z\subseteq W\) whose distinct
  pairs have colours outside \(S\), and every selected
  \(z\in Z\), some \(\alpha\in\Aut(W)\) satisfies
  \[
       \alpha(Z)\subseteq\operatorname{acl}_M(A_0),
                 \qquad \alpha(z)\in A_0 .
                 \tag{\(\mathrm{ACF}\)}\label{eq:acf-main}
  \]
\end{enumerate}
Thus the witness is ordinary EPPA and
automorphism-transport closure-relative
\(S\)-clique faithful, in fact with a specified
root.  No coherent extension assignment is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If \(M\) is finite, take \(W=M\).  Its real algebraic
closure over a nonempty input is all of \(M\);
homogeneity extends the partial maps and
transitivity moves any selected root into \(A_0\).
We may therefore assume \(M\) infinite.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{1. First handle the finite-exact-normalised case.}
Suppose no nonidentity finite-class invariant exact
equivalence remains.  Enumerate all minimal infinite
invariant equivalences \(E_1,\ldots,E_r\).
For \(r=1\), the greatest finite-class equivalence is
meet-irreducible. The fine-owner quotient \(M/E_1\) has
ordinary EPPA by the lower-rank hypothesis, since the infinite
exact equivalence \(E_1\) is nonidentity. Thus
Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-relative-mi-normal} with the trivial
\(\Top\)-owner quotient supplies a rooted witness directly.

Suppose \(r\ge2\).  Use the \emph{maximal} exact
pseudocomplements of Section~\ref{sec:acf-maximal-cores}:
\[
 R_i=R_{E_i},\qquad \widehat C_i
   =\bigvee\{H\le R_i:H\text{ exact}\},\qquad
 Q_i=M/\widehat C_i,\qquad F_i=R_i/\widehat C_i.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-hat-normalised} says that every \(Q_i\)
is already finite-exact-normalised, with one minimal infinite
direction and unary real acl \(F_i[-]\).  Their exact kernels
have meet \(\Id\).  Corollary~\ref{cor:acf-hat-factors}
provides global full-support strong-closed palettes \(S_i\)
and the one-exceptional-projection property:
for every unsafe clique all but at most one \(Q_j\) projection
lie in a single finite \(F_j\)-packet.  In particular every
original unsafe pair with distinct \(Q_i\) projection stays
unsafe in \(S_i\).

\medskip\noindent
\emph{2. One master for all actual owner quotients.}
For \(i\ne j\) put
\[
    J_{ij}=\widehat C_i\vee \widehat C_j,\qquad
    H_i=\bigcap_{j\ne i}J_{ij},\qquad
    H_*=\bigcap_{i<j}J_{ij}.
\]
These are actual exact equivalences, and
\(H_*\le H_i\).  More importantly
\(H_*>\Id\) by Corollary~\ref{cor:acf-hat-factors},
so the owner source is a \emph{strict} exact quotient
and its master is available from the lower-rank hypothesis.
Moreover
\[
                  H_i\vee H_j=J_{ij}.
                  \tag{\(\triangle\)}\label{eq:acf-owner-joins}
\]
Indeed both \(H_i,H_j\) are contained in
\(J_{ij}\), while
\(\widehat C_i\le H_i\) and \(\widehat C_j\le H_j\).
Let \(\Omega_\infty=M/H_*\); it is an
actual homogeneous exact quotient satisfying the
original A32 hypotheses.  The quotient
\(\Omega_\infty\to M/H_i\) is exact.

Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:acf-owner-master} in
\(\Omega_\infty\) to the finite nonempty owner
input \(\pi_{H_*}(A_0)\).  Denote its finite
witness by \(B_\Omega\), let
\[
  B_i=\pi_{H_i}(B_\Omega),\qquad
  \Gamma=\Aut(B_\Omega),\qquad
  \rho_i:\Gamma\twoheadrightarrow\Aut(B_i).
\]
By (M1)--(M3), every \(B_i\) is an owner
EPPA witness for the original projected owner
input, is closed under its ambient finite-class
equivalence, and \(\rho_i\) is surjective with
kernel transitive on every finite
\(B_\Omega\to B_i\) fibre.  In particular
\(B_i\) is invariant under every finite
thin-translation action induced from
a normalised \(Q_i\) factor.

Apply (M4) INSIDE the strictly lower-rank
actual owner source \(\Omega_\infty=M/H_*\)
to the family of exact quotient kernels
\(H_i/H_*\). We have
\[
 \bigcap_i(H_i/H_*)=\Id_{\Omega_\infty},
 \qquad
 (H_i/H_*)\vee(H_j/H_*)
                =J_{ij}/H_*,
\]
by \(H_*=\bigcap_iH_i\) and
\eqref{eq:acf-owner-joins}.
Consequently (M4) identifies the ACTUAL
finite master \(B_\Omega\), not merely
an abstract subset or a set of orbit
representatives, with the FULL set of
tuples in \(\prod_i B_i\) whose two
coordinates agree in the appropriate
\(B_{J_{ij}/H_*}\) owner quotient
for every \(i<j\).
Every such compatible owner tuple
already lies in the SAME fixed finite
\(B_\Omega\). This excludes phantom
compatible tuples outside the finite
owner witness and is the exact
surjectivity used below.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{3. Relative rooted witnesses in every MI coordinate.}
Write \(A_i^0=\pi_{\widehat C_i}(A_0)\) and
\[
              A_i=\operatorname{acl}_{Q_i}(A_i^0)
                    =F_i[A_i^0].
\]
The actual owner kernel of \(Q_i\to M/H_i\)
is \(H_i/\widehat C_i\).  It contains the unique
minimal infinite direction
\((E_i\vee \widehat C_i)/\widehat C_i\) of \(Q_i\):
indeed \(E_i\le \widehat C_j\) for \(j\ne i\),
so \(E_i\le H_i\).
Thus this possibly coarser owner cut is
eligible for the relative MI construction.

Each \(Q_i\) is already normalised by
Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-hat-normalised}.  Apply the \emph{normalised}
relative MI construction, Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-relative-mi-normal},
using the single preselected \(B_i\), original projected source
\(A_i^0\) and safe palette \(S_i\). Its fine-owner EPPA input is
available because
\[
 Q_i/((E_i\vee\widehat C_i)/\widehat C_i)
                   =M/(E_i\vee\widehat C_i)
\]
is a strictly smaller actual exact quotient of \(M\): its
kernel contains the nonidentity infinite \(E_i\).
The owner-input EPPA and full finite-packet closure conditions
follow from (M1)--(M2) of the lower-rank master.  For
clarity, (M3) already implies that every $B_i$ is
\emph{vertex-transitive}: take the nested quotient from its
exact owner sort to $\Top$, which has one point; its kernel is
all of $\Aut(B_i)$ and must act transitively on the unique
fibre $B_i$.  Because the original projected owner input is
nonempty, every $B_i$ owner consequently lies in the
$\Aut(B_i)$-orbit of an old projected owner.  This is
exactly the old-orbit coverage hypothesis of the relative
MI lemma; it is not an additional group-theoretic splitting.
There is no intermediate factor finite exact kernel \(L_i\),
no crossed owner map \(h_i\), and no use of the general
relative finite-owner-cover lemma at this stage.

Thus we obtain \emph{one} finite
\(F_i\)-closed induced \(D_i\subseteq Q_i\)
with owner projection exactly \(B_i\),
containing \(A_i\), such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item every compatible pair consisting of a
  partial automorphism of \(A_i^0\) and
  \(\gamma_i\in\Aut(B_i)\) extends in \(D_i\)
  with precisely that owner component;
\item every rooted unsafe \(S_i\)-clique of
  \(D_i\) is transported into \(A_i\), with
  its selected root transported into
  \emph{original} \(A_i^0\);
\item by Lemma~\ref{lem:acf-point-homogeneity},
  \(D_i\) admits prescribed-\(\gamma_i\)
  transport of any single point and of
  its whole \(F_i\)-packet.
\end{itemize}
All witnesses are fixed before choosing any
original partial map or unsafe clique.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{4. Assemble the one finite real witness.}
Let \(W\) be the set of compatible tuples
\[
 W=\bigl\{(x_1,\ldots,x_r)\in\prod_iD_i:
 \pi_{J_{ij}}(x_i)=\pi_{J_{ij}}(x_j)
                          \text{ for all }i<j\bigr\}.
\]
Apply \cref{lem:acf-multi-owner-point-homogeneity} with
$C_i=\widehat C_i$ and the owner kernels $H_i$ from Step~2.
The kernels have meet $\Id_M$, and
\eqref{eq:acf-owner-joins} is its join gate (JOG).
The lower-rank (M4) gives exactly the full compatible owner
set $B_\Omega$, with its induced actions $\rho_i$;
Step~3 supplies the surjections $D_i\to B_i$ and their
prescribed-owner point homogeneity. Thus $W$ is a finite
induced subset of $M$, projects onto every $D_i$ and onto
$B_\Omega$, and has precisely the automorphism group (JWA).
In particular, factor automorphisms inducing the components
of one $\gamma\in\Gamma$ assemble to an automorphism of
this original fine-coloured $W$.

The original \(A_0\) embeds
diagonally through its actual
quotient projections. Moreover,
by the finite-meet algebraic-closure
identity \eqref{eq:acf-closure-pullback},
the entire original real closure
is the full compatible tuple set
of the chosen \(F_j\)-closed
targets \(A_j\). Precisely,
\[
             \operatorname{acl}_M(A_0)
           =\{\text{compatible tuples in }\prod_iA_i\}.
           \tag{\(\square\)}\label{eq:acf-target-product}
\]

Ordinary EPPA now follows directly from the final clause of
\cref{lem:acf-multi-owner-point-homogeneity}: (M1) supplies
owner EPPA for $\pi_{H_*}(A_0)$, and Step~3 supplies every
projected source extension with its prescribed owner component.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{5. Rooted transport and the simultaneous owner calculation.}
Let \(Z\subseteq W\) be a nonempty original
unsafe clique, with selected root
\(z=(z_1,\ldots,z_r)\).
Choose an exceptional index \(i_0\)
from~Corollary~\ref{cor:acf-hat-factors}.  Thus
\(\pi_j(Z)\subseteq F_j[z_j]\)
for every \(j\ne i_0\).
The distinct-coordinate set \(\pi_{i_0}(Z)\)
is \(S_{i_0}\)-unsafe by the full-support
palette property, including the singleton case.

Apply relative rooted ACF in \(D_{i_0}\):
some \(\alpha_{i_0}\) has
\[
 \alpha_{i_0}(\pi_{i_0}Z)\subseteq A_{i_0},
 \qquad
 \alpha_{i_0}(z_{i_0})=a_{i_0}
                           \in A_{i_0}^0.
\]
Let its owner component be
\(\gamma_{i_0}\in\Aut(B_{i_0})\).
Choose \emph{one actual} \(a\in A_0\)
such that \(\pi_{i_0}(a)=a_{i_0}\),
and write \(a_j=\pi_j(a)\).
Let \(b,c\in B_\Omega\) be the
complete joint-owner values of
\(z\) and \(a\).
They satisfy
\(\gamma_{i_0}(\pi_{i_0}b)=\pi_{i_0}c\).
By (M3) and \cref{lem:prescribed-action-point-lifting}, choose
\(\gamma\in\Gamma\) with
\[
 \rho_{i_0}(\gamma)=\gamma_{i_0},\qquad \gamma b=c.
\]
It has the prescribed exceptional
component \(\rho_{i_0}\gamma=\gamma_{i_0}\)
and sends the \emph{entire} joint-owner
root tuple of \(z\) to that of the
single selected original \(a\).

For each \(j\ne i_0\), prescribed-owner
point homogeneity of \(D_j\) supplies
an automorphism \(\alpha_j\) with
owner component \(\rho_j(\gamma)\) and
\(\alpha_j(z_j)=a_j\).  Therefore
\[
 \alpha_j(\pi_j Z)
           \subseteq\alpha_j(F_j[z_j])
           =F_j[a_j]\subseteq A_j.
\]
The exceptional \(\alpha_{i_0}\)
already transports the entire complex
projection into \(A_{i_0}\), and its
owner component equals
\(\rho_{i_0}(\gamma)\).
All factor automorphisms thus have
compatible components of the \emph{same}
\(\gamma\). By \eqref{eq:acf-joint-owner-automorphisms},
their tuple is an actual
automorphism \(\alpha\) of \(W\), and
by~\eqref{eq:acf-target-product}
\[
          \alpha(Z)\subseteq
                    \operatorname{acl}_M(A_0),
             \qquad
          \alpha(z)=(a_1,\ldots,a_r)=a\in A_0 .
\]
This proves both assertions in the
normalised case.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{6. Restore an original finite exact kernel.}
Let \(K>\Id\) be the greatest finite-class exact equivalence
of the original source, if one remains. Its quotient
\(\overline M=M/K\) is fully normalised and is a strict actual
exact quotient. By \cref{lem:acf-finite-lift}, the full-matrix
palette
\[
 \overline S=\{q\in\mathcal D(\overline M):
                  \Lambda_K(q)\subseteq S\}
\]
has strong closed amalgamation. The lower-rank rooted theorem
therefore gives one finite ordinary EPPA witness
\(\overline W\) with rooted \(\overline S\)-ACF over
\(\pi_K(A_0)\). The nonrelative reconstruction clause of
\cref{lem:acf-finite-lift} now gives exactly the required
\(W=\pi_K^{-1}(\overline W)\): it contains
\(\operatorname{acl}_M(A_0)\), extends all original partial maps,
and preserves both the whole unsafe-clique target and its
selected original root. This is finite joint-map lifting;
no independent fibre permutations or group section are used.

The empty input has the empty witness
under the usual convention, with no
nonempty rooted requirement.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Dependency boundary of the rank step]
\label{rem:acf-firewall}
This lemma uses no same-rank global ordinary EPPA or
same-rank owner master.  For $r\ge2$ its joint owner
source is strictly smaller by
Corollary~\ref{cor:acf-hat-factors}; for $r=1$
the fine owner source $M/E$ is a strict quotient,
or a singleton when $E=\Top$.  The sole original-
source constructional obligation is the normalised
relative safe-MI HKN/R07 lemma, whose augmented safe
selector and orbit-covariant packet-matrix steps are
still subject to a separate end-to-end mathematical audit.
The following phased proof preserves these interfaces
and does not assert coherent EPPA.
\end{remark}
