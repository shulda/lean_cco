\section{Rooted closure-faithful ordinary EPPA}
\label{sec:acf-main}

The construction below is the induction step in the
\emph{phased} proof.  It invokes ordinary EPPA and finite
owner masters only on strictly smaller actual exact quotients,
not on the current real source.  The resulting global theorem,
including its ordinary EPPA corollary, is established in
Section~\ref{sec:global-phased-induction}.

\begin{lemma}[Joint-owner prescribed-action homogeneity of a full MI tuple witness]
\label{lem:acf-multi-owner-point-homogeneity}
Let $X$ be a source in the standing one-sort symmetric
homomorphic CCO setting. Let $r\ge1$ and let
$C_1,\ldots,C_r$ be ACTUAL
invariant FULL EXACT kernel
equivalences, with
$\bigcap_i C_i=\Id_X$,
and choose ACTUAL exact
$H_i\ge C_i$, with
$H_*=\bigcap_i H_i$.
Assume the CRUCIAL
pairwise-common-quotient
JOIN GATE
\[
               C_i\vee C_j
                  =H_i\vee H_j
                          \qquad(i\ne j).
\tag{JOG}\label{eq:acf-joint-owner-join-gate}
\]
Write $Q_i=X/C_i$,
$\Omega=X/H_*$, and
$O_i=X/H_i$.

Let $B_\Omega\subseteq\Omega$
be a finite INDUCED owner
structure whose projections
$B_i\subseteq O_i$ satisfy:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $B_\Omega$ is EXACTLY
the full set of compatible
tuples from the $B_i$,
with pairwise equality
tested in their actual
common quotient
$X/(H_i\vee H_j)$;
\item every
$\gamma\in\Gamma=
 \Aut(B_\Omega)$ induces
a well-defined automorphism
$\rho_i(\gamma)$ of each $B_i$.
\end{enumerate}
For every i, let
$D_i\subseteq Q_i$ be
a finite induced structure
projecting ONTO $B_i$,
with PRESCRIBED-OWNER POINT
HOMOGENEITY:
for EVERY $x_i,y_i\in D_i$
and EVERY
$\delta_i\in\Aut(B_i)$
carrying the owner of
$x_i$ to the owner of
$y_i$, there is
$\alpha_i\in\Aut(D_i)$
with EXACT owner component
$\delta_i$ and
$\alpha_i(x_i)=y_i$.

Let $W$ be the FULL finite
compatible tuple set
\[
 W=\{(x_i)_{i\le r}\in
             \prod_i D_i:
    \pi_{H_i\vee H_j}(x_i)=
    \pi_{H_i\vee H_j}(x_j)
                        \ (i<j)\}.
\tag{JOW}\label{eq:acf-joint-owner-full-witness}
\]
Then the ACTUAL exact CRT,
using the JOIN GATE
\eqref{eq:acf-joint-owner-join-gate},
identifies $W$ with a
finite induced subset of
original X, with owner
projection $\pi_{H_*}(W)$
EXACTLY $B_\Omega$.
Every coordinate projection $W\to D_i$ is also surjective.

Write $\operatorname{own}_i:\Aut(D_i)\to\Aut(B_i)$
for the actual induced owner-action map;
it is well-defined because the owner
kernel $H_i/C_i$ and all its quotient
pair orbitals are unions of the named
$Q_i$ fine binary orbitals.
The ENTIRE automorphism group, not just
the separately constructible maps, is
the actual finite group pullback
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \Aut(W)\cong
 \bigl\{(\alpha_i)_i\in\prod_i\Aut(D_i):
   \exists\gamma\in\Gamma\
   \forall i\
   \operatorname{own}_i(\alpha_i)
                    =\rho_i(\gamma)\bigr\}.
 \quad}
 \tag{JWA}\label{eq:acf-joint-owner-automorphisms}
\]
The witnessing $\gamma$ is UNIQUE:
the full owner set $B_\Omega$ is
determined pointwise by its complete
tuple of $B_i$ coordinates. This is
a group identity, not a choice of a
section $\Gamma\to\Aut(W)$.

Moreover for EVERY
$\gamma\in\Gamma$ and
EVERY $x,y\in W$ with
\[
            \gamma(\pi_{H_*}x)
                           =\pi_{H_*}y,
\]
there is ONE
$\alpha\in\Aut(W)$,
inducing precisely
$\gamma$ on ALL of
$B_\Omega$ and satisfying
$\alpha(x)=y$.
Thus the natural
$\Aut(W)\to\Gamma$
is ONTO and its kernel
is transitive on EVERY
full $B_\Omega$ owner
fibre. This conclusion
does not claim any
group section
$\Gamma\to\Aut(W)$.
The JOIN GATE is
ESSENTIAL: if two
$C_i=\Id$ while their
$H_i=\Top$ in a
nontrivial source,
coarse owner agreement
would admit ALL formal
ordered pairs $(x,y)$,
whereas the actual CRT
only permits $(x,x)$.

If in addition the
owner input
$B_\Omega$ has ordinary
EPPA for the projection
of a finite original
$A_0\subseteq W$, and
each $D_i$ extends
EVERY compatible partial
automorphism of its
projected input with
EVERY prescribed owner
component $\delta_i$,
then W has ordinary
EPPA for $A_0$ as well.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $B_\Omega$ is empty, every $B_i$ and $D_i$ is empty and,
since $r\ge1$, so is $W$. All groups in (JWA) are then trivial,
and all assertions follow. Assume $B_\Omega\ne\varnothing$.

The exact CRT of \cref{thm:abstract-exact-crt,lem:acf-exact-distributive},
together with $\bigcap_iC_i=\Id_X$ and the join gate (JOG),
identifies each tuple in $W$ with a unique original point of $X$.
Indeed the CRT tests equality in $X/(C_i\vee C_j)$, which by
(JOG) is precisely the owner compatibility tested in (JOW).
For each $b\in B_\Omega$, choose $x_i\in D_i$ above its $i$th
owner coordinate. These choices form a tuple in $W$ with owner
$b$. Conversely, the owner tuple of every point of $W$ lies in
$B_\Omega$ by (i). Thus $W\to B_\Omega$ is onto.
Each $W\to D_i$ is onto as well: for a prescribed $x_i$, choose
$b\in B_\Omega$ above its owner and complete the other coordinates
by choosing points of $D_j$ above the corresponding coordinates
of this same $b$.

We prove (JWA). Every automorphism of the induced $W$ preserves
the actual $C_i$ and $H_*$ kernels, since they are unions of named
original pair orbitals. The surjective projections therefore
induce automorphisms $\alpha_i\in\Aut(D_i)$ and
$\gamma\in\Gamma$ with
$\operatorname{own}_i(\alpha_i)=\rho_i(\gamma)$.
Their quotient pair orbitals are preserved because each is
determined by the original pair orbital. The tuple
$(\alpha_i)_i$ determines the automorphism of $W$, as
$\bigcap_iC_i=\Id_X$.
Conversely, a tuple $(\alpha_i)_i$ with one common owner action
$\gamma$ preserves $W$: its owner tuples are transformed by
$\gamma$ within the full set $B_\Omega$, and the inverse tuple
has the same property. It preserves every original fine pair
orbital by \cref{lem:exact-quotient-colour-meet}, since all its
$Q_i$ coordinate pair orbitals are preserved. This proves (JWA)
as a group identity. Two possible owner actions inducing the
same actions on all $B_i$ agree on each complete owner tuple,
so $\gamma$ is unique.

Now fix $\gamma\in\Gamma$ and $x,y\in W$ with
$\gamma(\pi_{H_*}x)=\pi_{H_*}y$. For each $i$,
prescribed-owner point homogeneity supplies
$\alpha_i\in\Aut(D_i)$ with exact owner component
$\rho_i(\gamma)$ and $\alpha_i(x_i)=y_i$.
By (JWA) these maps give one $\alpha\in\Aut(W)$, inducing
$\gamma$ and sending $x$ to $y$. Since $W\to B_\Omega$ is onto and nonempty,
\cref{lem:prescribed-action-point-lifting} gives surjectivity onto
$\Gamma$ and fibre transitivity of its kernel, without a group section.

Finally, let $p$ be a partial automorphism of $A_0$.
Its projections to the owner and to the $Q_i$ sorts are genuine
partial isomorphisms: their kernels and quotient pair orbitals
are determined by the original pair orbitals. Extend the owner
projection to $\gamma\in\Gamma$ by the assumed owner EPPA.
For each $i$, extend the $Q_i$ projection using the relative
hypothesis with prescribed owner action $\rho_i(\gamma)$.
The resulting tuple belongs to (JWA) and agrees with $p$ on
its domain, proving the final assertion.
\end{proof}
