\section{Standing assumptions and exact quotient notation}
\label{sec:internal-v27-premises}

The source for the following construction is the SAP pure-clone cover of
our original AP structure. Initially it has one transitive real sort;
after safe unary splitting its realised unary types are treated as finitely
many original transitive named sorts. The same-sort atomic relations are
symmetric. Logical equality remains a genuine diagonal and is never added
as a diversity colour between distinct formal real vertices.

\begin{assumption}[General hypotheses]\label{ass:general}
Throughout the binary core of the paper, unless a stronger assumption is
explicitly invoked, we assume the following.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(A\arabic*)]
\item \label{A:finite} The typed atomic language is finite and complete, with paired converses
      between different named sorts and symmetric atoms on each same-sort
      palette; $\K$ is triangle constrained.
\item \label{A:sap} $\K$ has the strong amalgamation property.
\item \label{A:cco} $\K$ has semantic cycle constrainedness
      $\mathrm{CCO}_{\mathrm{sem}}$: a finite atomic partial structure is
      completable precisely when each of its specified simple cycles is
      completable.  Equivalently, in the profile formulation developed in
      \cref{sec:profiles}, the finite family of reachable path profiles is
      $2$-Helly.
\end{enumerate}
\end{assumption}

The following additional hypothesis is imposed only in terminal arguments.
It is an output, not an input, of the finite-cover elimination below.

\begin{assumption}[Terminal infinite-cover hypothesis]\label{ass:standing}
In addition to Assumption~\ref{ass:general}, assume
\begin{enumerate}[label=(A\arabic*),start=4]
\item \label{A:cover} on EACH current named transitive real sort $M_a$, every strict
      cover in its full lattice of invariant equivalence relations has
      infinite relative index, including covers whose upper endpoint is
      the universal equivalence on that sort.
\end{enumerate}
We call this the \emph{terminal} or \emph{sortwise infinite-cover branch}.
\end{assumption}


\subsection{Profiles and exact-domain notation}
\label{sec:profiles}

For current named sorts $a,b$, the symbol $L^{ab}$ denotes the finite
palette of actual diversity atomic pair orbitals. A supported STRONG product
uses distinct formal vertices; on singleton atomic inputs it is given by
allowed typed triangles, and on unions it is extended by unions. We write
$\Prof(w)$ for the endpoint profile of a nonempty typed word $w$, and
$\Rch^{ab}$ for the finite family of its nonempty reachable profiles from
$a$ to $b$. With one original sort we omit the endpoint superscripts.
Associativity and the typed Set-Pasch law are the foundational results
\cref{sec:typed-strong,lem:typed-set-pasch}; the SAP+CCO positive-profile
Helly theorem is \cref{lem:typed-helly}.

The family $\Dat^{ab}$ of nonempty exact STRONG PC domains is supplied by
\cref{lem:typed-PC-fixedpoint-exact}: its members are precisely the
nonempty finite intersections of typed reachable profiles, with the empty
intersection interpreted as the full typed diversity palette. The typed
module below recalls the explicit domain and support-consistency notation.
These STRONG profiles and domains are distinct from FULL RAW exact relations,
which include the logical diagonal when they are same-sort equivalences.

\subsection{Full invariant-equivalence notation}
\label{sec:equivalences}

Fix ONE current named transitive real sort, write $M=M_a$ and
$L=L^{aa}$ in this subsection, and apply the notation separately
to each current sort. For a palette \(X\subseteq L\), put
\[
 xE_Xy\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=y\text{ or }
 \operatorname{col}(x,y)\in X.
\]
Let
\[
 \Lambda=\set{X\subseteq L:E_X\text{ is an equivalence relation on }M}.
\]
Thus \(\varnothing\in\Lambda\) represents equality and \(L\in\Lambda\)
represents the universal equivalence.  If \(X<Y\) in \(\Lambda\), write
\([Y:X]\) for the number of \(E_X\)-classes inside an \(E_Y\)-class; this is
independent of the chosen \(E_Y\)-class by homogeneity.

\subsection{The palette--lattice interface}

\begin{proposition}[All definable equivalences and their joins]
\label{prop:lattice-interface}
Every \(0\)-definable equivalence relation on \(M\) is uniquely of the form
\(E_X\) for some \(X\in\Lambda\).  The set \(\Lambda\) is a finite lattice,
ordered by inclusion, with
\[
 X\meet Y=X\cap Y,
 \qquad
 E_{X\join Y}=E_X\circ E_Y=E_Y\circ E_X.
 \tag{JC}\label{eq:join-composition}
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every \(0\)-definable relation is automorphism-invariant. By homogeneity,
the pair orbits in the complete coloured language are precisely logical
equality and the named diversity colours. Conversely, every union of these
finitely many named pair orbits is \(0\)-definable.  This proves the first assertion and finiteness of
\(\Lambda\).  Intersections give meets.

It remains to prove permutability.  Suppose
\(xE_XyE_Yz\).  The cases \(x=y\), \(y=z\), or \(x=z\) are immediate.  Otherwise let
\[
 a=\operatorname{col}(x,y)\in X,
 \qquad b=\operatorname{col}(y,z)\in Y,
 \qquad c=\operatorname{col}(x,z).
\]
The triangle \((a,b,c)\) is allowed.  Since the triangle relation is symmetric,
the triangle with the two incident colours interchanged is also allowed.
The one-point extension property of the Fra\"isse limit therefore gives a
vertex \(w\) with
\[
 \operatorname{col}(x,w)=b,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{col}(w,z)=a.
\]
Thus \(xE_YwE_Xz\), proving
\(E_X\circ E_Y=E_Y\circ E_X\).

The composition of two commuting equivalence relations is again an
equivalence relation: using relational multiplication,
\[
 (E_XE_Y)^2=E_X(E_YE_X)E_Y=E_X(E_XE_Y)E_Y=E_XE_Y.
\]
It contains both factors, and every equivalence containing both factors
contains their composition.  Hence it is their join.  It is
\(\Aut(\M)\)-invariant and therefore equals \(E_Z\) for a unique
\(Z\in\Lambda\), which proves \eqref{eq:join-composition}.
\end{proof}


\subsection{Actual hidden structures and real representatives}

The terminal hidden structure used below is the ACTUAL equivariant
interpretation in the original real structure. It retains the real sorts,
adds the represented proper meet-irreducible quotient sorts $B_{a,J}$,
and names the projections $F_J:a\to B_{a,J}$ and the upward quotient maps.
The typed module specifies the finite quotient-transition enrichment and
its actual binary semantic-cylinder relations. Thus it specifies the
complete hidden signature rather than adding arbitrary choices of
representatives as part of a finite structure.

For a finite set $D$ of actual real points, $D^{\coord}$ denotes its FULL
point-generated closure under the named unary coordinate and transition
functions. More generally $\cl_H(T)$ is the unary-functional closure of a
finite set $T$ of actual hidden vertices. The hereditary hidden age consists
of all finite induced unary-closed substructures of the actual hidden
structure; these may include quotient vertices with no real representative
in the finite substructure. In the one-sort notation, $\Kcoord$ denotes the
point-generated coordinate expansions of the original finite real objects,
and $\Hid=\Her(\Kcoord)$ their hereditary unary-closed envelope. No new
algebraicity or amalgamation claim is assumed in this notation.
